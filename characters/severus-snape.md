## Uncle Severus

*(settled)* DIV-031.

**The medicine.** Since soon after the curse, Snape has brewed the potion that holds Narcissa's curse at bay (DIV-030). He's one of a handful of people who can, and about the only one Lucius trusts with it. Lucius pays him well: in 1985, a rare artifact Snape asked for, plus a gift of Graphorn horn. The artifact has no plans for now; it's filed away in case it's useful.

**The meeting.** Around 1985, on one of his visits to the Manor, Dracaena, about five, asks if he's the one who makes mama's medicine. She runs up and hugs his legs, because mama always feels better when she drinks it, then asks whether she could make it herself. He nearly refuses, but her eager curiosity is the same *she* used to show. He kneels to her level and offers to set her on the path. First one of his old potions books, and if she gets through it, more instruction. She wants to brew plenty, so mama never runs out and stays well forever. She thanks "uncle Severus", and Lucius teases him with the name. For the first time in a long while, Snape laughs. Full beats: [uncle-severus](../scenes/b1/uncle-severus.md).

**Why he favours her.** She reminds him of Lily. On the page Lily is never named: his thoughts break off at "her". It's a hint that something doesn't add up, and a wink for readers who know canon.

**What he teaches her.** The refined method of the adult Snape. It's more elegant than the experimental tricks in the Prince's sixth-year book, which is why Harry can still beat her in B6 with the Prince's notes, when she has no drive left to one-up him (DIV-011). By Hogwarts she has a six-year head start and is the best potioneer in her year, possibly in the castle.

*(draft)* **The book.** The old potions book he sends her is his own, annotated in the margins. Her habit of correcting her fairy tales in the margins comes from him ([sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).

## Lucius

Lucius took him under his wing when he was sorted into Slytherin (canon: Lucius is a prefect at his Sorting, and in his last year). Lucius got him rare books without ever making it feel like charity, and taught him curses not even the Restricted Section has. Snape knows Lucius was cultivating a client, and doesn't mind: under the game, Lucius counts him a friend, and Snape has few, if any, left. *(settled)* After school, Lucius keeps him supplied by owl and commissions potions from him: paid work, never charity. In the war years that fits Lucius's role as Voldemort's logistics man ([lucius-malfoy](lucius-malfoy.md)). The 1985 lockbox is the same arrangement, grown up. (Q-040, answered)

## The Occlumency lessons

*(settled)* DIV-020. He teaches Dracaena in summer 1995, and his cover holds with Lucius: a general precaution for someone working under cover. The lessons continue their old apprenticeship. They also show him her memories of Harry: his favourite pupil, getting chummy with the son of his arch-enemy. What he does with it: Q-043.
