# Dracaena Malfoy

**Role:** Harry's rival and his tsundere love interest. 🔒 DIV-001
**Canon baseline:** Draco Malfoy. Anything not overridden follows canon Draco, adjusted for the knock-on effects of her being a daughter (Q-001).
**Changes from canon:** DIV-001, DIV-003–006, DIV-009–018, DIV-020–023, DIV-027–032, DIV-038–041, DIV-043, DIV-045–047

## Core

- **Want:** to live outside the script her family wrote for her (DIV-013).
- **Need:** _open_
- **Wound / fear:** that the dream was never real (DIV-015).
- **Lie she believes:** _open_

## Gifts

Gifted and brave, as befits wizard royalty (DIV-011):

- An excellent potioneer, with a particular interest in poisons. The best in her year and possibly in the castle, ahead of Hermione (DIV-011). See *Potions* below.
- Cunning.
- A voracious reader on whatever grips her: poisons, the strange boy's stories, then Muggle novels (DIV-010).
- Competent, and no coward.

## Heiress and tomboy

*(settled)* A tomboy in spirit, at least: Quidditch, flying, duelling, dragons. But she's still a refined heiress, and the two have to live together in her. Like canon Draco she's spoiled and almost always gets her way, but here it comes off as cute rather than pathetic, all the more because she comes into her powers quickly (DIV-011). Lucius would rather shelter her, and rarely wins ([lucius-malfoy](lucius-malfoy.md)).

## Potions

*(settled)* DIV-011, DIV-031. Snape has been her master since she was about five. She asked him to teach her so she could brew mama's medicine herself, and mama would never run out (DIV-030). Full story: [severus-snape](severus-snape.md).

- **The head start.** Six years of private lessons by the time she reaches Hogwarts, in the adult Snape's refined method.
- **B1.** At the first Potions lesson Snape shows her off, and she outdoes Hermione, whose hatred of her starts there ([uncle-severus](../scenes/b1/uncle-severus.md)).
- **B4.** She brews Harry a gillyweed extract for the Second Task (DIV-039, Q-051).
- **B6.** Harry beats her at the Draught of Living Death with the Prince's notes. That isn't because the Prince is better than his own older self. The sixth-year tricks are experimental and less elegant than what she was taught, but they work, and she has no drive left to one-up Harry: her priorities are elsewhere (DIV-015). She notices how much his new tricks resemble her technique, and so does Hermione (Q-042). In the same lesson she knows the Amortentia at a glance and doesn't raise her hand ([slughorns-first-lesson](../scenes/b6/slughorns-first-lesson.md), DIV-045).
- **Her perfume.** She brews her own pear perfume, the one Harry picked for her in B5, adding a new note to every batch and daring him to name it. She still wears it in B6 ([the-pear-perfume](../scenes/b5/the-pear-perfume.md)).
- **Mama's medicine.** One day she takes over brewing it in Snape's stead, a tangible sign of how far she's come (when: Q-041).

## Occlumency

*(settled)* DIV-020. She learns it from Snape in the summer between B4 and B5, continuing their old apprenticeship (DIV-031). The Malfoy discipline helps. Snape's stated reason is a general precaution for someone working under cover. It isn't enough: at their first meeting in summer 1996, Voldemort shatters her walls with ease (DIV-015). In the final duel she uses it another way. Instead of walling him out, she wraps Harry's mind in hers (DIV-021). Rules: [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md).

## Patronus

*(settled)* DIV-027. Mist from B3, when she studies the charm for Harry, until the Yule Ball gives her a corporeal one. Gone in B6, back at the Battle of Hogwarts. It's a dragon. Full arc: [threads/patronus.md](../threads/patronus.md).

## Scars

*(settled)* DIV-032. Snape saves her from Sectumsempra but arrives too late to prevent the scars, and she bears them for life. He's in time to dispel the curse itself, which counter-curses and potions fight off over the following days. How it happens and how Snape buries it: [sectumsempra](../scenes/b6/sectumsempra.md) (DIV-043). At least one scar runs from her belly to her neck, which reminds her a little of her mother's marks ([narcissa-malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md)). How many more, and where: Q-044.

- **Harry** didn't know what the spell would do, and can never forget the mistake he made in a moment of anger.
- **She** goes through something similar at first. Eventually she comes to like them, as a symbol of what they got over together. They match his lightning scar.
- **Their game.** She sometimes uses them to guilt-trip him into doing her favours. He always kisses them when he sees them, hoping one day it'll make them disappear.

## The Mark

*(settled)* DIV-015. Unlike canon Draco, she's never proud of it. She's ashamed of it and feels defiled, and it reminds her constantly of the chains that hold her. In the hollow weeks she won't let Harry touch her ([sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).

## The tsundere engine

- **When she feels exposed, she reaches for snobbery.** This first shows at Madam Malkin's: flustered at being charmed by a Muggle story, she sneers at Hagrid (DIV-009).
- **She admires competence, daring and valour, and it comes out as contempt.** Her line is "if *Potter* of all people can…" (DIV-013).
- **She does the right thing so Potter can't beat her at it** (Q-005, answered).
- **She's not above subterfuge and pettiness when throwing darts at her target.** She milks the B3 injury for weeks (DIV-017).
- **She wants to own his happiness.** In B3 she's delighted he has no happy memories, because then she can give him the only one he'll have, and prove his friends couldn't. Never spoken ([lap-pillow](../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)).
- **Theirs is an exclusive game.** She's delighted when he's jealous, but the thought of using anyone to provoke him disgusts her (DIV-029).
- **She studies him.** She watches all his matches like a hawk and takes overly detailed notes, not all of them technical ([quidditch](../threads/quidditch.md)).
- **Her tenderness is borrowed.** When she wants to give him something loving, her only model is how her mother loves her ([lap-pillow](../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)).
- **Her soft side leaks when she forgets herself.** The Potions "cooking" daydream, then a scramble to save face (DIV-017). When she beats Harry at the Duelling Club she forgets to gloat: "Did you see that? I won!" (DIV-028).
- **She'll treasure the "princess" moment forever and never admit it.** Harry regrets it.

Open: how the balance shifts in B6–B7, and what keeps her a person rather than a trope.

## Voice

_Open._ She tuts at Muggles who get real magic wrong.

## Appearance

Grey eyes (canon default), not the princess's purple. Otherwise canon default, open to change.

## Family

- Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy; she is their only child, because a curse meant for her father hit her mother (DIV-030). She loves them both. Her mother is "mama", and her model of love. Her father is strict, and she's a bit afraid of him, above all of disappointing him: her B3 Boggart is him looking at her with disgust ([remedial-boggart](../scenes/b3/remedial-boggart.md)). That's her fear, not what he feels ([lucius-malfoy](lucius-malfoy.md)).
- Narcissa quietly gets her the Muggle books (DIV-010, Q-018, Q-019), and is Harry's ally inside the house ([narcissa-malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md)).
- Her mother's curse needs a potion to hold it back. Dracaena learned Potions so she could brew it herself (DIV-030, DIV-031).
- Narcissa was born a Black, so Sirius is her mother's cousin, and Bellatrix and Andromeda are her aunts (canon default). Relevant to Q-006 and DIV-014.

## Wand

Hawthorn and unicorn hair (canon default). It matters: it's the wand she and Harry share in the final duel, his hand over hers (DIV-006, DIV-023).
