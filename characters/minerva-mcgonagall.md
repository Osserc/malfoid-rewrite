# Minerva McGonagall

**Role:** Transfiguration professor and Deputy Headmistress. In the first war, the Ministry's special-operations asset.
**Canon baseline:** Strict, fair, formidable. A member of the Order of the Phoenix in both wars, and briefly at the Ministry before she taught. The books give her no combat record before the Battle of Hogwarts.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-079, DIV-080, DIV-081.

## The war *(settled)* · DIV-079

She was not a spy. She led raids — a small-unit asset the Ministry pointed at Death Eater houses and gatherings, working with squads of Aurors. Her signature spell was Lustrum ([magic](../setting/magic.md)), and it is why the other side called her **Hellfire**.

- **The battle garb.** Riding boots, a faded tartan kilt belted and drawn over the shoulder with a luckenbooth brooch, a dragonhide jerkin, a plaited hairpiece with metal inserts, her hat with riding goggles fastened above the brim, and a small insignia: a sword and wand crossed, gold on dark red. All of it singed. She transfigures it onto herself in front of the school in B3, which is the first anyone at Hogwarts has seen of it.
- **The scars.** Lustrum always burns the caster's own account, so her arms are marked and her garb is burnt through in places. She has never had the scars treated or the garb repaired. She calls it penance; the school, which knows nothing, reads it as the mark of a woman who walked through a great many fires.
- **The raid on Strix palace.** Late in the first war she led the attack that destroyed the Strix household. Basil Thynne, a half-blood social climber who had married into the family and attached himself to Voldemort for the standing, died in it. His wife Arvilla Strix and their infant son Balthier survived — which, under Lustrum's rule, was not luck: the fire weighed them and let them go. Balthier remembers the golden flames and a witch flying slowly over the ruins, and nothing else. See DIV-082, [house-unity](../threads/house-unity.md).

## What she actually is *(settled)* · DIV-079

The twist under the legend: she is a battle junkie, and she hides it almost perfectly.

- **Her happy memories are her raids.** She casts her Patronus off them, and names each one aloud as she does — a place and a date, like a battle honour. *Ambleside, 4th April 1978.* *Dunfermline, 17th September 1980.* To anyone listening it is a tic. Across the books the reader assembles what the dates are ([patronus](../threads/patronus.md)).
- **The penance is not for the killing.** It is for having loved it. The charm works because the joy was real, and that is the whole horror: the happiest nights of her life were nights she burned people, and she has never once pretended otherwise to herself.
- **Discipline as a cage.** This is why she is the strictest teacher in the castle. The rules she enforces on everyone else are the ones she needs. When the cage opens in B3 she laughs — not cruelly, and not maliciously; joyously, with a tinge of mania — and the whole castle hears it.
- **Afterwards she denies it flatly.** She refuses to acknowledge the night at all and threatens detention to anyone who raises "this outlandish notion", which only feeds the legend.

## Lustrum

The spell's rules live in [magic](../setting/magic.md). What belongs to her:

- **The vial.** After the Dementors swarmed the Quidditch pitch she had Flitwick make her an enhancer for Lustrum — a charmed trinket, custom and extremely expensive. It is what turns one casting into the small sun over the castle. She has been waiting to use it ever since, and Flitwick knows it. *(Proposal, not agreed: she left it with Flitwick to hold because she did not trust herself to carry it, and his line at the siege is him choosing to arm her.)*
- **Extermination.** She is a loud and unpopular proponent of destroying the Dementors outright, and has been since the war. The Ministry keeps them because they are useful ([azkaban](../problems/azkaban.md)).
- **At the Quidditch pitch.** When the Dementors swarmed the match she stood down only because Harry was in the middle of it. She left scorch marks on the stadium railing in a downpour. Lee Jordan, sitting beside her, is the only one who saw, and had the sense to say nothing.
- **In B7.** She can still cast it and does, but not freely: the Dementors are among her students, and Voldemort is simply the better wizard. Precision, not a supernova. Whether it touches Inferi at all: Q-103.

## Voice

Clipped, exact, Scottish. Under fire she gets shorter and politer, and the politeness is the warning sign. Her taunts are courteous ("Would that you had been in the field") and land harder for it.
