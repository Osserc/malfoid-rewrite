# Narcissa Malfoy

**Role:** Dracaena's mother, her "mama" and her model of love. Soured on the Death Eaters, and Harry's ally inside the house.
**Canon baseline:** Narcissa Malfoy, née Black. Anything not overridden follows canon.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-010, DIV-030, DIV-031, DIV-040

## Core

- **Want:** for Dracaena to be happy, almost in her stead (DIV-030).
- **Need:** _open_
- **Wound / fear:** the curse that took her future children (DIV-030).
- **Lie she believes:** _open_

## The curse

*(settled)* DIV-030. After Voldemort's fall, Lucius escapes Azkaban by renouncing him and claiming he was under the Imperius Curse (canon). A Death Eater still at large tries to curse him for the betrayal, and the curse hits Narcissa instead. It leaves her unable to have more children. Healers can mend almost anything, but damage done by Dark magic stays, as it does in canon (Bill's scars, Dumbledore's hand, the Longbottoms). That's why Dracaena is an only child.

- **The necrosis.** The curse is still at work: a magical necrosis on her belly, like the curse on Dumbledore's hand. At its worst it would spread.
- **The medicine.** A very complex potion shrinks the necrosis and keeps it at bay, but never lifts it. After years of treatment the curse is weaker and she lives a more or less normal life. She still needs regular doses, or it regresses, and she still bears its dark marks. Snape is one of a handful who can brew it, and about the only one Lucius trusts with it. He has made it for her since soon after the curse, and Lucius pays him well ([severus-snape](severus-snape.md), DIV-031). This keeps the Snape–Malfoy connection going. One day Dracaena takes over brewing it in Snape's stead, a tangible sign of her growth. When she takes over: Q-041.
- **Lucius** never forgives himself. Narcissa does forgive him, but she uses his guilt to manoeuvre him where Dracaena is concerned ([lucius-malfoy](lucius-malfoy.md)).
- **The Death Eaters** took her future children, and she has soured on the whole cause. She wants Dracaena to be happy, almost in her stead.

## Harry

*(settled)* Her souring makes her Harry's ally inside the house. She understands what it means to be in love, and Harry is hardly a bad match for a Malfoy: almost a pure-blood, with a pure-blood father. Rowling's own essay on the Malfoy family says they avoided marrying within the small pure-blood pool, and half-bloods turn up throughout their family tree. It first shows in B4. Before the Yule Ball she picks out a dress to go with Harry, confirms Lucius's suspicions and plays Harry's advocate, and Lucius reluctantly sees her point ([the-green-dress](../scenes/b4/the-green-dress.md), DIV-040). Where else it shows: Q-039.

## Relationships

- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): her only child. Quietly gets her the Muggle books (DIV-010). Dracaena learned Potions to brew her medicine (DIV-031). Her Sectumsempra scars echo Narcissa's marks (DIV-032).
- [Severus Snape](severus-snape.md): brews the medicine that holds her curse back (DIV-030).
- [Lucius Malfoy](lucius-malfoy.md): her husband. She has forgiven him for the curse, and uses the guilt he still carries.
- Harry Potter: her ally in all but name (Q-039).
- Sirius Black, Bellatrix Lestrange, Andromeda Tonks: her cousin and her sisters (canon default).
