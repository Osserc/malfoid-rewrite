# Ron Weasley

**Role:** Harry's best friend. Sorted into Hufflepuff, where Cedric Diggory takes him under his wing.
**Canon baseline:** Ron Weasley. Anything not overridden follows canon.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-033, DIV-036, DIV-051

## Core

- **Want:** to step out of his brothers' shadow (canon).
- **Need:** _open_
- **Wound / fear:** being the family failure. The Hat seems to confirm it on his first night.
- **Lie he believes:** that Hufflepuff is where the rejects go.

## The Sorting

DIV-033. The Hat doesn't hesitate for a second: Hufflepuff. Most Sortings are quick, and the Hat only weighs a pupil's choice when it's torn, as it was with Harry. With Ron it isn't, so his wish for Gryffindor never comes into it. That squares with canon's point about choices.

The speed is what hurts. He's the first Weasley outside Gryffindor, sent without a moment's doubt to the House he thinks takes the rejects, and he's teased for not making it.

## Cedric

DIV-033. Cedric Diggory takes him under his wing and shows him Hufflepuff isn't what he thinks. Away from his legendary brothers, Ron finds out he can thrive on his own.

- **B4.** Cedric is Hufflepuff's champion. Ron is caught between his best friend and his mentor, and divided loyalty, not jealousy, drives the falling-out. He can carry tips between the champions (Q-010).
- **Cedric's death** becomes a wound inside the trio, not a near-stranger's death. How Ron takes it: Q-010.
- **B7.** Loyalty is Hufflepuff's virtue, so leaving the tent betrays his House's own value, and his return counts for more.

## Relationships

- Harry Potter: his best friend, in another House.
- [Hermione Granger](hermione-granger.md): in Ravenclaw. Their B3 feud over Crookshanks and Scabbers becomes a House incident (DIV-036).
- Cedric Diggory: his mentor (DIV-033).
- His family: every Weasley before him was a Gryffindor. Ginny's House: Q-049.
- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): obnoxious to him on the train, which is why Harry refuses her ([hogwarts-express](../scenes/b1/hogwarts-express.md)).
