## B4: the falling-out

*(draft)* Q-010. Canon's falling-out holds, jealousy and all, and DIV-005 gives it a second edge. Canon's Ron resents the glory; ours watches the whole school find out that the person Harry would miss most is Malfoy. His best friend has been lying to him for months, and lying about *her*, the girl who has sneered at his family since they were eleven. Which wound leads is open, and so is what he makes of the relationship once it's public.

## End of B4: the second rift *(settled)* · DIV-063

Canon's quarrel mends and the three part at King's Cross as friends. Ours opens a second break in the last days of term, and it doesn't mend before the summer.

- **What he does.** With Harry sleeping out of the tower every night, Ron goes through his trunk for the Marauder's Map, and finds neither Harry nor Malfoy anywhere on it.
- **The row,** on the train. Harry goes cold at the rifling, not at the question. Ron's case is sound and he knows it: Voldemort is back, a Death Eater was in the castle all year, and his best friend is sneaking off at night with Lucius Malfoy's daughter.
- **How it ends.** Dracaena opens the door mid-row to fetch Harry, and Harry goes with her without answering him ([the-train-home](../scenes/b4/the-train-home.md)).
- **Afterwards.** Ron is sure she has him hexed. Hermione concedes they were probably too overprotective, and notices what should worry them more: Dracaena didn't acknowledge them at all.
- **Into B5.** Canon's summer silence at Privet Drive now sits on an injury from the year before, on both sides. How it mends is open (Q-010).
- **What he never learns.** The twins' shop money came to them dressed as an investment so they could take it, and the framing was Dracaena's idea (DIV-062). Fred and George take the word literally and spend years repaying an investor who never wanted repaying.
