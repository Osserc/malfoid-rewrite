# Lucius Malfoy

**Role:** Dracaena's father. A Death Eater, and a skilled Occlumens.
**Canon baseline:** Lucius Malfoy. Anything not overridden follows canon.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-013, DIV-020, DIV-030, DIV-031

## The first war

*(settled)* Voldemort's logistics man: procurement, money, contacts. That's what makes the Imperius defence plausible. Officially, he's almost a bureaucrat and a schemer who never did the heinous work himself. Voldemort surely made him kill or torture at least once. Commissioning potions from Snape is part of the job ([severus-snape](severus-snape.md)).

## His daughter

*(settled)* He isn't resentful about having a daughter rather than a son. He thinks a daughter is too precious and fragile, and should be somewhat sheltered. But he's up against two formidable opponents: his doting wife, and his beloved daughter, who almost always gets her way ([dracaena-malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md)). When she wants to fly, he has to give in, and sneaks in his condition: no training without supervision. As the years pass his worries ease, because he sees how capable she is, but he stays very protective. He's strict. Her Boggart is him looking at her with disgust ([lap-pillow](../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)), but that's her fear, not what he feels. How sheltering her squares with the rest of his story: Q-036.

## Occlumency

A skilled Occlumens, out of the discipline the Malfoys instil (DIV-020). He can't lie to Voldemort's face as Snape does, but in their regular B5 meetings he tweaks what Voldemort perceives through passive Legilimency. Rules: [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md).

## The spy strategy

- He comes up with the "keeping tabs on Potter" strategy himself, at the end of B4, in a scene the author has in mind (DIV-013, Q-022).
- In B5 he tells Voldemort he has planted his daughter as a spy on Potter (DIV-020). He meets Voldemort regularly, but never at the Manor before B6.
- He's in Azkaban from June 1996 (canon default). That summer Voldemort sees in Dracaena's head that she was never a spy (DIV-015). What that costs Lucius: Q-030.

## Relationships

- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): his only child.
- [Narcissa Malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md): his wife. She forgave him for the curse; he never forgave himself (DIV-030, Q-019).
- [Severus Snape](severus-snape.md): his protégé since Snape's Sorting, and among the few Snape calls a friend. After school, books by owl and paid commissions. Lucius pays him to brew Narcissa's medicine, and trusts no one else with it (DIV-030). He let Snape take Dracaena on as a pupil at five (DIV-031). Snape teaches her Occlumency in summer 1995 (DIV-020).
