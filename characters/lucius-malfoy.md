# Lucius Malfoy

**Role:** Dracaena's father. A Death Eater, and a skilled Occlumens.
**Canon baseline:** Lucius Malfoy. Anything not overridden follows canon.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-009, DIV-013, DIV-020, DIV-030, DIV-031, DIV-037, DIV-040, DIV-047, DIV-052, DIV-053, DIV-057, DIV-060, DIV-068, DIV-069

## Core

- **Want:** *(draft)* his family kept whole and untouchable. Fear of losing them is as much a motive as love of them, and both of them pull against the Dark Lord having a claim on him (DIV-030).
- **Need:** _open_
- **Wound / fear:** guilt, because the curse meant for him hit Narcissa and cost her any more children; he never forgives himself, and she uses it (DIV-030). And fear: it's the only thing keeping him with the Death Eaters, the same reasoning Dracaena will follow in B6 (DIV-015).
- **Lie he believes:** _open_

How his marriage works and what he knows about his daughter: Q-019 (the author has ideas).

## The first war

*(settled)* Voldemort's logistics man: procurement, money, contacts. That's what makes the Imperius defence plausible. Officially, he's almost a bureaucrat and a schemer who never did the heinous work himself. Voldemort surely made him kill or torture at least once. Commissioning potions from Snape is part of the job ([severus-snape](severus-snape.md)).

*(settled)* **What he actually contributed, and what it cost everyone else** · DIV-084. His one great service to Voldemort is procurement in its purest form. Voldemort had Dementors and no way to aim them, and asked whether anything existed for herding; herding is a sourcing problem, which is Lucius's whole function. He went through the family holdings and came back with his great-great-grandfather's charm, ***Arceo***, built generations ago to push Dementors *away* from a house or a road or a child. He did not make it and did not see what else it was. **Macnair** did, and drilled the horde into an army with it.

- **He is not off the hook for that.** He found it, he handed it over, and he has never once had to think about it since.
- **And it bought his freedom twice.** The Imperius claim is the story; the price was the original spell, sold to a Ministry that had just discovered it had no hold on the Dementors at all. It uses it to this day. Very Lucius: he sold the method and kept the house.
- **Dracaena does not know any of it.** She has the Imperius story, and she believes it. Harry gets the shape of the truth in the B4 Pensieve, in language he cannot use yet. What she does with it, if she ever has it, is Q-109 — and it would be the third and heaviest charge against him, after Dobby and Buckbeak (Q-098).

## His daughter

### Why she is the way she is *(settled)* · DIV-030

**He built her self-regard, and he did it by almost never telling her no.** That is the mechanism, and this is the reason for it: **she is the only child they will ever have.** The curse that was meant for him took the rest (DIV-030), so everything the house will ever be rests on one girl, and he is quietly terrified of losing her. Refusing her costs him something he cannot afford to spend.

- **And she is the image of her mother,** so he cannot look at her without seeing Narcissa and seeing the wound in the same glance. It compounds every time.
- **He is not a doormat, and this is not weakness.** He is still the patriarch of the house and acts like it. Buckbeak is the proof: when the family's standing is in question he does the cold thing and does not consult anybody (🔒 DIV-003).
- **What it produces** is a girl who genuinely believes nothing in the world can hurt her, and is wrong about exactly one thing — her prince's opinion of her ([dracaena-malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md)). Her arrogance is not a pose she adopted; it is a thing her father spent fourteen years constructing, for reasons that were never about her at all.
- **Which is where the conflict is.** Love and the fear of loss drive him equally, and both of them pull against a Dark Lord who can demand anything at any time. He is in Voldemort's pocket out of fear (DIV-030) while being a man whose every other instinct is to put his family somewhere nothing can reach them.
- *(parked)* **It may matter a great deal in B2,** depending on who the diary is actually meant for: Q-130.

### The sheltering

*(settled)* He isn't resentful about having a daughter rather than a son. He thinks a daughter is too precious and fragile, and should be somewhat sheltered. But he's up against two formidable opponents: his doting wife, and his beloved daughter, who almost always gets her way ([dracaena-malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md)). When she wants to fly, he has to give in, and sneaks in his condition: no training without supervision. When she makes the Slytherin team on merit he celebrates by buying the whole side Nimbus 2001s, purely to show off — which is why people say she bought her place, and why her housemates owe her something they'd rather forget (Q-081). He celebrates achievement and does not console failure: there is no new broom after she loses the B3 final, and she wouldn't want one (DIV-068, DIV-069). As the years pass his worries ease, because he sees how capable she is, but he stays very protective. He's strict, and *(settled)* **she is not afraid of him**: when he puts his foot down and she can't walk all over him, what it produces is anger, not fear. Over Buckbeak she doesn't defy him to his face, she goes around him (🔒 DIV-003). Her B3 Boggart is Harry, not him ([remedial-boggart](../scenes/b3/remedial-boggart.md), DIV-017). How sheltering her squares with the rest of his story: Q-036.

### The boy from the robe shop *(settled)* · DIV-009

**He has known about Harry Potter since his daughter was eleven, and he has never once managed to decide what to think about it.** She comes back from Diagon Alley on 31 July 1991 announcing she has made a friend, and he dislikes the sound of him at once: a boy she met unsupervised, of no known family, raised somewhere among Muggles. The consolation is thin and he takes it anyway — she asked, and both the boy's parents were magic, so a half-blood at worst.

- **It is the first person she ever chose.** Every friend she has was selected out of his own connections — Parkinson, Greengrass, Nott, and the two little trolls the Crabbes and Goyles produced, kept about her for muscle. This one is hers, and he cannot vet what he did not arrange.
- **Then the train, and he learns who it was.** She comes home from the Hogwarts Express with the boy's name and the grudge, and he is left genuinely divided. **His daughter close to Harry Potter would be worth a very great deal** — to the family, and one day to the Dark Lord — and she has gone and spoiled it inside a month.
- **And he is quietly, enormously relieved, for a reason that has nothing to do with any of that.** She is not close to the boy she chose, so she is still **his**. It is a father's jealousy and he would never once name it as one.
- **She does not stop talking about Potter. Ever.** Not for six years. Every letter, every holiday, every meal: what Potter did, what Potter got away with, how insufferable Potter is, at length and in detail, by a girl who is certain she is complaining. Lucius has the boy's entire school career narrated to him by his own daughter. **Narcissa works out what she is listening to long before he does**, which is why at the Ball one of them is choosing a dress to go with Harry and the other is asking questions (DIV-040).
- **So B4 is not a surprise so much as a bill.** When the owl says *Hogwarts' champion* without saying which, his suspicion has three years of dinner-table evidence behind it, and his wife only confirms what he already feared and half wanted.

It is the same conflict that runs the rest of him: the Dark Lord's claim on his house against everything else he wants for it (DIV-030).

## Occlumency

A skilled Occlumens, out of the discipline the Malfoys instil (DIV-020). He can't lie to Voldemort's face as Snape does, but in their regular B5 meetings he tweaks what Voldemort perceives through passive Legilimency. Rules: [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md).

## Finding out *(settled)* · DIV-040

Before the Yule Ball, Dracaena writes home twice: to him, that she'll be attending with Hogwarts' champion, without saying which of the two; to her mother, for the backless green dress. He questions Narcissa, who confirms his suspicions while choosing a dress to go with Harry. He's annoyed she's calmly matchmaking their daughter with the Dark Lord's sworn enemy, but reluctantly sees her point: Harry is a good prospect for Dracaena, and she has soured on the Death Eaters. She sends a more modest dress ([the-green-dress](../scenes/b4/the-green-dress.md)). Right after the Ball, Rita Skeeter prints that they went together.

## The graveyard *(settled)* · DIV-057

In the graveyard he does two things, and nobody sees either. A furtive *Accio*, cast as he runs, takes his daughter's ribbon off Harry's wrist. A well-placed Stunner on Avery opens the gap Harry gets out through. It isn't sympathy for Potter: it's his daughter's token tied round the arm Wormtail opened with a dagger. He keeps the ribbon in his pocket, recognises her knot in it, and tells no one — least of all his wife, his daughter or the Dark Lord.

He has, in other words, already committed treason for the boy by the time Harry keeps his name from Fudge the same night (DIV-047). Neither of them knows what the other did. What it would cost him if Voldemort ever found out: Q-030. Whether Harry ever learns, and what becomes of the ribbon: Q-072.

## The spy strategy

- *(settled)* At the end of B4 he orders her to cut ties with Potter, and she adamantly refuses. He resigns himself to protecting her from herself: he comes up with the "keeping tabs on Potter" strategy, and Snape teaches her Occlumency (DIV-013, DIV-020, DIV-040). The scene is one the author has in mind (Q-022).
- *(settled)* **Harry covered for him.** After the Third Task, Harry keeps Lucius's name from Fudge, out of concern for Dracaena, and she's in the hospital wing to see it (DIV-047). When Lucius orders her to cut ties, she throws it back at him: Harry covered for you, so why can't you do it for him, and for me? For Lucius, covering for Potter means deceiving Voldemort, of all people. It calls back her noblesse oblige over Buckbeak (Q-005): a debt of honour has to be repaid. Dumbledore notices the missing name too, and welcomes it as a chance at redemption for Lucius (DIV-046).
- In B5 he tells Voldemort he has planted his daughter as a spy on Potter (DIV-020). He meets Voldemort regularly, but never at the Manor before B6.
- He's in Azkaban from June 1996 (canon default). That summer Voldemort sees in Dracaena's head that she was never a spy (DIV-015). What that costs Lucius: Q-030.

## The elf *(settled)* · DIV-076, DIV-050

He knew what the sock meant. Everyone knows a dismissed elf does away with itself; nobody knows why, and nobody has ever asked (DIV-075). He discarded it anyway — no temper, no aim, no malice, just a nobleman stripping a filthy Muggle sock off a book and flinging it aside as rubbish, which Dobby then catches. Carelessness, not cruelty, and that is the whole charge. Then he spent five years deciding it did not matter.

- **What he actually did** was cut his own house's spirit loose. The Manor goes cold from that night and never recovers, and he blames the war when the war eventually turns up — five years late ([house-elves](../setting/house-elves.md)).
- **He does not attack Harry.** Brawling with a twelve-year-old is beneath him and he has never needed to. What he does instead is stop at the end of the corridor and tell him he hopes he knows what he has just done — the only warning anyone gives, and nobody in the scene can read it.
- **At the Manor in B7** he sees what crumbles out of Dobby's body: a translucent figure taller than a man, fair-haired, with Malfoy grey eyes. Everyone present sees it and nobody knows what it means; he recognises *a Malfoy* and has no context whatever for why. Hermione supplies the context afterwards, which is its own humiliation.
- **His apology** is therefore not for kicking a servant. It is for killing his line's house out of pique and living five years in the body of it without noticing (DIV-050).
- **He learns what it was from Hermione Granger**, who is the only person alive who can read the image, because she spent a year in the archives while his sort looked away (DIV-075).

## Relationships

- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): his only child.
- [Narcissa Malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md): his wife. She forgave him for the curse; he never forgave himself (DIV-030, Q-019). The veela leave him unmoved at the World Cup (DIV-037) — the Potter boy does not leave his wife unmoved in the same hour, and she teases him with it for years. **She teases him in public and always has, and he bears it**, which is the warmest thing anybody sees a Malfoy do in four books (DIV-116).
- [Severus Snape](severus-snape.md): his protégé since Snape's Sorting, and among the few Snape calls a friend. After school, books by owl and paid commissions. Lucius pays him to brew Narcissa's medicine, and trusts no one else with it (DIV-030). He let Snape take Dracaena on as a pupil at five (DIV-031). Snape teaches her Occlumency in summer 1995 (DIV-020).
