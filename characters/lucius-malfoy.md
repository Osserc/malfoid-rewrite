# Lucius Malfoy

**Role:** Dracaena's father. A Death Eater, and a skilled Occlumens.
**Canon baseline:** Lucius Malfoy. Anything not overridden follows canon.

## The first war

*(settled)* Voldemort's logistics man: procurement, money, contacts. That's what makes the Imperius defence plausible. Officially, he's almost a bureaucrat and a schemer who never did the heinous work himself. Voldemort surely made him kill or torture at least once. Commissioning potions from Snape is part of the job ([severus-snape](severus-snape.md)).

## His daughter

*(settled)* He isn't resentful about having a daughter rather than a son. He thinks a daughter is too precious and fragile, and should be somewhat sheltered. But he's up against two formidable opponents: his doting wife, and his beloved daughter, who almost always gets her way ([dracaena-malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md)). When she wants to fly, he has to give in, and sneaks in his condition: no training without supervision. As the years pass his worries ease, because he sees how capable she is, but he stays very protective. He's strict. Her Boggart is him looking at her with disgust ([remedial-boggart](../scenes/b3/remedial-boggart.md)), but that's her fear, not what he feels. How sheltering her squares with the rest of his story: Q-036.

## Occlumency

A skilled Occlumens, out of the discipline the Malfoys instil (DIV-020). He can't lie to Voldemort's face as Snape does, but in their regular B5 meetings he tweaks what Voldemort perceives through passive Legilimency. Rules: [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md).

## Finding out *(settled)* · DIV-040

Before the Yule Ball, Dracaena writes home twice: to him, that she'll be attending with Hogwarts' champion, without saying which of the two; to her mother, for the backless green dress. He questions Narcissa, who confirms his suspicions while choosing a dress to go with Harry. He's annoyed she's calmly matchmaking their daughter with the Dark Lord's sworn enemy, but reluctantly sees her point: Harry is a good prospect for Dracaena, and she has soured on the Death Eaters. She sends a more modest dress ([the-green-dress](../scenes/b4/the-green-dress.md)). Right after the Ball, Rita Skeeter prints that they went together.

## The spy strategy

- *(settled)* At the end of B4 he orders her to cut ties with Potter, and she adamantly refuses. He resigns himself to protecting her from herself: he comes up with the "keeping tabs on Potter" strategy, and Snape teaches her Occlumency (DIV-013, DIV-020, DIV-040). The scene is one the author has in mind (Q-022).
- *(settled)* **Harry covered for him.** After the Third Task, Harry keeps Lucius's name from Fudge, out of concern for Dracaena, and she's in the hospital wing to see it (DIV-047). When Lucius orders her to cut ties, she throws it back at him: Harry covered for you, so why can't you do it for him, and for me? For Lucius, covering for Potter means deceiving Voldemort, of all people. It calls back her noblesse oblige over Buckbeak (Q-005): a debt of honour has to be repaid. Dumbledore notices the missing name too, and welcomes it as a chance at redemption for Lucius (DIV-046).
- In B5 he tells Voldemort he has planted his daughter as a spy on Potter (DIV-020). He meets Voldemort regularly, but never at the Manor before B6.
- He's in Azkaban from June 1996 (canon default). That summer Voldemort sees in Dracaena's head that she was never a spy (DIV-015). What that costs Lucius: Q-030.

## Relationships

- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): his only child.
- [Narcissa Malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md): his wife. She forgave him for the curse; he never forgave himself (DIV-030, Q-019). The veela leave him unmoved at the World Cup (DIV-037).
- [Severus Snape](severus-snape.md): his protégé since Snape's Sorting, and among the few Snape calls a friend. After school, books by owl and paid commissions. Lucius pays him to brew Narcissa's medicine, and trusts no one else with it (DIV-030). He let Snape take Dracaena on as a pupil at five (DIV-031). Snape teaches her Occlumency in summer 1995 (DIV-020).
