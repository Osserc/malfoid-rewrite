## The Sorting

DIV-033. Ravenclaw, which canon's Hat seriously considered for her.

- **Still lonely in B1.** A competitive House can resent a know-it-all as much as Gryffindor did, and canon's Ravenclaws steal Luna's things. The troll still makes the friendship (canon).
- **Her housemates** include Cho, Luna and Marietta. That makes the DA betrayal sharper: Marietta is one of her own.

## Muggle Studies

*(draft)* DIV-012. She takes it in B3 and drops it, as in canon. She grew up among Muggles and already knows a fair bit, and what's left is mostly how wizards see Muggles, which they mostly get wrong. It's the mirror of Dracaena tutting at Muggles who get magic wrong. A timetable clash between electives gives her the excuse ([hogwarts](../setting/hogwarts.md), Q-002). Dracaena frames it as conceding defeat, while she herself stays on as Burbage's star pupil ([sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).

## Magic *(draft)*

Weaker than most pure-bloods, as a rule for Muggle-borns ([society](../setting/society.md)). She makes up for it with immense technical skill, and excels at charms and enchantments that need precision rather than power: the brains of the operation and a support, not the magical muscle. Dracaena wipes the floor with her at the Duelling Club ([duelling-club](../threads/duelling-club.md)). She can't easily take someone along when she Apparates, least of all when she's already spent ([apparate-dancing](../scenes/b7/apparate-dancing.md)).

## Relationships

- Harry Potter: her friend. She can't resonate with him: friendship isn't enough (DIV-021).
- [Ron Weasley](ron-weasley.md): in Hufflepuff. Her cat in his dormitory turns their B3 feud into a House incident (DIV-036).
- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): her rival from the first Potions lesson, where Dracaena shows her up (DIV-011). Taunts her for dropping Muggle Studies (DIV-012). From B4 she stops slurring her, for Harry's sake. In B6 she calls Hermione "Mudblood" again, plenty, to hurt her ([dracaena-malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md)). *(settled)* In B7 Hermione treats her with kid gloves anyway. She's perceptive, and knows Dracaena wasn't herself in B6, and still isn't: from the Ball to the end of B5 she was not too hostile for a year and a half. At Shell Cottage, her sealing spell saves Narcissa's medicine, and Dracaena starts treating her as a person for good ([narcissa-malfoy](narcissa-malfoy.md)).
- Crookshanks: her cat, and Black's accomplice (DIV-036).
