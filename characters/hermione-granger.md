# Hermione Granger

**Role:** Harry's friend and housemate in Gryffindor. Second to Dracaena in Potions, and hates her for it.
**Canon baseline:** Hermione Granger. Anything not overridden follows canon.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-011, DIV-012, DIV-018, DIV-021, DIV-049, DIV-063, DIV-069, DIV-073, DIV-074

## Core *(draft)*

- **Want:** to be needed. An awkward girl who has finally been handed a role she can play — the capable older sister who keeps everyone fed, safe and cheerful — and who plays it relentlessly and badly.
- **Need:** to be wanted for herself rather than for her usefulness.
- **Wound / fear:** that without the role there is no reason for anyone to keep her.
- **Lie she believes:** that being useful and being loved are the same thing.

**She is not a love interest and never becomes one, and there is no triangle.** Her grip on Harry is a sister's: he is scrawny, he is miserable, and she has decided both are hers to fix. It is affectionate and it is suffocating, and he resents it while being grateful for it.

No divergence is needed for any of this — it is a reading of canon Hermione, and canon beats run on it. The Firebolt in B3 is this engine at full throttle: she would rather be right about his safety than liked, and it costs her the friendship for weeks.

**How it shows in B1** *(settled)*. After he lets slip about the Mirror and the nightmares she attaches herself to him entirely — she will not let him copy her work, she makes him do it beside her so she can catch his mistakes, and she feeds him. He finds it unbearable because it makes him feel weak. When Dracaena calls her a Mudblood in front of him she says nothing about it afterwards, because the sister does not bring her own injuries to the boy she is looking after (DIV-074).

## Muggle Studies

*(draft)* DIV-012. She takes it in B3 and drops it, as in canon. She grew up among Muggles and already knows a fair bit, and what's left is mostly how wizards see Muggles, which they mostly get wrong. It's the mirror of Dracaena tutting at Muggles who get magic wrong. A timetable clash gives her the excuse, as it must without a Time-Turner (Q-002). Dracaena frames it as conceding defeat, while she herself stays on as Burbage's star pupil ([sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).

## Magic *(draft)*

Weaker than most pure-bloods, as a rule for Muggle-borns ([society](../setting/society.md)). She makes up for it with immense technical skill, and excels at charms and enchantments that need precision rather than power: the brains of the operation and a support, not the magical muscle. Dracaena wipes the floor with her at the Duelling Club ([duelling-club](../threads/duelling-club.md)). She can't easily take someone along when she Apparates, least of all when she's already spent ([apparate-dancing](../scenes/b7/apparate-dancing.md)).

## The elves *(draft)* · DIV-075, DIV-077

Hers is the win. The noble houses buried their own shame so completely that nobody alive knows there was anything to bury, and she is the one who digs it up — not from a document, from a hole in the evidence. Elves enter the record abruptly, fully formed, already in service, with no origin, no habitat, no young and no natural history. A species with no natural history is not a species. She finds the shape of the cover-up and never finds the deed.

- **B4 is the collision.** She learns early, and from Harry: he mentions he owns an elf, she is appalled, and Dobby turns up uncalled because he feels Harry thinking about him. Her first-hand account of elf life comes from the elf (DIV-077).
- **Winky dies in front of her,** with Harry, Ron and Dobby there, and **this is where she comes to hate Bartemius Crouch** — permanently, personally, and not as a political objection. She has watched a man's discarded servant take eight months to stop existing because he told her she was not worth housing. She is the only person in the castle who extends him no sympathy through the back half of the book.
- **The hats are undecided** (Q-093). If they stayed they were traps — but she is not the castle's household, so they may have done nothing at all, and nobody can tell her (Q-099).
- **B5 is the research**, dragged out across the year and hung on the business she is already doing ([books/b5/outline.md](../books/b5/outline.md)).
- **It is also DIV-008 inverted.** [painted-life](../setting/painted-life.md) gives the old houses unwritten memory the books don't have. This gives the Muggle-born the history the old houses lost, because they stopped looking at their own shame. Using the same device twice would make it a formula; using it in both directions makes it an argument.
- **At Dobby's death she is the one who can read it.** Everyone present sees the true form; only she knows what it is, and she tells the Malfoys. It is the one moment her research changes anything for anyone (DIV-050).
- **And then she builds the answer.** Not freeing, which kills them: a method for a bound elf to recover its natural state over time, in a household that has decided never to use what it holds. Slow, partial, procedural. She is not powerful and it must not need power — she finds the seam rather than beating the magic. Kreacher is the prototype, at Malfoy Manor, after the war. **Entirely undesigned: Q-100**, and it is her whole S.P.E.W. arc.
- **What she never gets is Harry's remedy**, and he never gets her history. They never put the two together ([house-elves](../setting/house-elves.md)).
- **It pays off B1.** Clea's painted creatures refuse rescue because for them freeing is destroying and there is no remedy at all. The elves are the same shape with a door in it (Q-091, answered).
