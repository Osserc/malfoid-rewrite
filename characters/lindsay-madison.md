# Lindsay Madison

**Role:** Madam Malkin's clerk. The one person in the room who sees Harry and Dracaena's first meeting for what it is.
**Canon baseline:** original character. Canon gives Madam Malkin a silent assistant in later books.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-115

## Core

- **Want:** _open._ To do a good job and be left alone to do it, as far as B1 goes.
- **Need:** _open._
- **Wound / fear:** _open._
- **Lie she believes:** _open._

## What she is for

- **The witness.** Harry doesn't understand what has just happened to him at Madam Malkin's. She does, from the first cold question, and there is nothing she can do but step between them and get him out. Her *tch* is the reader's permission to be angry on his behalf while he's still working it out.
- **The one who doesn't make it about the scar.** She knows who he is — her eyes go to his forehead in the first minute — and what she does with it is wink, ruffle his hair, say she gets stared at all the time, and never mention it again. Everyone at the Leaky Cauldron was kind to him too, and kind *because* he was Harry Potter, which is the thing he is already sick of. **Her restraint is the kindness.** The fringe stays down, which is also why the girl by the window never works out who she is talking to (DIV-009).
- **The shop's economics.** After the Malfoys go she asks why they don't simply refuse them, and gets shown the blank cheque. That's the pure-blood case from the losing side, in book one, with nobody arguing it (DIV-008, DIV-115).

## Voice

A Southern drawl, carried in **word choice and rhythm, never in phonetic spelling**. Eye-dialect is out: it reads as mockery and ages badly.

- Lexis: *git*, *sugar*, *darlin'*, *honey*, *little bitty*, *I declare*, *folks*, *fixing to*, *carry him on over*, *waiting on* rather than *waiting for*.
- Rhythm: the doubled intensifier (*awful passionate*), statements turned up into questions, *well* at the head of a sentence.
- One or two dropped g's a scene, no more.
- Warm by default, and unbothered by rank. She calls an eleven-year-old sugar and the proprietress ma'am in the same breath.

## Appearance

Tall. Pitch-black hair knotted up with oversized dressmaker's pins, no visible tie. A close-cut sable robe that looks to be made of crow feathers, dark enough that she reads as a walking crow — frightening to an eleven-year-old until she opens her mouth, and frightening again on the way out. Dark, almost magnetic eyes, with make-up that makes them intimidating rather than pretty. Early twenties at least; young, but not a girl.

## Relationships

- [Harry Potter](harry-potter.md): she's kind to him for an hour and he remembers it. **She recognises him and says nothing**, which is the whole of it (DIV-115).
- Madam Malkin: her employer, who calls her a handful and means it fondly.
- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): a customer she can't refuse and privately can't stand, and in B4 a girl she gets to watch win ([the-fitting](../scenes/b4/the-fitting.md)).
