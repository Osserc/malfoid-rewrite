# Alastor "Mad-Eye" Moody

**Role:** Defence Against the Dark Arts, 1994–95. Harry's best teacher, and the instrument that delivers him to the graveyard.
**Canon baseline:** retired Auror. In canon he spends the whole of B4 starved in his own trunk while Barty Crouch Jr wears his face, and arrives as a character only in the last chapter.
**Changes from canon:** DIV-086, DIV-095, DIV-096, DIV-097, DIV-098, DIV-099, DIV-100, DIV-101.

## The year

*(settled)* · DIV-086. **It is him.** Every lesson, every drill, every piece of coaching Harry gets this year is the real Alastor Moody's, given sincerely. He is under the Imperius Curse from late August to the night of the Third Task, and Crouch Jr is in the trunk.

- **He is not hollowed out.** The curse does not run him like a puppet; it supplies compliance and, with it, contentment. He is recognisably, entirely himself — paranoid, foul-tempered, brilliant, and a little mad already, which is the whole reason nobody looks twice.
- **There is no lost time.** He remembers everything he did, including the things Crouch did wearing him, and remembers having had good reasons at the time. That is the Imperius working as canon describes it, and it is what makes the end unbearable: he remembers *wanting to*.
- **He fights it, all year.** He surfaces, and Crouch is always a few feet away to put him back under. The grip is maintained, not cast once — which is why Crouch has to be in the castle at all (DIV-086).
- **The one thing he can still do, he does.** He drills the class on resisting the Imperius Curse harder than the curriculum asks for, repeatedly, and Harry is the only one who throws it off. Whether that is the man leaving a trail or the curse letting him indulge a hobby is never settled on the page (Q-114).

## The lessons *(settled)* · DIV-096

He makes the boy formidable, sincerely, and the damage is in the framing rather than in any spell. **He hands over a doctrine, not a syllabus** — *spells are weapons, they are not good or bad* — and in a world where Dark magic runs on intent that is enough to stain him for life (DIV-084). He also tells Harry he would make a good Auror, and means it, which a hunted fourteen-year-old wears like a borrowed jacket for a year.

- **He teaches him nothing to be ashamed of and gives him permission for everything.** Harry has improvised past every problem since he was eleven; what the year removes is the apology.
- **The Conjunctivitis Curse is not even his.** The chain runs Krum, then Sirius approving it, then Harry deciding to learn it. Moody's contribution is the climate (DIV-096).
- **He says the doctrine out loud after the First Task:** *spells are weapons, Potter. Hit them first, hit them hard, and you won't be an easy target when you go down.* **Not *if*.** The gallows humour is how he talks; the fatalism is the part Harry absorbs without noticing (DIV-097, [the-flask](../scenes/b4/the-flask.md)).
- **The whisky is the same doctrine with no spell in it** — a colleague's drink for a boy back off a job, offered as procedure and not as mischief, and the only regard of that kind anyone in the castle offers him. **He also teaches the boy that the rule has exceptions, and that he is one of them** (DIV-097).

- **And none of it is Crouch's doing**, which is the cruelty of it. The lessons are the real man's and they are good lessons, and they are also the thing Harry reaches for in a bathroom in B6 (DIV-043).

## What gives him away *(settled)* · Q-114

The tells are not villainy. **They are a frightened young man coming up for air inside a grizzled Auror**, which is the throughline: Barty Crouch Jr went to Azkaban very young and never finished growing up in there. Leading candidates:

- **He takes a stranger's drink without checking it.** A smell from Crouch's schooldays gets him at Hogsmeade and he takes Moody over to have the thing himself: a mug of Butterbeer, which passes as nostalgia in a hard old man and is worse once you know. **The tell is the missing check, not the drink.** His guard has an off-switch marked *among allies* and the books show it, so drinking what he did not pour would be covered — but a public house is not that room, and he has personally taught Harry to verify before swallowing anything (DIV-097). **Harry watches it happen and files it under *even the best of them slip sometimes***.
- **The flask is innocent, and it is spent proving it.** After the First Task he pours Harry a measure of Firewhisky, every reader of canon has a very bad second, nothing whatever happens — and then *CONSTANT VIGILANCE*, and the lecture that plants the word *check*. The offer is sincere and the test is real and they are the same act (DIV-097, [the-flask](../scenes/b4/the-flask.md)).
- **He startles at the name Winky.** Days after the Yule Ball he asks after Dobby — in character, since a paranoid Auror wants to know why the Malfoy elf is handing Harry Potter socks — and Harry answers that Dobby is in the castle minding another elf. Moody has no reason at all to know the name. Crouch has every reason: she was his family's elf.
- **He keeps nearly saying who did it.** He remembers putting the name in the Goblet and remembers thinking he had a reason, so he would say so if it came up — and all year he opens his mouth about the culprit and then shrugs it off. That is Crouch silencing him for a split second at a time, spending grip he cannot spare (DIV-086). The confession names it in one line.
- And the ones already on the table: the seventh lock, the eye that can see into his own trunk and looks away, and the Imperius lesson pressed far harder than the curriculum asks.
- **The ferret is not a tell at all, and that is worse.** Harry files the cruelty under *that is how Moody does things* and goes on to love the man — so the one act of the year he has to excuse is the only one that was not Moody's (DIV-095).

## He takes himself back

*(settled)* · DIV-086. In Dumbledore's office, with Harry in front of him, Crouch rides him too hot — appetite is the one thing Crouch has never been able to govern — and the grip slackens. Moody uses that instant and throws him out.

It is the hand on the wand wavering, not a speech. He is the reason Harry is alive at the end of the night, and the first thing he is told afterwards is what he spent the year doing.

**It prefigures what he is in the Order.** He loses to the Death Eaters twice in canon and is mostly a cautionary tale; here the reader has watched him break a hold that no one else in Britain could have broken, and B5 gets to show what he can actually do.

## Afterwards

He drilled the boy and delivered him, and Cedric Diggory is dead. He knows the help was real and that the plan needed exactly that help, which is a thing with no answer in it. How much of this is on the page in B5 is open (Q-140); the guilt is not negotiable.

### What he does with it *(settled)* · DIV-099

**He doubles down.** *I made a soldier of him, and it is the one thing I did right last year* — everything else he did in 1994–95 ends at a headstone, so he holds that piece up hard, and it puts him on Sirius Black's side of the Order's argument about how much Harry is told.

- **It follows from B4 and cannot be avoided.** A man who ran a debrief and poured the boy a drink for a job well done cannot argue six months later that he is a child who should be told nothing.
- **He has the file to argue from:** a dragon, a lake, a maze stocked to kill, three charged champions, Voldemort, and a castle stormed by Dementors two years before that (DIV-081). It is a better record than most of his old colleagues ever had.
- **And they lose**, with Lupin as the voice of reason who settles nothing (Q-136). Harry stays in the dark, and the only thing the axis buys him is the knowledge that competent adults said otherwise and were overruled.
- **He is not looking at Harry either.** Sirius sees James; he sees a soldier. **The bill arrives at the Ministry** (DIV-099, Q-140).
- **In public, first, over a drink:** at the prefect party he mentions the Firewhisky in front of Molly Weasley, entirely fondly, and the room divides along the line it will keep all year (DIV-098, [the-prefect-party](../scenes/b5/the-prefect-party.md)).

### The apology *(settled)* · DIV-100

**"I failed you both."** Not the Imperius Curse and not penitence about having enjoyed it: **his shame is professional.** He was taken at his own house before term began and he should have fought it off, and a schoolgirl went down a corridor as a ferret because he did not — **and he knows it could be done, because he did it in the end**, which turns every month he did not into a failure he can measure.

It is a sentence, not a speech. Harry offers him the excuse — *you were under the Imperius* — which is word for word Lucius Malfoy's defence, handed to him by the Malfoy girl's boy, and **he declines it** (Q-117). Then he leaves; he is not asking to be forgiven and does not wait to find out whether he is. He asks Harry to pass it to her rather than going himself (Q-139).

## Relationships

- [Harry Potter](harry-potter.md): his pupil, honestly. What he taught Harry works, and the boy can never sort which parts were Crouch's. What he actually handed over was permission (DIV-096) — and, after the First Task, the belief that he was safe with him (DIV-097). By B5 he is one of the two men arguing that the boy is a grown man (DIV-099).
- Sirius Black: not friends, and on the same side of everything that matters in B5. They reach the same verdict about the Malfoy girl independently, in opposite registers (DIV-101), and they lose the same argument together (DIV-099).
- [Dracaena Malfoy](dracaena-malfoy.md): he transfigured her and thought it just, he owes her an apology he will not deliver in person, and his professional read of her is that she will be the death of Harry or his greatest asset (DIV-100, DIV-101).
- [Minerva McGonagall](minerva-mcgonagall.md): he knows what she was in the first war and respects her for it, and he is the one person who can allude to it in company. He is himself when he says it — the beat belongs to the real man, not to Crouch.
- Albus Dumbledore: an old friend who tested him on arrival with something only the two of them knew, and got it right, because he is Moody.
- Barty Crouch Jr: asleep in his trunk, holding him down, for ten months.
