# Hogwarts

Canon by default. This file records where the school works differently.

## Size · DIV-035

Why wizarding Britain is small and shrinking: [society](society.md). What that means for the school:

- **A third full.** The castle was built for about a thousand pupils, the figure Rowling has given. In the 1990s it holds perhaps a third of that. Hence canon's endless disused classrooms and empty corridors.
- **About forty a year.** Every pupil at Hogwarts during the series was born during the first war, and Harry's year is about forty. The war years are proportionally more Muggle-born.
- **The boom.** Children born after the war start arriving from about 1993, and each Sorting runs a little longer than the last. It's a quiet sign of peace, and in B7 the castle is fuller of young children than it has been in years.
- **On the page,** one line is enough: Hermione, who has read *Hogwarts: A History*, notices the castle was built for far more pupils than it has. After that the prose keeps crowds modest. The Great Hall is never full.
- **Still the only school.** Hogwarts stays the only wizarding school in Britain (canon). Why we didn't add more: [problems/scale.md](../problems/scale.md).

## The Malfoy room *(settled)* · DIV-060

A small round chamber below the dungeons, two corridors from the Slytherin common room, reached by a stair that opens out of a dead end. Nobody knows who built it; it may be another of Salazar Slytherin's. Brutus Malfoy found it while at school, about three centuries ago, charmed it shut behind a password and passed it down the family.

- **Not on the Map.** Neither the room nor anyone inside it shows on the Marauder's Map.
- **The furniture** is Lucius's choice, not his wife's: a canopy bed, a large mirror over the fireplace, a round sofa, a bathtub. The firelight is green, and can be changed at a flick of a wand.
- **The password** came to Dracaena from her mother, in B3, not from her father ([narcissa-malfoy](../characters/narcissa-malfoy.md)).
- **Across the books.** B3: Harry sometimes looks for her at night, finds no Malfoy anywhere on the Map, and thinks nothing of it. B4: he learns where she goes, and she refuses him the password ([the-malfoy-room](../scenes/b4/the-malfoy-room.md)). B5: she gives it to him. B6 *(draft)*: she never goes back. The room stays exactly as they left it while she works in the Room of Requirement, so when Harry misses her on the Map and lets himself in, he finds it cold and dusty and untouched, with his scarf still on the stand. She didn't throw him away; she stopped coming.
- **Two rooms.** The dream has this one, the nightmare has the Room of Requirement ([sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).
