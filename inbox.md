# Inbox

Raw ideas, not yet accepted. Nothing here is canon. When an idea is accepted, move it to its home through the decision workflow in `CLAUDE.md` and delete it from here.

- **B2: Dracaena witnesses Harry kill the Basilisk** with the sword of Gryffindor. Hearing about it is nothing next to seeing it: a killshot for her heart, to capitalise on later. How she gets into the Chamber is open.
- **Lucius and Snape after the fall.** Both stay out of Azkaban while the rest go in, so to the loyalists both are traitors. The curse that hit Narcissa could as easily have been meant for Snape. (Q-040)
- **Her fairy tales at Hogwarts.** She brings them along. Someone in the Slytherin common room catches her rereading *Sleeping Beauty*, asks why the pictures don't move, and spots the scribbles in the margins. It can stay a small hint of how deep the tale went with her, or grow into something more. Snag: a Muggle book in the Slytherin common room puts her secret at risk (DIV-010). Who asks matters (Pansy?), and so does when: after B3, Muggle Studies is a cover ("know your enemy", Q-020). The margins habit comes from Snape (DIV-031).
- **Dracaena at the Department of Mysteries.** She fights there, so she shares the blame for Lucius's failure, and for what Voldemort does to her family over it (Q-030). Snag: fighting the Death Eaters in front of Bellatrix is open treason, not a lie, which collides with DIV-015 (he doesn't care that she "lied"). Voldemort would need a reason to keep her alive (Q-013). It also puts her at Sirius's death, at her aunt's hand.
- **B1: mean because she can't handle rejection.** Her meanness comes from clumsy attempts at friendship with Harry that go wrong.
  - **Norbert:** she finds out about him and tags along under threat of snitching, because she *really* wants to see a baby dragon: cute and dangerous, like snakes. Then she's the one who gets them caught, by mistake. It sours everyone on her and foils her attempts at friendship. Ties in with her dragon Patronus (DIV-027).
  - **The flying lesson:** her broom obeys her perfectly, and she arrogantly offers Harry a demonstration. His obeys at the first "Up!". At his satisfied "Shut up, Malfoy" she barely holds back tears: "I only wanted to teach you…". The Remembrall chase stays: she's getting back at him and grabbing his attention.
- **Luna Lovegood from B2.** *(rough, nothing committed)* She starts Hogwarts in September 1992, so B2 is her real first year: putting her on the page needs no divergence, only page time. Ginny's yearmate, and the Lovegoods live near the Burrow. Back line only — a few funny scenes, and a rumours magnet Dracaena occasionally summons for questioning, which Luna takes for an invitation to spend time together.
  - **How it starts.** Her housemates bully her; Dracaena makes them stop, not out of kindness but because it's squalid, and because the girl is hers to use (DIV-070, DIV-071). Luna takes it for friendship and is unembarrassable about it. The asymmetry is the point: "Miss Malfoy" against a dismissive "Lovegood", and Luna feeling a warmth that is not being given.
  - **Not a mole, ever.** No tradecraft, no secrets, nothing asked of her she could blow. In B2 the school thinks Harry is the Heir, so the Heir rumours are the Harry rumours, and Dracaena needs no investigation plot of her own — just her usual Potter intelligence, two years before Lucius invents "keeping tabs on Potter" as her cover (DIV-040).
  - **B3: the pawn.** With Time-Turners gone, the Buckbeak night needs legwork Dracaena can't do while she's freeing the hippogriff (Q-003, DIV-003, DIV-018). Luna can carry the fake tip that sends the Dementors to the lake, or scout for the rescue, without knowing what any of it is for. Weigh it: if she delivers the tip, a twelve-year-old is materially responsible for the swarm that nearly destroys three people, and Dracaena's confession at the lake might not even mention her, because Luna doesn't register as a person whose involvement counts. Darker than the rest of the subplot, and possibly the best thing in it. The clean alternative is reconnaissance only.
  - **It dissolves.** By B4 Harry and Dracaena are on the same page and there's nothing left to spy on. What survives is Luna defending "some Slytherins" as nice on the grounds that Miss Malfoy is — which the reader knows is approximately false. Candidate voucher for the Slytherins who join the DA (Q-070, [house-unity](threads/house-unity.md)), and it costs her nothing to say it about a girl who is on the Inquisitorial Squad at the time.
  - **The gift beat.** *(possibility)* Luna gives her a present, because she thinks they're friends. Dracaena has nothing to give back and can't bear being out-mannered by a first-year, so she overcorrects with something absurdly expensive that means nothing to Luna, who keeps the wrapping.
  - **Odds and ends.** The glasses: she tries them on because she's an empiricist and can't stand not knowing whether they do anything, and Harry catches her in them with no context to read it by (canon puts Spectrespecs in B6; moving them earlier costs nothing). A Nargle remark in B2 that pays off at the Yule Ball. Luna at the back of the Duelling Club naming the trophy custom for what it is — *she's collecting him* — which nobody believes: a candidate answer to Q-073.
  - **Depends on** what Dracaena is actually doing in B1–B2, which is unplanned. Note also that canon introduces Luna to Harry in B5 through Ginny, as a stranger; that beat changes.
