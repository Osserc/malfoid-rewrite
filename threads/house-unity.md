# House Unity

Cross-House unity as a subplot: Slytherins who turn out to be people, and who are there at the end.

## Shape of the arc

*(draft)* DIV-055. Canon states the theme and never delivers it. The Hat warns the school every year, Dumbledore calls for unity at the end of B4, and the Houses go on hating each other to the last page. Slytherin in particular is a wall of sneers: in seven books almost no Slytherin is allowed to be an ordinary person, and before the battle the entire House is marched out of the castle.

We deliver it, but as a subplot, with canon's own device and nothing structural touched. The main argument is Harry and Dracaena: the romance crosses the deepest House line in the school, with real stakes, in the part of the book the reader is already watching. This thread is what grows around that.

- **The seeding (B1–B6).** A handful of named Slytherins behave like ordinary people. Not allies, not secretly nice: just pupils with homework, friends and bad days, who don't join in when Dracaena's circle is being vile. It costs a line here and there, and it means the ending isn't a conversion.

- **The DA (B5).** Some of them join, and the entry is where the theme pays rather than asserts. Canon bars Slytherins outright on distrust, so it has to cost something and somebody has to vouch: **Ron vouches for Pansy and Hermione objects** (DIV-125). Balthier and Nola are in their last year. Dracaena joins too, and co-teaches. Full entry below.
- **The battle (B7).** When McGonagall clears Slytherin House out of the castle, some refuse to go. They fight, and they're alongside Slughorn at the end (DIV-023, DIV-025).

The measure of the theme is who is standing in the Great Hall at the finish: not a House, a mixture.

### The DA, and who gets into it *(draft)* · DIV-125

**Pansy joins on Ron's word. Dracaena joins and teaches. The warning about the raid is hers.**

Q-070 had already cleared the Squad objection — in B5 she is dreaming and barely does the job (DIV-013) — and left only whether she knows. **She knows because Harry tells her.** By B5 he trusts her and there was never a version where she didn't find out.

- **The order matters, and it is the point.** Ron tells Pansy about the Hog's Head, not from malice but because he does not experience her as a security risk. Pansy spends days working up to asking Dracaena to come with her, because she won't walk in alone. Dracaena, bored, has known for a week, from Potter. **Pansy's initiative survives and is immediately made small by Dracaena's access,** which is her whole life in one exchange: the reader feels it and Dracaena doesn't notice. ([ron-pansy](ron-pansy.md), DIV-121)
- **Ron is in the wrong, characteristically.** The Hog's Head is Hermione's meeting and her security — she brings the jinxed parchment because she is worried about leaks, and the leak is Ron, before the meeting happens. **That gives her objection a documented breach rather than a prejudice**, which is the difference between an argument and a bias.
- **Both sign the parchment.** Pansy signs knowing her family may one day make her choose. Dracaena signs without reading it and, on learning what it does, says something approving about Granger through her teeth.
- **Dracaena's motive is the club, not the cause.** Umbridge shut the Duelling Club and both of them are miffed about it (DIV-028): **the DA is the club in exile**, and she is reclaiming her piste. She would be insulted by any other description, and no obligation is involved anywhere.
- **Why she teaches.** She is Harry's equal and the better technician, so the room gets the argument the duelling thread already wrote — **her school against Moody's**, pin-shields and knowing the counter against move and put matter in the way (DIV-108, DIV-109). Her objection to cover is *concession*. Forty students watch the two best duellists in the school disagree about whether taking cover is cowardice. **She drills form; Harry drills what to do when form fails.** It costs him nothing, and it plants Q-144.
- **She is challenged twice.** October, as a prefect who is publicly Potter's girlfriend. April, when the Squad forms and she pins on Umbridge's badge while still attending a secret anti-Umbridge club — far worse, and by then the room has had six months of her actually turning up. She defends neither badge; per DIV-013 both are toys and she is baffled anyone thinks it is a question.
- **The raid.** Marietta still triggers it, and **Dracaena is the counter — the Squad is what makes it possible**, since she is in the room when Umbridge musters. The badge finally does something. She says she did it to spite the toad; nobody believes her; the room decides she did it for Potter; both are true and she would die before conceding the second (the scarf engine, DIV-009, DIV-059). **It cannot save Harry**, because Dumbledore taking the blame is load-bearing: everyone else scatters and Harry is last out, because he would be. Who catches him is parked (Q-158).
- **One at a time, on a named person's word** *(draft)*. That is the rule that keeps the entry expensive and stops the Slytherins arriving as a bloc. **Pansy on Ron's. Balthier on Harry's**, off the B3 corridor conversation where he told Harry not to be ashamed of fainting — and Balthier brings Nola. **Pansy recruits nobody and tells nobody**: making her a channel would undo DIV-093. It leaves the slot open for the younger cohort Q-070 still wants.
- **It is also where Dracaena finds out she is the exception** *(draft)*. She comes in to sneer at cover as a crutch and discovers that the pin-shield is not something the school does slightly worse but something it **cannot do** — the first time she has been measured against a sample of the whole castle rather than against Slytherin and one boy. **Which hands her side of the argument live ammunition:** if what she can do at fifteen is unreproducible in a room of forty, the craft is being lost, and that is the decline in miniature (DIV-035, DIV-008). Contemptuous and correct at once. Full entry: [dracaena-malfoy](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md), DIV-109.
- **What gets Pansy past her distaste for offensive magic** is not the cause. She is in danger whichever way her family goes, and **this time she was asked rather than dragged** — the same girl who walked out of the Duelling Club in B2, making the opposite choice (DIV-121, DIV-122).

### The people *(draft)* · DIV-082

- **Balthier Thynne.** Fifth year in B3, so seventh in B5 and gone by B7 — he comes back for the battle of his own accord. One of two survivors of his family: his father Basil, a half-blood social climber who married into the Strix family and attached himself to Voldemort for the standing, died in McGonagall's raid on Strix palace (DIV-079). He was three. What he has of it is an image and not a story — fire, a figure moving slowly above the ruins, a laugh — and a name his mother gave him for it. **He has known since before he could decide what to do about it that the witch who burned his house teaches him Transfiguration**, and he has never once let on.
  - **He already thinks his father was wrong**, which is the hinge the whole character turns on and the reason he can love a Muggle-born at all. So he is carrying a grievance he cannot morally justify and cannot put down: the woman who ruined his life did it for the right reasons.
  - **What the Dementors give him is the fire**, not the laugh. He has never been certain he did not invent the laugh. That is what the siege is for: he hears it again, in the open, over the castle, and it was real.
  - **And he is not pitying Harry.** He does not faint, so he assumes he has the easier end of it, and reasons from there that whatever a third-year is hearing must be worse than what he hears — and what he hears is bad enough. He takes Harry aside once to say he has nothing to be ashamed of, mostly talking to himself. He is the only person in the castle who works it out, and he is right.
  - **He is good at Transfiguration.** Of all subjects. Nobody remarks on it and nobody knows why it would be worth remarking on.
  - **She knows exactly who he is.** She never forgets a mission. She does not treat him differently in any way anyone could point to; what we get, in his POV, is that her eye rests on him half a second longer than on anyone else, often enough that he has stopped telling himself he imagined it. Neither of them has ever said a word.
  - **Held, not planned:** that she says something to him when he leaves the school, in B5. Maybe. Not a commitment.
  - **He is our route to Hellfire's past.** The early glimpses come through him, as pressure in a classroom and never as exposition. The Strix raid itself stays unstated in B3; the full story is B5 or later.
- **Nola Laurel.** His girlfriend, same year, **Muggle-born and in Slytherin**, no connection to Voldemort. Taller than he is. She is the cheapest strong statement available for this thread: the Hat sorts on ambition, so the House's ideology is a lie it tells about itself. Balthier has been hiding her from his mother; after the siege he stops.
  - **She is not the frightened one.** No more than anyone is with a Dementor swarm overhead. She is the one who got him through the Quidditch match, which puts the pair on the page months before the siege and means that by June she already knows what the fire does to him — and stands there with him anyway.
- **Arvilla Strix.** His mother. Pureblood heiress, not of the Sacred Twenty-Eight, the only other survivor of her house.
  - **The fire marked her and let her live**, which was arithmetic and not mercy: it found little in her, and nothing at all in a three-year-old ([magic](../setting/magic.md), DIV-080). She carries handprint burns from the night her husband died.
  - **So Balthier has grown up knowing what Lustrum scars look like.** He has never seen McGonagall's; the day he does is available and unplanned, and it belongs to B5 or later with the rest of the Strix material.
  - **And it sharpens what he is asking in the summer of 1994.** The woman he wants to accept a Muggle-born is wearing the verdict of the war on her body — except it was never a verdict, which neither of them will ever know (DIV-080).
- **Pansy Parkinson** *(settled)*. Dracaena's real friend, in the DA, and one of those who refuses the evacuation — the direct inversion of canon, where Pansy calls for Harry to be handed over. **A pureblood who cannot compete with purebloods, whose safety in her own House is borrowed from a Malfoy** — which is what makes refusing the evacuation cost her something. She is in the DA on Ron Weasley's word and ahead of Dracaena. DIV-055, DIV-121, DIV-122, DIV-125, [pansy-parkinson](../characters/pansy-parkinson.md)
- **Daphne Greengrass** *(settled)*. Not one of ours. The snake in the grass, and therefore the candidate for Rita's source (Q-038). She is a POV at the siege precisely because she is unpleasant: the reader gets the spectacle through someone who despises the people feeling things in front of her. **She is also the next queen bee, and she starts reaching for it in B3** (DIV-094).

## What Slytherin notices *(settled)* · DIV-094

The secret cannot be kept by authorial convenience in a House of forty people sharing a common room, so from B3 it is not kept. Two of them act on it, and the badges in B4 are late rather than first.

- **The scarf arrives in B3.** She wins it off Harry at the last duel of their second year (DIV-059). **She never wears it to be seen in it** — wool in warm weather looks ridiculous and her taste forbids that (DIV-070) — so it simply becomes the one she reaches for whenever it is cold, which by B3 is automatic and unexamined. Most visible on a winter Hogsmeade weekend: a Slytherin in Gryffindor colours in a crowd of scarves. "Trophy" covers it for a while and then stops ([duelling-club](duelling-club.md)).
- **B3 breaks the arithmetic.** She trains herself into the ground to beat him *properly* rather than merely beat him (DIV-017, DIV-069), and goes on needling him the whole time. The two do not fit, and people can see them not fitting.
- **The needling never stops,** which is what makes it such good cover — through B4 up to the Ball and for the rest of their lives. The undertone changes; the surface never does.
- **Daphne moves in B3.** A distracted queen bee is a vacancy, and she notices vacancies early. Her candidacy as Rita's source gains a year (Q-038). **Her first real ammunition is the Defence exam** ([the-trunk](../scenes/b3/the-trunk.md)): deniable, which is her method — she states the true thing, that Dracaena couldn't handle a Boggart, and lets the queue draw the line. She has no idea what she is actually pointing at.
- **Pansy notices in B3 too,** and has both the most data and the least appetite for using it. In B4 the missing badge is the thread that finally will not go away (DIV-093). **At the exam Lupin hands her a shaking Dracaena and explains nothing, and she doesn't ask** — the difference between her and Daphne in one gesture, and the proof that an arranged friendship can be a real one.

## The other pole: who makes the badges *(settled)* · DIV-093

The thread needs its ugliness owned by somebody, or Slytherin's cruelty reads as weather. B4's badges are where that gets a name.

- **Canon has Draco make them.** Ours cannot: a Malfoy is a nobleman, not a craftsman, and nobody in that family has made anything with their hands in three hundred years.
- **The Hufflepuffs make them,** being the industrious House with the champion in the running. Theirs say SUPPORT CEDRIC DIGGORY and mean it, with nothing about Harry on them at all.
- **The insult is added by an older Slytherin** — a sixth year, so the year above Dracaena and the same year as Balthier and Nola, **one of the people who made Nola Laurel's life a misery** for being Muggle-born (DIV-082), delighted to have a new target and a whole school to hand it to. Hufflepuff loyalty supplies the badge; he supplies the other side of it. **He joins the Inquisitorial Squad in B5** — which puts him in the same room as Dracaena, who is in it as a toy to get Harry alone (DIV-013, Q-070), standing next to the boy who made POTTER STINKS and saying nothing. He needs a name: **Q-129.**
- **Ruled out, each for its own reason.** Daphne is a schemer rather than a ringleader of merchandise, and is already carrying Rita's source. Pansy is Dracaena's real friend. Crabbe and Goyle are trolls and could not have had the idea.
- **Dracaena does not wear one,** and **Pansy notices** — the friend clocking a badge that is not there, which is a candidate first thread in her working out there is something between those two (DIV-093).
- **What it buys the thread:** the same House produces Nola, Balthier, Pansy and this man in the same corridor, and the badge sorts the school by who pins it on.
