# House Unity

Cross-House unity as a subplot: Slytherins who turn out to be people, and who are there at the end.

## Shape of the arc

*(draft)* DIV-055. Canon states the theme and never delivers it. The Hat warns the school every year, Dumbledore calls for unity at the end of B4, and the Houses go on hating each other to the last page. Slytherin in particular is a wall of sneers: in seven books almost no Slytherin is allowed to be an ordinary person, and before the battle the entire House is marched out of the castle.

We deliver it, but as a subplot, with canon's own device and nothing structural touched. The main argument is Harry and Dracaena: the romance crosses the deepest House line in the school, with real stakes, in the part of the book the reader is already watching. This thread is what grows around that.

- **The seeding (B1–B6).** A handful of named Slytherins behave like ordinary people. Not allies, not secretly nice: just pupils with homework, friends and bad days, who don't join in when Dracaena's circle is being vile. It costs a line here and there, and it means the ending isn't a conversion.

- **The DA (B5).** Some of them join. Canon bars Slytherins outright, and the reason given is distrust, so getting them in has to cost something and someone has to vouch. Pansy is the friend (DIV-055); Balthier and Nola are in their last year. Dracaena herself is in the Inquisitorial Squad that year (DIV-013), which pulls hard the other way: see Q-070.
- **The battle (B7).** When McGonagall clears Slytherin House out of the castle, some refuse to go. They fight, and they're alongside Slughorn at the end (DIV-023, DIV-025).

The measure of the theme is who is standing in the Great Hall at the finish: not a House, a mixture.

### The people *(draft)* · DIV-082

- **Balthier Thynne.** Fifth year in B3, so seventh in B5 and gone by B7 — he comes back for the battle of his own accord. One of two survivors of his family: his father Basil, a half-blood social climber who married into the Strix family and attached himself to Voldemort for the standing, died in McGonagall's raid on Strix palace (DIV-079). Balthier was an infant and remembers the golden flames and a witch flying slowly over the ruins, and nothing else. The Dementors wreck him all through B3. He envies Harry for having it easier, which is wrong of him, and he takes Harry aside once to say he has nothing to be ashamed of for fainting — mostly talking to himself.
- **Nola Laurel.** His girlfriend, same year, **Muggle-born and in Slytherin**, no connection to Voldemort. Taller than he is. She is the cheapest strong statement available for this thread: the Hat sorts on ambition, so the House's ideology is a lie it tells about itself. Balthier has been hiding her from his mother; after the siege he stops.
- **Arvilla Strix.** His mother. Pureblood heiress, not of the Sacred Twenty-Eight, the only other survivor of her house.
- **Pansy Parkinson** *(settled)*. Dracaena's real friend, in the DA, and one of those who refuses the evacuation — the direct inversion of canon, where Pansy calls for Harry to be handed over. DIV-055
- **Daphne Greengrass** *(settled)*. Not one of ours. The snake in the grass, and therefore the candidate for Rita's source (Q-038). She is a POV at the siege precisely because she is unpleasant: the reader gets the spectacle through someone who despises the people feeling things in front of her.

## Open

Q-070 (how many more, how they get into the DA, and the collision with the Inquisitorial Squad). The friend is answered: Pansy.

**The spread is the live problem.** Balthier and Nola leave after B5; Pansy and Daphne are Dracaena's year and are in the castle for B7. So the thread currently sits in two cohorts and has nobody younger. A couple from Ginny's year or below cost nothing to seed now.
