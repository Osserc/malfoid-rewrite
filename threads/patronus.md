# The Patronus

Dracaena's Patronus, and how it mirrors Harry's. Hers tracks her faith in the dream: mist while she studies it for him, corporeal in the dream, gone in the nightmare, back when she wakes. His B3 Patronus runs on memories of her, and hers on memories of him.

## Shape of the arc

*(settled)* DIV-027. She first studies the charm for Harry's sake and can't cast it, not even at the lake, where he saves everyone with a memory of her. She resolves to master it, but gets only mist until the Yule Ball gives her a memory strong enough. After the death of faith she can't cast one at all. At the Battle of Hogwarts it comes back.

**Form:** a dragon (Q-034). Her name means she-dragon, and "princess or dragon" is her question ([sleeping-beauty](sleeping-beauty.md)).

## Proposals (not yet agreed)

- **The same memory.** In B7 she casts from the Yule Ball again. In B6 it fails because she believes the dream was never real. In B7 it works because she believes again. The memory didn't change; she did. That fits "she wakes herself" ([sleeping-beauty](sleeping-beauty.md)).
- **The slot.** In canon, during the battle, Harry's Patronus falters against the Dementors and Luna, Ernie and Seamus step in. Here it's her.
- **B6 on the page.** She tries and gets nothing, not even mist. It shows the death of faith without anyone explaining it.
- **All hers.** The power is hers alone. The stained soul still blocks resonance before the forest (DIV-021).
