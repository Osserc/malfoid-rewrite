# Sleeping Beauty: the Dream and the Nightmare

Dracaena's private fairy tale. It begins with one sincere compliment, and it carries her arc from dream to nightmare.

## The motif

- **Origin:** at Madam Malkin's, Harry asks whether she's Sleeping Beauty (DIV-009).
- **The books:** she wants to know what he saw, so Narcissa's contact brings her "a lot" of Muggle books (DIV-010). Who the contact is: Q-018.
- **The versions:** Harry knows only the Disney film. She owns many versions of the tale, plus a picture book of the film, which is where she finds the purple-eyed princess and realises what he saw when he looked at her. The written versions don't match his: no purple eyes, no dragon, and Perrault's carries on past the wedding into a nightmare.
- **Her voice:** she tuts at the silly Muggles who got real magic wrong. Poisons and sleeping draughts interest her as much as the stories do.
- **Our Sleeping Beauty is active** *(settled)*. She saves Buckbeak, she's a competent witch, and by the end she isn't woken: she wakes herself and holds the wand (DIV-006). She can't wake from nothing, though. By B7 she's a walking dead, and it takes Harry's initiative to reach her: he comes to the Manor to apologise. The choice to fight, and to ask him for help for her family, is hers (DIV-052).

## The dream and the nightmare

The dream is believing she can live outside the script, away from her family's allegiance to Voldemort. Harry is the one who lets her. The nightmare is the script reclaiming her.

### The death of faith *(settled)* · DIV-015

She is Marked that summer (canon default), and meets Voldemort for the first time. Unlike canon Draco, she's never proud of the Mark. She's broken from the outset: ashamed of it, defiled by it, and it's a constant reminder of the chains that hold her. She has studied Occlumency (DIV-020) and spent an almost blissful fifth year with Harry, and Voldemort shatters her walls with ease. He doesn't care that she "lied". He wants obedience and people broken into submission, and her love for Potter is only more suffering to inflict.

That's what convinces her Harry can't possibly win: Voldemort is too powerful. She sinks into despair, panics, and goes along with the assassination plot to at least save her parents. Back at school she can't let go of Harry, and can't believe in him either.

Where the meeting happens, and what Voldemort makes of Lucius's spy story: Q-030.

### The hollow weeks and the breakup *(settled)* · DIV-015

She doesn't end it at once. For a few weeks, less time than it sounds, she tries to stay with Harry, but she's despondent, nothing like the cheerful girl he knows.

**Why she acts this way.** The conflict is internal. She has decided the dream is over, and she feels defiled by the Mark. Still she tries, fruitlessly, to snatch a little happiness through Harry. She's desperate for crumbs of the dream, and that's where the aggressiveness comes from.

- **Only physical, on her terms.** When they're together she kisses him, violently, and otherwise almost ignores him. But she won't let him touch her, and insists on it: she's ashamed of the Mark.
- **No talking.** Harry is still raw from Sirius's death and expected his girlfriend to support him. She won't talk. She shuts him up with kisses, and gets upset when he protests.
- **Gone.** She's already working in the Room of Requirement, and Harry notices she keeps disappearing. When he asks where she goes and why they aren't together more, she snaps at him.
- **Her ammunition.** She takes his complaints as accusing her of treating him like an object and of not being with him enough, both at once. When he says he's frustrated she rejects his touch, she turns it around: he only ever saw her as a thing. She's wrong, and she's equivocating on purpose, but it gives her ammunition. She fires it in the Hospital Wing: that's all she was to him ([after-sectumsempra](../scenes/b6/after-sectumsempra.md)).
- **Letting go of the rest.** She quits the Quidditch team ([quidditch](quidditch.md)).

The break comes when Flitwick reopens the Duelling Club. Harry sets up a "date": he tells her, and says he's missed their bouts. She refuses, they fight, and it ends badly ([the-breakup](../scenes/b6/the-breakup.md), Q-031). She never goes back to the club ([duelling-club](duelling-club.md)). From then until Sectumsempra she believes Harry should hate her, and tries desperately to make him: she can't see how he could ever love her after Voldemort co-opted her. It's a fine dance, because part of her still wishes her prince were real. Sectumsempra is the final nail in the coffin: in his arms, in a pool of her own blood, she asks whether he hates her this much ([sectumsempra](../scenes/b6/sectumsempra.md)). She still loves him.

## The Muggle line

Fairy tales lead to novels, and novels lead to Muggle Studies, where she becomes Charity Burbage's star pupil (DIV-012). *(draft)* Hermione takes it in B3 and drops it ([hermione-granger](../characters/hermione-granger.md)), and Dracaena frames it as conceding defeat. The jab doubles as cover: the pure-blood heiress keeps the subject the Muggle-born dropped, out of a real interest she can't admit. Her parents pay for it in B7 (DIV-016). When she starts, and how it gets out: Q-020.

## Echoes

Motif echoes to use at the moments that carry weight, not everywhere.

- **The Yule Ball** is the dance at the end of the film.
- **The Second Task** is the enchanted sleep, with Harry crossing danger to bring her back.
- **The little basilisk** plays princess or dragon for laughs: Fleur casts Dracaena as the villainess and concedes to "a fierce dragon" ([the-little-basilisk](../scenes/b4/the-little-basilisk.md)).
- **Sixteen:** she turns 16 on 5 June 1996 and is Marked that summer. The Mark is the spindle.
- **The Draught of Living Death**, the tale's curse as a potion, bookends B1 (she knows it, he doesn't) and B6 (he beats her with the Prince's notes) (DIV-011).
- **The Mirror of Erised** shows her the Yule Ball. The memory that gave her a Patronus is now a wish she can only watch (DIV-043, [patronus](patronus.md)). Harry, for a second, sees their wedding: he can still see a future, while she retreats to the past. The Mirror breaks in the fight: the vision is gone, and whoever wants it now has to work for it in the real world. Never spelled out. In the Hospital Wing she asks Harry why he couldn't let her have her sweet dream ([after-sectumsempra](../scenes/b6/after-sectumsempra.md)).
- **The Prince.** She tells Harry princes are supposed to save their princesses. The spell that cut her came from the Half-Blood Prince's book.
- **Her Patronus** tracks the dream: mist until the Yule Ball, corporeal in the dream, gone in the nightmare, back when she wakes ([patronus](patronus.md)).
- **The final duel** is love breaking the curse (DIV-006).
- **Her name:** Dracaena is from *drakaina*, a she-dragon. In Harry's version of the tale the villain becomes a dragon. Princess or dragon is the question she keeps facing. Her Patronus is a dragon (DIV-027).
