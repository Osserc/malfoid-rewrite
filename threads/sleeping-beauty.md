# Sleeping Beauty: the Dream and the Nightmare

Dracaena's private fairy tale. It begins with one sincere compliment, and it carries her arc from dream to nightmare.

## The motif

- **Origin:** at Madam Malkin's, Harry asks whether she's Sleeping Beauty (DIV-009).
- **The books:** she wants to know what he saw, so Narcissa's contact brings her "a lot" of Muggle books (DIV-010). Who the contact is: Q-018.
- **The versions:** Harry knows only the Disney film. She owns many versions of the tale, plus a picture book of the film, which is where she finds the purple-eyed princess and realises what he saw when he looked at her. The written versions don't match his: no purple eyes, no dragon, and Perrault's carries on past the wedding into a nightmare.
- **Her voice:** she tuts at the silly Muggles who got real magic wrong. Poisons and sleeping draughts interest her as much as the stories do.
- **Our Sleeping Beauty is active** *(settled)*. She saves Buckbeak, she's a competent witch, and by the end she isn't woken: she wakes herself and holds the wand (DIV-006).

## The dream and the nightmare

The dream is believing she can live outside the script, away from her family's allegiance to Voldemort. Harry is the one who lets her. The nightmare is the script reclaiming her.

### The death of faith *(settled)* · DIV-015

She is Marked that summer (canon default), and meets Voldemort for the first time. She has studied Occlumency (DIV-020) and spent an almost blissful fifth year with Harry, and Voldemort shatters her walls with ease. He doesn't care that she "lied". He wants obedience and people broken into submission, and her love for Potter is only more suffering to inflict.

That's what convinces her Harry can't possibly win: Voldemort is too powerful. She sinks into despair, panics, and goes along with the assassination plot to at least save her parents. At the start of B6 she breaks up with Harry, brutally enough to lead in the end to Sectumsempra (Q-031).

Where the meeting happens, and what Voldemort makes of Lucius's spy story: Q-030.

## The Muggle line

Fairy tales lead to novels, and novels lead to Muggle Studies, where she becomes Charity Burbage's star pupil (DIV-012). *(draft)* Hermione takes it in B3 and drops it ([hermione-granger](../characters/hermione-granger.md)), and Dracaena frames it as conceding defeat. The jab doubles as cover: the pure-blood heiress keeps the subject the Muggle-born dropped, out of a real interest she can't admit. Her parents pay for it in B7 (DIV-016). When she starts, and how it gets out: Q-020.

## Echoes

Motif echoes to use at the moments that carry weight, not everywhere.

- **The Yule Ball** is the dance at the end of the film.
- **The Second Task** is the enchanted sleep, with Harry crossing danger to bring her back.
- **The little basilisk** plays princess or dragon for laughs: Fleur casts Dracaena as the villainess and concedes to "a fierce dragon" ([the-little-basilisk](../scenes/b4/the-little-basilisk.md)).
- **Sixteen:** she turns 16 on 5 June 1996 and is Marked that summer. The Mark is the spindle.
- **The Draught of Living Death**, the tale's curse as a potion, bookends B1 (she knows it, he doesn't) and B6 (he beats her with the Prince's notes) (DIV-011).
- **Her Patronus** tracks the dream: mist until the Yule Ball, corporeal in the dream, gone in the nightmare, back when she wakes ([patronus](patronus.md)).
- **The final duel** is love breaking the curse (DIV-006).
- **Her name:** Dracaena is from *drakaina*, a she-dragon. In Harry's version of the tale the villain becomes a dragon. Princess or dragon is the question she keeps facing. Her Patronus is a dragon (DIV-027).

## Props

- Her fairy-tale books, with her corrections in the margins. A hidden person found in a book's margins, which echoes the Prince's textbook in B6. *(draft)* She picked up the habit from the potions book Snape sent her as a child, annotated in his hand (DIV-031).
