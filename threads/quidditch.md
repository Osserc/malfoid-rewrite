# Quidditch

The other arena of their rivalry, alongside the [Duelling Club](duelling-club.md): two Seekers who can't stop watching each other.

## Shape of the arc

*(settled)* Once both are on their house teams, they always watch each other's matches. Harry is fascinated by how unreservedly happy she looks flying, and she loves the attention. When he flies, she stares daggers at him from the stands, and being watched like a hawk leaves him uneasy. She takes overly detailed notes on his style, and some of them aren't technical at all, like his wind-swept hair.

The game itself needs reworking to carry a real rivalry: Seeker duels have to be a thing, not a Snitch caught in the first few minutes (Q-037).
