# Harry × Dracaena

The romance at the centre of the series. The two of them as people are in [harry-potter](../characters/harry-potter.md) and [dracaena-malfoy](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md); this thread is what happens between them. Her side of it runs through [the Sleeping Beauty thread](sleeping-beauty.md). When each of them realises or admits their feelings is tracked in [continuity/reveals.md](../continuity/reveals.md). Their Patronuses mirror each other: [patronus](patronus.md). Their rivalry plays out at the [Duelling Club](duelling-club.md) and in [Quidditch](quidditch.md).

## Shape of the arc

Her side: a fairy tale she builds from one sincere compliment, a grudge when the prince refuses the script, a slow repair as he proves himself, then the dream in B4–B5, the death of faith before B6, and the nightmare. At Christmas he comes to the Manor to apologise, and she wakes: she decides to fight and joins him (DIV-049, DIV-052). By the time he walks to the forest they're back together, and he tells her never to give up hope. This time she doesn't despair (DIV-022, DIV-023).

His side: like canon, he wants little to do with her and regrets ever being kind. Still, the Duelling Club becomes a game they both enjoy, and he finds her rare, unguarded "I won!" endearing (DIV-028). At her matches he's fascinated by how happy she looks flying; at his, being watched like a hawk unsettles him ([quidditch](quidditch.md)). B3 turns him:
- **Potions:** her strange sweetness weirds him out (DIV-017).
- **The Patronus lessons:** she spies on them and studies the charm for him. In the lap-pillow scene he catches her smiling at him. He writes the scene off as a prank and won't count it as a happy memory until the lake, but he keeps going back to that smile and finding it very pretty, which confuses him (DIV-018).
- **The lake:** it all adds up. She stole Buckbeak to save him, the smile and the Patronus study weren't jokes, and now she's trying to drive the Dementors off herself. There's something more to this girl (DIV-018).

By the World Cup in summer 1994 he's already in love without knowing it. Fresh off the lake, where she saved his godfather, he sees her again and can't explain to himself why he's happy to, and the veela don't touch him (DIV-037). In B4 Sirius plants the idea of asking her to the Ball (DIV-019), and she's the only one he asks: Cho never comes into it (DIV-041). He can be harsher than canon, with his Horcrux playing a bigger part (Q-024). In B6 he spends the whole year angry over the breakup, and his heart isn't in Dumbledore's lessons. In B7 he breaks off from the Manor raid to apologise to her, sure she no longer wants him (DIV-049). Later he walks to the forest to die for Dumbledore's plan, and at King's Cross he goes back for her, not for the job (DIV-048).

## Cho, only in her head *(settled)* · DIV-041

Harry never asks Cho to the Ball. Dracaena is the only one he asks. Cho is a rival only in Dracaena's head:

- **The World Cup.** She sees the veela leave him cold (DIV-037), so she knows he's in love, and assumes it's Cho, whom she's seen him notice. She watches his matches like a hawk ([quidditch](quidditch.md)).
- **The autumn.** She's jealous of Cho.
- **The invitation** throws her. Fleur's line settles it ([the-little-basilisk](../scenes/b4/the-little-basilisk.md)).

Why Harry never asks Cho: asking Ravenclaw's Seeker first and only then Dracaena would cheapen the invitation. Cho has nothing on her anyway: a few interactions with Harry, against a fellow Seeker who is always around him. Building Cho up would mean writing her into more scenes, only to throw it all away when Cedric wins her and Harry is turned down. With Dracaena the outcome is inevitable, and Harry has been primed all summer.

## Dumbledore's blessing *(settled)* · DIV-046

He approves of them, and encourages them more than once. What he says each time is open.

- **B4, the eve of the Second Task.** When he puts the hostages to sleep (canon), he has a word with her ([second-task](../scenes/b4/second-task.md)).
- **End of B4.** He has noticed Harry keep Lucius's name from Fudge for her sake, and welcomes it: Harry is offering Lucius a chance at redemption, out of love for his daughter (DIV-047). He tells Harry that Dracaena is in a very tough spot, with her father a Death Eater.
- **End of B5.** As Harry rages in his office over Sirius's death (canon), Dumbledore tells him to treasure the love he has left, and not only his friends'. Soon after, she comforts him (DIV-014, [after-sirius](../scenes/b5/after-sirius.md)).
- **B6.** On the night of the cave, he says something cryptic about Sectumsempra (DIV-044, [dumbledores-counsel](../scenes/b6/dumbledores-counsel.md)).

## The night terrors *(settled)* · DIV-061

After the Third Task Harry can't sleep without a draught. He wakes the dormitory screaming for two nights, and on the third morning she sends him a folded paper snake with a time and a place.

- **She tries a potion first,** and fails, which he has never seen her do. She can brew Dreamless Sleep in her sleep and refuses to: it's addictive, and Pomfrey rations it for a reason.
- **So she offers her lap,** as in B3, and reads while he sleeps. He has no nightmares. For the rest of term he sleeps in her room and goes back at dawn ([the-malfoy-room](../scenes/b4/the-malfoy-room.md), DIV-060).
- **The green light.** The room's firelight is the colour of the curse that killed his parents and Cedric. She reads it off his face in the mirror and changes it, without a word and without asking. Not asking is the kindness, and he never notices it happen.
- **What she learns.** She set out to solve him with a potion and finds out she's the answer. She doesn't say so, and neither does the prose.
- **The callback.** This is the lap pillow (DIV-018) repeated where neither of them can pretend it was a prank. Harry counted the first one as a joke until the lake; he can't do that with this one.
- What happens to the night terrors once term ends: Q-075.

## The summer letters *(draft)* · DIV-065

Through the summer of 1995 she writes to him at Privet Drive. Hedwig is far too visible and his post is being watched, so Dobby carries them: free, lodging at the Burrow, and willing to do it for Harry. He can, because a free elf is anchored and not confined (Q-076, answered; DIV-075). She has to ask a favour of the elf her family owned — and, by everything she knows, of something that should have died when Potter freed it three years ago (DIV-076, Q-098).

Canon's isolation stands and gets worse. The people who claim to be protecting him tell him nothing; the one person treating him like a person is a Death Eater's daughter, and his friends aren't writing because they were told not to — on top of the rift they parted on (DIV-063). The Order finds out eventually and is appalled. Sirius takes her side.

## Velvet gloves · DIV-054

*(settled)* After the Manor, Harry isn't over Sectumsempra. He's afraid of hurting her, and handles her with velvet gloves: it shows in how gently he Apparates her ([apparate-dancing](../scenes/b7/apparate-dancing.md)).

*(draft)* It greatly annoys her. She enjoyed the rough and tumble of their rivalry, the duelling and the Quidditch ([duelling-club](duelling-club.md), [quidditch](quidditch.md)). Eventually she confronts him about Sectumsempra and asks him to stop. When, where, and what she says: Q-069.
