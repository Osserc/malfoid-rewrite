# The Trunk

## Purpose

The proof that she is **not over it**. The Boggart happens in late autumn and then goes quiet ([remedial-boggart](remedial-boggart.md)); without this the reader has no evidence it is still in her. The exam puts that evidence in June, **days before the lake**, so the night at the lake is the second time the thing bites her that year, not the first ([buckbeak-rescue](buckbeak-rescue.md)).

It is also the scene where her year stops being a private matter. In autumn she had the luxury of no witnesses. Here she has none.

## Canon

Lupin's Defence exam is an outdoor obstacle course — grindylow, red caps, hinkypunk — with a Boggart in a trunk at the end, taken alone, where nobody sees what anyone gets. Hermione fails hers. Both kept.

## Beats

1. **The course.** She goes through it admirably. She is a gifted witch and the whole queue can see it (DIV-011).
2. **The trunk.** She goes in. She does not come out.
3. **The wait.** Nothing happens for far too long, in front of everybody.
4. **Lupin opens it** and takes her out himself.
5. **He hands her to Pansy** and says nothing about any of it.
6. **Daphne's line.** Deniable, true, and aimed: *she couldn't even handle a Boggart.* She doesn't claim anything beyond the fact, and lets the queue draw the line themselves (DIV-094).
7. **She doesn't explode.** Visibly shaken, and that is all — which is the measurable difference from the Quidditch final, where she turns on her own Beater (DIV-071). She can't, because Daphne is right.
8. **The trio.** Ron relishes it openly. Hermione files it, and spends it on Harry rather than on her. Harry doesn't know what to make of any of it.

## Don't go inside the trunk *(settled)*

We have already seen her Boggart. Showing it twice spends it. Stay outside with the queue, watch the time run, watch Lupin decide to open the lid. **The reader supplies the content, which is worse than seeing it.**

That is also why the POV is Harry's rather than Daphne's: he is the one person present who has been told some of this and can't assemble it.

## Hermione spends it in the scene *(settled)*

She is too decent to goad Dracaena with it, and she will not forget it either. Saving it for later doesn't work — by the time it could be used it reads as a pointless callback — so **it gets spent here, as a dig at Harry.**

She knows there is *something* between those two, and she would hate for it to become anything real: partly because Dracaena is vile to her on a regular basis, and partly for Harry's own sake. It is the B3 ancestor of *"Oh Harry, why did you have to choose her?"* ([the-dragons-hoard](../../sketches/b4/the-dragons-hoard.md)). She has no idea she is already the rival in Dracaena's head (DIV-041).

**Ron is the uncomplicated version** — he simply enjoys it, which is what makes Hermione's version land as something more pointed.

## What Harry gets out of it *(settled)*

Nothing he can use, which is the point (Q-035). He does not comfort her and he is not softened; he is confused, standing between a friend gloating and a friend digging.

But he has been told, in the spring, that she couldn't beat her Boggart ([lap-pillow](lap-pillow.md)), and now he watches it happen in front of the whole year. **One thing she told him that term turns out to be true.** It is filed and not understood, and it is there to be reached for at the lake.

## Pansy and Daphne *(settled)*

The two of them get sorted by the same minute, which is what the scene is for in the house-unity thread ([house-unity](../../threads/house-unity.md)).

- **Daphne** gets the first concrete ammunition she has ever had against the incumbent, and it is deniable, which is her whole method — she schemes, she doesn't lead a mob (DIV-093, DIV-094). She also has no idea what she is actually pointing at, which makes it an unearned hit that happens to land exactly right.
- **Pansy** is handed a shaking friend by a teacher who explains nothing, **and she doesn't ask.** That is the difference between her and Daphne in one gesture, and it settles the question of whether an arranged friendship is a real one by behaviour instead of assertion (DIV-093).

## Must keep

- She is excellent on the course and fails only the trunk.
- We never see inside it.
- Lupin takes her out and hands her to Pansy, saying nothing.
- Daphne's line is true, deniable, and aimed.
- She does not explode. She is shaken, in public.
- Pansy doesn't ask.
- Ron gloats; Hermione digs at Harry, not at her; Harry understands nothing.
