# The Robe Shop

## Purpose

Lindsay's second appearance, and the shortest. A door opening and closing.

Three small jobs: Harry proves again he can't be charming on purpose; the fake triangle gets an outside witness a year before Dracaena makes the same mistake (DIV-118); and the reflex that costs him Quidditch for life in B5 gets its first and smallest showing (DIV-067).

**Her job here differs from B1 and B4.** Witness, then gloater — here she's the first person in the series to work out what's going on, and she gets there by accident, which is funnier.

## POV

**Hers**, and that's the technique. She shows the reader her work and tells nobody in the room anything. From inside Harry's head the scene would spend its length dodging his feelings; from outside we just watch a boy complain for a minute, bristle when someone agrees, and watch her read it.

## Beats

1. **The crow clerk.** Ron and Hermione looked for Harry here a few hours earlier and missed him; they come in still talking about the uncanny clerk in the crow feathers. Harry: *oh, that's Lindsay.* **He has her name, from one hour, two years ago.** She hears all of it from behind the counter.
2. *Well, if it ain't the Harry Potter. Regret not giving me that compliment all those years back?*
3. **He isn't smooth and must never be.** He says, plainly, that she frightened him half to death. True, and not a compliment. **She decides to be flattered anyway** and makes a show of fainting.
4. **The teasing, and the princess is girlfriend number one.** She warns him off complimenting other girls in front of his young lady — meaning herself, meaning nothing — and makes a show of how fast he gets through them: there was the little blonde princess back when, and now there's this one on his arm. **Two already, at thirteen.** She is working the only thing she has: she watched an eleven-year-old get flattened by the eyes line.
5. Hermione asks, perfectly sincerely, why everyone keeps saying that. She does not let go of his arm.
6. **Ron takes hold of the *other* girlfriend** — the blonde one — and wants to know who that is.
7. **She asks after the princess, and Harry vents.** Unbearable. Better than him at that. Did this in March. Has his scarf and wears it where he'll see it. An inventory of exceeding precision from a boy certain he's complaining. Ron and Hermione exchange a look.
8. **The tell.** She says the girl sounds like a right piece of work, and he corrects her instantly. *She's not. She's just—* and stops. He knows he said it. He has no idea he's done two opposite things in a row.
9. **She knows she's hit.** She fired blind on the one thing she had, and a fitting is twenty minutes of reading a face at close range — she has done thousands. She says nothing.
10. Robes, and out.

## Must keep

The crow clerk opening, the sincere non-compliment and the fake faint, **the two-girlfriends gag that puts the princess on the page before Ron grabs her**, "why does everyone keep saying that", the inventory, and the correction.
