# The Quidditch Final

## Purpose

The first full Seeker's dilemma under our rules (DIV-066), and the day Dracaena learns that playing straight gets her the one thing she wants and can't take by force. It supplies the second half of her Buckbeak motive (DIV-003): the guilt makes her want to act, this makes her believe acting straight will work. It is also the loudest of the supporting scenes for Harry's turn at the lake (Q-035).

## The shape of the match

Gryffindor need a margin, not just a win, so Harry can only catch the Snitch once **(Gryffindor + 30) − Slytherin ≥ the Cup margin**. Every Slytherin goal pushes that line back by one Gryffindor goal. The exact margin is set when the scene is written.

1. **Slytherin foul their way through it** (canon), and she is the only one who doesn't, and says so to her own team, out loud, mid-match.
2. **Phase one: Slytherin ahead by more than 30.** Harry can't take the Snitch without handing Slytherin the Cup, so his entire job is to stop her ending the match. This is the hard half for him, while she still has everything.
3. **The lead flips,** and the job reverses.
4. **Phase two: her one move.** She can still save the Cup by catching the Snitch herself and capping Gryffindor's margin — losing the match on purpose. Harry has to keep himself between her and it. From the stands it looks like neither of them doing anything. She knows precisely what it is and can't break it, and her fury is at being contained.
5. **The fake-out.** The margin comes within reach and Harry breaks for the Snitch. She goes with him. Her own Chasers score, the line moves back a goal, and he has to break off — saved by her team, who have no idea. He turns and gives her a smug look: *I could end this whenever I want.* She is incandescent.
6. **The real duel** takes seconds. She sticks far closer this time, having seen what he has in reserve, and it still isn't enough. He takes it, and Gryffindor take the Cup.

## Why she loses

- **The cost of her style.** She flies the way she duels: precise, corrected, controlled, and expensive. The longer the match runs the less she has left. Harry flies on instinct and wastes nothing.
- **The broom suits him** and would not suit her (DIV-068). Hers is a Nimbus 2001, flown at its ceiling with perfect technique. His is wild, he fights it for most of the match, and the final duel is where he stops fighting it.
- **A better broom wouldn't have helped her,** and she knows it: a Firebolt is the wrong instrument for the way she flies (DIV-068). There is nothing to point at afterwards. Her father doesn't offer her one either — he celebrates achievement, he doesn't console failure ([lucius-malfoy](../../characters/lucius-malfoy.md)).

## The hand

1. **Her team desert her.** She is left sitting alone in the middle of the pitch, staring at her hands, crying: she tried everything she had, played it straight, and lost anyway.
2. **He looks back.** Harry is being hauled off by his own side. He sees her there, alone, and it bothers him, because the flying was the best he's had.
3. **He goes back,** crouches, and holds out his hand. Something close to: *I had a lot of fun playing with you. Can we do it again?*
4. **It mirrors the train.** She offered her hand and he didn't take it, and that refusal is the root of the whole grudge (DIV-009). This is the same gesture, reversed, from the person who refused it.
5. **She takes it.** She wants to read it as mockery and can't — the smile is too plain for that.
6. **The race.** He's back on his broom challenging her to one, something daft as the forfeit, and she chases him. They fly off together.
7. **Detention** for both, later.

## Must keep

- He is not being noble. He is happy, and the generosity is a by-product. Conscious kindness would read as pity and she'd throw it back at him.
- She loses to *him*, not to his equipment.
- The mask goes back on the next morning, colder than ever (DIV-017's reflex), and Harry is confused and a little hurt by it. Nothing between them visibly changes for the rest of the year.
- The final gives Harry **affection**; the lake gives him **revelation** (she stole Buckbeak, the lap pillow was sincere, the Patronus study was real). None of that leaks here ([buckbeak-rescue](buckbeak-rescue.md)).
