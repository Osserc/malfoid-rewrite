# The Boggart Lesson

## Purpose

Canon's Boggart lesson, reshaped for a class of the whole year (DIV-034). Lupin runs it House by House, which keeps each House's fears among its own, and keeps Dracaena's Boggart from Harry.

## How it runs

- **Four sessions** of about fifteen minutes each, back to back, filling an hour. Each House takes its turn at the Boggart while the others wait outside. A session is about ten pupils, close to canon's handful.
- **Why:** a Boggart shows you your deepest fear. Lupin won't hand detractors in the other Houses ammunition. Within a House, fears stay among people who share a common room. He knows what a Boggart can give away: his own is the moon.
- **The theme:** a teacher keeping the Houses apart cuts against the unity theme, on purpose. In B3 the Houses can't yet be trusted with each other's fears. A later moment where they can would show how far the clumps have dissolved (DIV-034).

## Beats

1. **Gryffindor** is canon's lesson, from Harry's POV: Neville's Snape, and Lupin stepping in before Harry's turn, his own Boggart becoming the moon.
2. **Hufflepuff:** Ron faces his spider, out of Harry's sight.
3. **Ravenclaw:** Hermione misses the moon, one of her canon clues to Lupin's secret. How she gets it: Q-054.
4. **Slytherin:** Dracaena sits it out in her sling (DIV-017). She faces hers later, alone: [remedial-boggart](remedial-boggart.md).
