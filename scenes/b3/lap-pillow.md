# The Lap Pillow

## Purpose

The memory behind Harry's Patronus at the lake (DIV-018), and one of the things that add up for him there ([buckbeak-rescue](buckbeak-rescue.md)). It's also where her own Patronus arc starts (DIV-027): she studies the charm for him before she ever wants it for herself.

## Beats

Settled unless marked. The order is tentative, and the scene itself is the author's, not yet outlined (Q-026).

1. Harry takes secret Patronus lessons (canon). Dracaena spies on them.
2. She looks up the charm in the library. She wants him to succeed.
3. The lap pillow. For a few precious seconds he catches her giving him a heartfelt, sweet smile. *(open)* The rest of the moment comes from the author's sketch.
4. Her official reason, along the lines of "If you faint during our match, how can I prove I'm better than you?" (Wording to polish.) It's the tsun side talking.

## Must keep

- It becomes the memory that wins the day at the lake.
