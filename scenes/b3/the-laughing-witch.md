# The Laughing Witch

## Purpose

Pays off the Dementor garrison that has hung over the whole book, turns Harry's Patronus at the lake into an event with consequences, and gives Dracaena the cover she needs to reach Sirius. It also opens the Azkaban argument ([azkaban](../../problems/azkaban.md)) and puts the Thynne–Laurel pair on the page.

## The rule that protects the climax

**Harry and Dracaena never get a POV on this.** The spectacle belongs to side characters — Balthier and Daphne at a tower window — so it cannot displace the book's real ending, which is intimate: the Patronus, the confession, the flight. The fight itself is short. Minerva is having a field day; it is not a battle.

## Beats

1. The Dementors, maimed and starving after the lake, mass on the castle. *"They are wounded, Filius, and they seek to replenish their strength"* — six words that recast what a thirteen-year-old did at the lake.
2. The staff hold the walls with their Patronuses. The castle is awake; the corridors are full.
3. McGonagall transfigures her battle garb onto herself as she walks, in front of the students. Nobody has ever seen it.
4. Flitwick covers her. She goes up, casts Lustrum with the vial, and the swarm burns. The fire does not spread: the grass and the trees are untouched.
5. The orb wanes; she gathers the embers into a halo of smouldering orbs and comes down, throwing them at the fleeing Dementors on the ground.
6. She laughs. Not cruelly — joyously, with a tinge of mania. The whole castle hears it. DIV-079
7. She lands with fresh burns and a sleeve gone, showing the marked flesh. Fudge arrives with Macnair and shouts about an unauthorised incantation and destroyed Ministry property. She taunts Macnair, takes Fudge by the collar, and walks off for medical attention.
8. **The aftermath is the cover.** An empty sky, survivors that will not come near the castle, and grounds covered in cinders nobody can touch. Dracaena flies Buckbeak out over it — her first sight of what a war actually leaves on the ground (DIV-018, [buckbeak-rescue](buckbeak-rescue.md)).
9. Balthier and Nola in the common room afterwards: *"I never knew fire could be so beautiful."* He decides to stop hiding her from his mother. DIV-082
10. The morning after: the grounds cordoned off, the theories, McGonagall's flat denial, and the legend.

## Seeding, earlier in the book

- **The Quidditch pitch.** She grips the railing while the Dementors swarm the match and stands down only because Harry is in the middle of it, leaving scorch marks in a downpour. Lee Jordan sees and says nothing.
- **The vial.** She commissions the enhancer from Flitwick after the pitch and wears it from then on. **Hermione notices the trinket, cannot place it, looks it up and finds nothing** — a frustration, not a clue, which is what keeps "nobody knows what Lustrum is" true. Her eye for precise enchantment is already established ([society](../../setting/society.md)).
- **Balthier.** Takes Harry aside earlier in the year to tell him he has nothing to be ashamed of for fainting. He is mostly talking to himself, and he is quietly wrong about Harry having it easier than he does.

## Must keep

- Harry and Dracaena have no POV here.
- The fight is short.
- The fire spares the grass.
- The laughter, and the flat denial afterwards.
- Fudge loses the prisoner, the hippogriff and the garrison in one night and answers with a hearing.
