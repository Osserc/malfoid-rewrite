# Buckbeak's Rescue

## Purpose

Replaces the canon Time-Turner climax (DIV-002). It's her first real act against her father, and the night Harry's opinion of her turns.

## Motive

1. 🔒 Dracaena regrets getting Buckbeak condemned.
2. She realises her weeks of play-acting with the injury gave Lucius the fuel for the execution (DIV-017).
3. *(settled)* Lucius wants the beast dead because no one crosses House Malfoy. She finds punishing a creature for her own mistake petty and beneath a Malfoy.
4. *(settled)* Harry always stands by what he believes, and the thought of him beating her at that leaves her very upset.

## Beats

The order and logistics are provisional: the night has to be rebuilt without Time-Turners (Q-003).

1. She plants a fake lead with the Ministry so the Dementors swarm the lake and leave her room to act (Q-025).
2. 🔒 She saves Buckbeak from the execution herself.
3. She can't control him and ends up at the lake. She knows the Patronus Charm, having studied it for Harry, and tries it, but she can't cast it (DIV-027).
4. Under the Dementors' influence she breaks down sobbing and confesses, unprompted, that the swarm is her fault.
5. It all adds up, and Harry re-evaluates her (*Why Harry turns*, below). The lap-pillow memory wins the day ([lap-pillow](lap-pillow.md)): his Patronus drives off the swarm and saves everyone. As he casts there's a first flicker of resonance between them (DIV-021); how it works is open (Q-028). She watches him do what she couldn't, and resolves to master the charm.
6. Hermione has the idea: Dracaena should fly Buckbeak to Sirius.
7. She agrees: she owes Harry, and knows it. And her prince just drove off a horde of demons, for her.
8. 🔒 She brings Buckbeak to Sirius, who escapes on him.
9. Sirius recognises Narcissa's daughter, and they talk briefly: "maybe the Black family did make something good after all", if Narcissa gave birth to a girl this brave. He doesn't see her again before the Yule Ball (DIV-019).

## Why Harry turns

*(settled)* Seeing her break down isn't what does it. At the lake four things add up at once:

1. She stole Buckbeak to save him. Harry has only just found out.
2. The lap pillow wasn't a joke. The heartfelt, sweet smile he caught for a few seconds was real, and the memory he'd written off as a prank becomes one he can cast with ([lap-pillow](lap-pillow.md)).
3. Her Patronus study wasn't a joke either. She really was trying to help him.
4. Right now she's trying to drive the Dementors off herself.

His conclusion: there really is something more to this girl. That's the turn in his opinion of her ([harry-dracaena](../../threads/harry-dracaena.md)). It needs more supporting scenes in B1–B3 (Q-035).

## Must keep

The locked beats, her motive, the confession, Hermione's idea, Sirius's recognition and his line. Why Harry turns: the four things adding up.
