# The Slap

## Purpose

Canon's version is a straight laugh: Draco is vile about Hagrid and Hermione hits him. Ours keeps the laugh for everyone except Harry, who is standing close enough to see her face. The first faint alarm bell before the final (DIV-069), and the one scene where her guilt over Buckbeak is visible before she acts on it (DIV-003, DIV-017).

## Beats

1. **The appeal is lost** and Hagrid is in pieces over it.
2. **The jeer.** She says it anyway, cruelly, because her house is watching and she has appearances to keep. Her voice isn't right and she's close to tears while she does it.
3. **The bait.** She aims it at Hermione, not at Hagrid, and keeps going until she gets what she's after.
4. **The slap.** She doesn't flinch, doesn't draw her wand, doesn't tell her father. She takes it like something owed, and walks off.
5. **What Harry saw.** He was close enough to catch her face and isn't sure what he caught. He says nothing. Ron and Hermione are busy enjoying themselves.

## Must keep

- **Hermione is not in the wrong.** She sees the jeer and nothing else, and the slap is earned from where she's standing. Keep her win intact; the ambiguity belongs to Harry alone.
- She is genuinely cruel here, not secretly kind. The jeer lands and Hagrid is hurt by it.
- The alarm bell stays faint and deniable. The loud version is the final.

## Open

How public it is, and whether anyone in her own house notices she didn't retaliate (Q-081).
