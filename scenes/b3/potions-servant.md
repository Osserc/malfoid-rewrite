# The Potions Servant

## Purpose

A first look at her soft side, in front of witnesses, while she's still at her pettiest.

## Beats

1. Pointing to her "injured" arm, she badgers Harry into preparing her ingredients (canon default).
2. She turns him into her servant.
3. The potion isn't coming out right, because he's not much of a potioneer, so she steps in.
4. She's strangely sweet to him under the barbs. In her head they're a married couple, cooking together.
5. She realises she's been daydreaming in front of the whole class and scrambles to save face.
6. Harry is weirded out.

## Must keep

All six beats.

## Open
