# Sirius's Break-ins

## Purpose

Keep canon's misdirection (Black is after Harry) now that Ron and Harry sleep in different Houses, and use the split: Harry's friends are in danger because of him, and he isn't there to protect them.

## Beats

1. **Halloween, Gryffindor.** Black slashes the Fat Lady (canon). He assumes Ron is a Gryffindor, like every Weasley before him. He has been in Azkaban for twelve years, and the Prophet photo of the family in Egypt doesn't give Ron's House. Everyone believes he's after Harry. It's a quiet rhyme with Ron's wound: the convict makes the same assumption everyone does.
2. **The feud.** Pets roam the castle (canon: Crookshanks meets Black on the grounds, and Mrs Norris goes everywhere). Crookshanks hounds Scabbers in the shared spaces, where Scabbers rides in Ron's pocket.
3. **Into Hufflepuff.** Hufflepuffs let the big, cuddly cat in. The House that welcomes everyone gets got through its welcome. Scabbers's fake death happens in the Hufflepuff dormitory, and a Ravenclaw cat in the Hufflepuff dorms turns the Ron–Hermione feud into a House incident.
4. **Crookshanks sets Black straight.** He has been stalking Scabbers and knows where he sleeps.
5. **Hufflepuff.** Black gets in through the same hospitality, in place of Neville's lost list of passwords (who and how: Q-045), and stands over Ron's bed with a knife.
6. **The school's reading.** With Gryffindor Tower now guarded, Black has gone after Harry's best friend to get at Harry. Harry wasn't there.
7. **The climax** seems to confirm it: Black drags Ron into the Shack and Harry follows, just as bait would make him. The reveal recasts both break-ins: the first was a mistake, the second the real target. The night itself is being rebuilt (Q-003).

## Must keep

The misdirection on Harry holds until the Shack.

## Open

Q-045 (who lets Black into Hufflepuff, and how). Q-003 (the rebuilt climax).
