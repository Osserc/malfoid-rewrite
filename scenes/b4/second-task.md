# The Second Task

## Purpose

Shows, in front of the whole school, who Harry would miss most. It comes two months after the Yule Ball (DIV-004). Motif echo: the enchanted sleep, and Harry crossing danger to bring her back. For her, it's when she slips fully into the dream.

## Beats

1. 🔒 Dracaena is taken as Harry's hostage, in Ron's place.
2. *(settled)* He goes down on a gillyweed extract she brewed for him, not the raw weed (DIV-039).
3. Harry rescues her (canon default). The sight of her in the enchanted sleep stops him in his tracks. *(settled)* Fleur never reaches her sister, so he brings Gabrielle up too (canon default).
4. *(settled)* She's elated at being his treasure. She keeps her edge, but from here she's dreaming (DIV-013).
5. *(settled)* At the surface she clings to him and berates him for being stupid. Hagrid has to pry her off (DIV-038).
6. *(settled)* When Fleur comes to thank Harry with a hug, Dracaena shoves her away, calls her a slag, claims him as hers and kisses him in front of the whole school. It isn't their first kiss: that was at the Ball (DIV-038).

## Must keep

She is his hostage, not Ron, and she's elated. She claims him in public, and Fleur's thanks never land.
