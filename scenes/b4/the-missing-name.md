# The Missing Name

## Purpose

🔒-adjacent: this is where Harry keeps Lucius's name back and she watches him do it (DIV-047). It's also where she defends him to the Minister's face, which is what reaches her father and triggers the order to cut ties (Q-022).

## Beats

1. *(settled)* Fudge arrives while Harry is sleeping off the draught; the row wakes him. Dracaena is holding his hand and digging her nails in.
2. *(settled)* Fudge dismisses the account: a lunatic's ramblings and a boy who relishes attention.
3. *(settled)* **She speaks.** Cold, open hostility to the Minister for Magic. She defends Parseltongue as a gift of the old lineages (DIV-008), and when he implies Harry is damaged she says flatly that he is not.
4. *(settled)* Fudge patronises her: a girl with an understandable affection for the boy.
5. *(settled)* **The names.** Harry's rage breaks through and he starts listing Death Eaters. Macnair — her head snaps round. Avery, Nott, and then he starts on "L—", catches her face, coughs, and finishes with Crabbe and Goyle. Those are her friends' fathers, and he names them anyway.
6. *(settled)* Fudge dismisses every name as a cleared man. Harry's silence costs him nothing, which is the point.
7. *(settled)* From there the scene runs as canon: Dumbledore sends out the Order. She gives Harry the last dose herself, and the whole room stays the night.

## Must keep

- Harry stops himself on the "L", and she sees it happen.
- Fudge dismisses the names anyway.
- Dumbledore notices the gap and welcomes it (DIV-046).
