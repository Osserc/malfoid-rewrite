# The Malfoy Room

## Purpose

The hoard made literal, and the lap pillow paid off in a register nobody can mistake for a prank. She sets out to solve his nightmares with a potion and finds out she's the answer ([lap-pillow](../b3/lap-pillow.md), DIV-061).

## Beats

1. *(settled)* Two nights of screaming in the dormitory. On the third morning a folded paper snake carries a time and a place in her handwriting. He tells neither Ron nor Hermione, who have been keeping them apart since the hospital wing.
2. *(settled)* He arrives an hour early at a dead end in the dungeons, works himself into a state about traps, and her voice comes from under the floor.
3. *(settled)* **The stair.** The stone opens into a spiral stair, clean and maintained, lit green. On the way down he thinks of the graveyard: the green is the colour of the curse that killed his parents, and Cedric four days ago. The reader gets this before she does.
4. *(settled)* **The room.** Circular, small, luxurious, green-lit: canopy bed, large mirror over the fireplace, round sofa, bathtub. She's at a cauldron, failing, which he has never seen before.
5. *(settled)* **The sloughsilk** (DIV-064). She's in white pyjamas with a snake hood and snake slippers, out of her armour and smug about it. Black family tradition — he should ask Sirius.
6. *(settled)* **The light.** While he's staring at her, the green goes out and a soft purple comes up at a flick of her wand. He sees it in the mirror and doesn't register what it was. She read it off his face, said nothing and asked nothing. Not asking is the kindness.
7. *(settled)* **The daydream.** In the violet light he goes somewhere else: a sofa, a window, a blanket, hot chocolate, her in his lap. Domestic, not romantic, and he has no name for it. He doesn't work out what it was. The only one in B4 → the Mirror in B6 (DIV-043).
8. *(settled)* **The hoard** (DIV-059). He teases her that the room could use some red or gold, and finds a Gryffindor banner — which, under the green, he'd have taken for a Slytherin standard. Her changing the light for him is what lets him see it. Under it, a stand: a quill, a Chocolate Frog card, a worn Seeker's glove, his old scarf, won off him in their second year. He owns up to a smaller trove of her things.
9. *(settled)* **The room's story.** Brutus Malfoy, three centuries ago; the password; not on the Map; her mother told her last year; her father chose the furniture (DIV-060). She refuses him the password: this is an emergency, not an invitation.
10. *(settled)* **The lap pillow.** She admits she spent two days hunting a solution and found nothing. So, the thing she has: her lap, as in B3. The sleep mask goes on because she's staying up to read. He sleeps through the night.

## Must keep

- She fails at a potion, in front of him, for the first time.
- She changes the light without a word, and he never notices.
- The daydream is short, and he doesn't understand it.
- The hoard is found by accident, and the accident is her kindness.
- No password. The cure is her, not access.
