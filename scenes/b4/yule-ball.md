# The Yule Ball

## Purpose

🔒 The turning point of Harry and Dracaena's relationship. For her, it's the first step outside the script. Motif echo: the dance at the end of the film.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* Harry asks her (DIV-013). Sirius planted the idea, going only on their one encounter in B3 (DIV-019). She's the only one he asks: no Cho first (DIV-041).
   - **What Sirius actually says** *(settled)* · DIV-101: with Slytherin women you either hit the jackpot or an early grave — and either way it is worth it. **He is talking about his own family**, his mother and his cousins, and telling Harry to go for it anyway. Moody reaches the same verdict after the lake in the opposite register, and Harry takes this one.
2. *(settled)* She accepts. If *Potter* can step outside the script, so can she. She half-believes the boy who beat Voldemort twice, killed a Basilisk and drove off a horde of Dementors might really be her prince.
3. 🔒 They go to the ball together.
4. 🔒 Something here turns their relationship. What, exactly, is open.
5. *(settled)* **Hermione comes in on Krum's arm** (canon, kept, and now load-bearing). The girl Dracaena has spent four months believing Harry is in love with arrives on another man's arm, in public, on the one night Dracaena is on his — **the rival taken off the board without a word being said about it** (DIV-041). She is not gracious about it and does not have to be; the relief is enormous and entirely private. *(open)* Whether she lets any of it show.
6. *(settled)* **The socks** (canon, kept). Moody admires Harry's socks; Harry says Dobby gave them to him. Funny on the night, and the only reason the beat days later can exist: Moody asks after Dobby in passing, Harry mentions he is minding an elf called Winky in the castle, and **Moody startles** (Q-114, DIV-086).
   - *(open)* **Dracaena's reaction.** Incensed that he wore peasant socks and that she never noticed; or genuinely impressed, since our Dobby is no caricature; or nothing said at all and a small smile to herself, because her old servant is alive and happy (Q-098, Q-114).
7. *(settled)* Their first kiss.

## The second dance *(draft)*

The deal is one dance. During it, for an instant, Harry catches a warm smile on her face, and he wants to make sure of what he saw. So he asks for another, firmly, even when she tries to dodge. She obliges, but raises the bar: she ups the tempo and all but drags him across the Great Hall. He keeps up through sheer improvisation and Quidditch reflexes. She smiles happily the whole time, until she catches herself at the end of the ball and excuses herself. Harry follows her. What happens then is for another session.

It's her challenge, and he meets it. That's what she comes to love most about him: he never runs away, he always stands up, and he always meets her challenge. She raises the bar on him again in B7 ([apparate-dancing](../b7/apparate-dancing.md)).

## The balcony *(settled)* · DIV-095

What happens when he follows her out. **The leading candidate for beat 4** — the locked turn — though the lock only says the turn is here, not what it is.

- **He gets close and says he wishes it could stay like this.** Not a line he has prepared; he is simply happier than he has ever been and says so badly.
- **She asks: *Am I allowed to dream, Harry? Someone like me?*** It is not modesty and it is not fishing. She means it as a question of fact, and she is the only person in the castle who would ask it of herself in those terms.
- **It is the first time she uses his given name**, and neither of them remarks on it. Everything before this is *Potter*.
- **What she is asking about is September** — the corridor where she drew on Hermione Granger in front of him, and the hospital bed where he told her what she did was hideous (DIV-095, [the-ferret](the-ferret.md)). **She has carried that for three months without answering it**, because her ledger has no way to pay it off ([dracaena-malfoy](../../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)). The question is whether a person who does that gets to have this — and it is his own verdict coming back out of her mouth.
- **Nothing is promised here either.** The promise about his friends comes after, once she has given herself permission; putting it in this conversation would turn her question into an undertaking (Q-135).
- **It is the other half of the year's symmetry.** He gives her permission to want him; she gives him permission to want to win (DIV-089, Q-128). Each does it once, neither notices, and this is her half.
- *(open)* What he answers, and how it runs into the kiss.

## Must keep

He asks, she accepts, they go together, and it's the turning point.
