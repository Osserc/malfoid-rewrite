# The Yule Ball

## Purpose

🔒 The turning point of Harry and Dracaena's relationship. For her, it's the first step outside the script. Motif echo: the dance at the end of the film.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* Harry asks her (DIV-013). Sirius planted the idea, going only on their one encounter in B3 (DIV-019). She's the only one he asks: no Cho first (DIV-041).
2. *(settled)* She accepts. If *Potter* can step outside the script, so can she. She half-believes the boy who beat Voldemort twice, killed a Basilisk and drove off a horde of Dementors might really be her prince.
3. 🔒 They go to the ball together.
4. 🔒 Something here turns their relationship. What, exactly, is open.
5. *(settled)* Their first kiss.

## The second dance *(draft)*

The deal is one dance. During it, for an instant, Harry catches a warm smile on her face, and he wants to make sure of what he saw. So he asks for another, firmly, even when she tries to dodge. She obliges, but raises the bar: she ups the tempo and all but drags him across the Great Hall. He keeps up through sheer improvisation and Quidditch reflexes. She smiles happily the whole time, until she catches herself at the end of the ball and excuses herself. Harry follows her. What happens then is for another session.

It's her challenge, and he meets it. That's what she comes to love most about him: he never runs away, he always stands up, and he always meets her challenge. She raises the bar on him again in B7 ([apparate-dancing](../b7/apparate-dancing.md)).

## Must keep

He asks, she accepts, they go together, and it's the turning point.
