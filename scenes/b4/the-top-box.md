# The Top Box

## Purpose

Harry meets Narcissa Malfoy and charms her flat without the faintest idea he has done anything. It's where her alliance with him begins — not politics, not strategy: she met the boy once and liked him. It's also the first showing of the leak (DIV-117), and the only place in four books where a Malfoy is seen being warm in public.

Comedy throughout. Nothing in it is played for weight.

## Beats

1. **From across the stands.** Ron or Hermione points out the Malfoys: that must be Narcissa, Malfoy's mother. Harry: *no wonder Dracaena is that pretty, then.* His friends round on him. He has no idea what they mean — he's certain he only thought it (DIV-117).
2. **The introduction.** Lucius presents his wife to the box. Niceties.
3. Harry notices she hasn't stopped looking at him. *You must be Harry Potter, then. My daughter has been regaling me with tales of your accomplishments.* True, and funny, and it is four years of complaining handed back as a compliment — Lucius has been listening to all of it ([lucius-malfoy](../../characters/lucius-malfoy.md)).
4. **The queen.** He blurts it out, exactly as he blurted out the other one three years earlier (DIV-009). Queen, not empress: it pairs with princess.
5. **She goes.** Helpless giggling, leaning on her husband, in front of the Minister and half the box.
6. **The hand.** Still laughing: *well, now I want a proper greeting.* He doesn't clock that it wasn't a request — he can feel he's meant to, so he does it — and that finishes her.
7. **Dracaena sulks.** She is supposed to be the princess, and she has never once got anything like it.
8. **Lucius, exasperated,** and bearing it. She teases him in public and has for years; he comes out of the scene well.
9. Same hour as the veela (DIV-037). The boy nothing can touch is undone by nothing at all.

## Must keep

The leak from the stands, the *regaling me with tales* line, "queen", the hand and his failure to read it as anything but an instruction, and Dracaena's sulk.
