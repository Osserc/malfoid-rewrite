# The Hospital Wing

## Purpose

The first full statement of the hoard: she guards him, in public, in front of people who despise her for it. It also puts her at his bedside for the Fudge row ([the-missing-name](the-missing-name.md)).

## POV

*(draft)* Sirius, for the whole scene. Harry is barely conscious, it keeps the head-hopping out, and it gives us her from outside, which [style](../../core/style.md) asks for.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* Sirius walks Harry down from Dumbledore's office in dog form, holding him up. Harry is spent and his leg is failing.
2. *(settled)* The room swarms him. Dracaena is the only one who doesn't move: she stands apart, white-knuckled, not daring to approach.
3. *(settled)* Dumbledore clears the air — Harry has told it once tonight and won't be asked again — and gets him into bed.
4. *(settled)* Sirius crosses the room and bodily shoves her to the bedside. Every eye in the room goes to her.
5. *(settled)* **The ribbon.** The first thing Harry says to her is an apology for losing it. She breaks: climbs onto the bed, shouts at him, demands to know who did this to him. Molly has to prise her off.
6. *(settled)* Pomfrey threatens to throw her out. Sirius growls her down, and Dumbledore overrules her, quietly: Miss Malfoy may need to be here more than Harry does (DIV-046).
7. *(settled)* **"Sleep well, my prince."** She steadies the goblet when his hand fails, and says it in front of the whole room as he goes under. Harry is too far gone to react. Nobody understands what it means (DIV-010).
8. *(settled)* **The coda, from the others.** Ron apes the line. Molly can't decide whether it's sweet or sinister. Bill saw Dracaena on Harry's arm during the tour of the castle that morning and finds it charming. Hermione, who saw her tie the ribbon on before the Task and heard "a charm for my brave knight", can't believe the change of heart and has to admit that if it's an act she's a gifted actress.
9. *(settled)* She guards him all night. No one is allowed to move a chair closer, the dog excepted. She only loses when exhaustion takes her and she falls asleep on his chest, which is how the others finally get near him.

## Must keep

- She hangs back until Sirius makes her move. She never claims him by walking up to him.
- The apology for the ribbon comes before anything else.
- Dumbledore shelters her, and says why, quietly.
- "My prince", in front of everyone, misunderstood by everyone.
- The others' coda. It keeps her hostility from reading as an act, and it stops the scene being all sugar.
