# The Durmstrang Challenger

## Purpose

Shuts down any rival-suitor angle, hard, before the Yule Ball. Shows Harry is a real champion and not a fluke who cheated his way in, and that he can go too far. Feeds Rita Skeeter.

## Beats

1. The visiting students from Durmstrang and Beauxbatons join the Duelling Club (DIV-028).
2. A Durmstrang student takes an interest in Dracaena. *(draft)* Not Krum, so Krum and Hermione stay as in canon.
3. Harry challenges him and beats him, more aggressively than he needs to.
4. Dracaena is delighted that he's jealous, in a way. But the thought of using anyone to provoke him disgusts her: theirs is an exclusive game.
5. *(draft)* She challenges the Durmstrang student herself and humiliates him further.
6. The fight is prime ammunition for Rita Skeeter. A champion who hexes people into submission becomes a violent, tormented boy (Q-038).

## Must keep

- The rival-suitor angle dies here, hard.
- Harry wins, and goes too far.
- Her delight at his jealousy, and her disgust at the idea of using anyone to provoke him.
- Rita gets her story.
