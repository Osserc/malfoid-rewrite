# The Fitting

## Purpose

The payoff on Madam Malkin's, three years later and from the other side. The one adult who watched an eleven-year-old talk herself out of the only sincere compliment she had ever been paid now has to put her in a dress for him. Comedy, and the only scene in the series where someone who knows the whole story gets to say so out loud.

It is also where Narcissa's matchmaking stops being deniable: she doesn't send a dress, she sends someone to put her daughter in it.

## Beats

1. Narcissa arranges an unscheduled Hogsmeade visit through Snape — Dracaena's head of house, and "uncle Severus" since she was five (DIV-031).
2. Lindsay Madison comes up from London with the dress Narcissa chose.
3. **It is not the dress she asked for.** Dracaena says so. Lindsay enjoys this enormously.
4. It is also slightly too small — she has grown since it was made — so it gets fitted on her by magic, and we watch the American witch work.
5. Dracaena wants green. Pressed on why, she says it's to match his stupid eyes.
6. Lindsay, who has forgotten nothing, asks whether she finally gets to be Harry Potter's princess.
7. Dracaena's reaction. She has no idea this woman was in the room, and no way to find out how much she knows.

## Must keep

The wrong dress, the magical fitting, "his stupid eyes", and Lindsay's line. The fitting has to fall *after* Harry asks her and she says yes, or none of it lands.
