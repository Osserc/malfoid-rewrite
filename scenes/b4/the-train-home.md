# The Train Home

## Purpose

Breaks Harry from his friends at the end of the book (DIV-063), hands the hoard its name through Lucius's eyes, and sets up the carriage ride to the Manor.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* **The winnings** (DIV-062). Harry gives Fred and George the thousand Galleons as an investment in the shop, not a gift, so their pride survives it. Away from Ron. The framing was hers.
   - *(settled)* He has no intention of ever collecting; the word was only cover. The twins take it literally, insist on repaying their investor, and won't be argued out of it. They end up settling on free products now and again. The shop stays entirely theirs (Q-074).
2. *(settled)* **The row.** Hermione and Ron confront him over where he's been sleeping. Ron went through his trunk for the Map and found neither Harry nor Malfoy on it. Harry goes cold at the rifling, not at the question.
3. *(settled)* Ron's case: Voldemort is back, a Death Eater was in the castle all year, and Harry is sneaking off at night with Lucius Malfoy's daughter.
4. *(settled)* Dracaena opens the door mid-row to say she's found them a compartment. Harry leaves with her without answering, and settles for a look he hopes reads as an apology. She takes his hand on the way out and makes sure they see it.
5. *(settled)* **Their coda.** Ron is certain she's hexed him. Hermione concedes they may have been too overprotective, and notices the thing that should worry them more: Dracaena didn't acknowledge them at all.
6. *(settled)* **The back compartment.** She locks the door and draws the curtains. She calls the gift stupid — magnanimous, but stupid — and he thanks her for the idea. "It befits my sweet prince." Harry has never been claimed by anyone in his life, and doesn't work out what the knot in his chest is. Then she puts him to sleep again.
7. *(settled)* **Lucius, at King's Cross** (DIV-057). His daughter doesn't come off the train. Parkinson and Greengrass do, then Crabbe and Goyle, then the Weasley boy and Granger without Potter. He runs through the year — the owls, the dress, Rita's pieces, the Second Task — and ends on the graveyard, and her ribbon on Potter's wrist. He has it in his pocket: a furtive *Accio* as he ran, and a Stunner on Avery to open the boy a way out.
8. *(settled)* He boards, breaks her lock, and finds Harry Potter asleep against his daughter, who has him clutched from behind like a dragon guarding her hoard. He backs out, puts a respectable distance between himself and them, and announces himself with his cane.
9. *(settled)* He will pretend he witnessed nothing untoward. She kisses Harry's cheek on the way out, in front of her father, and tells him to think of her.

## Must keep

- Nobody says "hoard" before Lucius thinks it.
- Harry leaves without answering Ron.
- Lucius does not deny what he's looking at. He declines to see it.
- The ribbon stays in his pocket.
