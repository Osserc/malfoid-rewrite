# The Flying Lesson

## Purpose

The only flying in a book with no Quidditch season, and the scene that makes Viktor Krum a person before the maze makes him an obstacle (DIV-090).

What it is really for: it assembles the maze's cast months early, enjoying themselves. Three of the four champions, plus her, plus the other house Seekers, in the air, with children watching. In June three of those people are in a hedge trying to put each other down, and this is what the reader has to remember while it happens.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* **Hooch calls a special session.** She still teaches the first years to fly. With a world-class player in the castle a few months into the year, she arranges an exhibition and invites the schools' Seekers, who would all want to see the young star up close.
2. *(settled)* **It is an exhibition, not a contest.** They show off for the children. Nobody competes and nobody wins.
3. *(settled)* **No duel.** Harry against Krum belongs to the maze and must not be spent early. Both outcomes are bad on their own terms as well: Harry losing damages him in front of Dracaena, and Harry winning makes a fourteen-year-old better than a professional at the professional's own game.
4. *(settled)* **The one result the session produces.** Krum does the thing he is famous for, and we find out who follows him down. Harry does. She does.
5. *(draft)* **Dracaena is in it,** not in the stand. She is a Seeker, the best flier at Hogwarts bar one, and Hooch would invite her. Krum rattles her: he is what she is trying to build herself into by technique and what Harry half is without trying. Harry notices none of that and only wants another hour against her.
6. *(draft)* **The rest of the cast.** Cedric, Cho and the other house Seekers are there. Fleur is not a Seeker, which is a quiet marker of the one champion outside the flying fraternity — and the first out of the maze. Q-126.

## What it picks up for nothing

- **It is a first years' flying lesson with Hooch.** The scene that started Harry, and started the two of them: same teacher, same lawn, three years on. The text never points at it.
- **It is nearly the last time he flies for the pleasure of it.** No Quidditch this year (canon), and B5 takes flying off him for life (DIV-067). The book does not know that.

## Must keep

- No contest between Harry and Krum.
- Children on the lawn. It is what makes the scene gentle, and it is why Krum is kind in it.
