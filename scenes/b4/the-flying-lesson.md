# The Flying Lesson

## Purpose

The only flying in a book with no Quidditch season, and the scene that makes Viktor Krum a person before the maze makes him an obstacle (DIV-090).

Mostly, though, it is a domestic hour for Harry and Dracaena — in public, in daylight, surrounded by children — months before the Task sets him on the rest of this cast. It is what the reader has to remember while the maze happens.

## Beats

1. *(settled)* **Hooch calls a special session.** She still teaches the first years to fly. With a world-class player in the castle a few months into the year, she arranges an exhibition and invites the schools' Seekers, who would all want to see the young star up close.
2. *(settled)* **The stands fill up,** because Krum has a fan club and it is not staying indoors for this.
3. *(settled)* **On the lawn:** Harry, Dracaena, Krum, Cedric and Cho.
4. *(settled)* **Fleur watches from the stands, miffed.** She is not a Seeker and this is a gathering of champions she has been left out of, and she does not trouble to hide what she thinks of that. First clear sighting of the social appetite that produces the pantomime in the Great Hall three months later (DIV-038).
5. *(settled)* **The hour before the show is the point.** Harry and Dracaena go among the first years correcting their technique, and bicker about how it ought to be done — in front of everybody, at length, with total conviction on both sides. The watching school concludes they are a married couple. The children adore it.
6. *(settled)* **She is nice to the small Gryffindors.** Because she is enjoying herself too much to remember not to be, and it does not occur to her until afterwards.
7. *(settled)* **Then the show, and the Feint.** Krum does the thing he is famous for, and they all go down after him.
   - Cedric and Cho pull out earliest.
   - Then Dracaena.
   - Then Harry, shortly after her.
   - Then Krum, last, because it is his.
8. *(settled)* **Harry loses, and it costs nothing.** A dive is not a duel: the star is allowed to take this one home, there is nothing riding on it, and Harry against Krum stays unspent for the maze. He still out-lasts her by a second and a half, and neither of them is going to drop that for a week.

## Why no duel here

Harry against Krum belongs to the Third Task and must not be spent early (DIV-089). Both outcomes are bad on their own terms as well: Harry losing a duel damages him in front of Dracaena, and Harry winning one makes a fourteen-year-old better than a professional at the professional's own game. The dive has neither problem, because nobody is proving anything.

## What it picks up for nothing

- **It is a first years' flying lesson with Hooch.** The scene that started Harry, and started the two of them: same teacher, same lawn, three years on. The text never points at it.
- **It is nearly the last time he flies for the pleasure of it.** No Quidditch this year (canon), and B5 takes flying off him for life (DIV-067). The book does not know that.
- **It assembles the maze's cast, enjoying themselves.** Three of the four champions, plus her, plus Cho, in the air with children watching. In June, three of these people are in a hedge trying to put each other down.

## Must keep

- No contest between Harry and Krum.
- Children on the lawn. It is what makes the hour gentle, and it is why she forgets herself.
- Krum wins the dive. He is the star of this scene and nothing is taken off him in it.
