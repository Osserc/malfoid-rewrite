# The Glue Demonstration

## Purpose

Puts the Disarming Charm's mechanism in front of the reader years before it is needed (DIV-127), by letting a clever idea be **tested** instead of refuted. It is also the first time the reader sees what Flitwick is actually like as a teacher, and it hands Dracaena a joke she will be making for the rest of her life.

## Beats

1. A Duelling Club session, **relatively early in the club's run**. Harry asks the question himself: why not simply stick your wand to your hand, so that nobody can ever Disarm you? **He is Muggle enough to think of glue**, which is exactly why it occurs to him and to nobody else in the room.
2. Flitwick begins to answer, stops, and offers a demonstration instead. He will glue Harry's wand to his hand and let Dracaena try to take it. **"I'll deal with Madam Pomfrey myself."**
3. **Dracaena has already worked out what is going to happen** — or knows outright — and is openly delighted at the prospect. Harry goes squeamish. *"Scared, Potter?"* That is what decides him, not Flitwick's authority. Our lovable idiot goes through with it.
4. She casts Expelliarmus. The charm takes hold of the wand and pulls; the wand cannot leave the hand; **the shoulder gives instead.**
5. Harry to the infirmary. Dracaena laughing — helplessly, hysterically, well past cruelty and into delight.
6. **She goes with him.** At the end of it she pokes the arm, and it is not mean. Something close to tender, in a twisted way, and then: *this was fun, let's do it again sometime.* It weirds Harry out completely, and correctly. **Is this her idea of a playdate?** She is plainly mental and plainly bad news, and the reader should file it as a warning rather than a thaw — which is precisely what makes the Chamber land ([the-chamber](the-chamber.md)).
7. **The aftermath is a joke at his expense.** His friends want to know why Malfoy has been flapping her arms about all week, and the whole school laughs at him. It is also **pure ammunition for Hermione's case** against her.

## Must keep

- **Flitwick does not explain first.** A spoken answer is forgotten by Easter. A classmate carried out of the room is permanent for everyone who was in it.
- **He knows exactly what is going to happen,** and the line about Pomfrey proves it. He is neither surprised nor sorry. He is also perfectly aware that a Ministry-approved instructor would never allow this, and unbothered by that (see [duelling-club](../../threads/duelling-club.md)).
- **The injury is a dislocated shoulder,** because the joint is what gives when the wand will not.
- **She laughs, and then she comes to the infirmary.** Both. Second-year Dracaena is still genuinely unkind, and this is the register — the casual cruelty the thread file names, before the club turns the rivalry into something they both look forward to.
- **The rule is legible from the event.** The reader should come away knowing that the spell grabs the wand and takes what is attached to it, without anybody stating it as a law.

## Open

- **Who is in the room.** *Lean, settled in outline:* **Ron is not** — his wand has been broken since the Whomping Willow and is worse than useless, which is his reason for dropping the club (DIV-126). **Hermione is not** — she has already been beaten badly by Dracaena and has decided duelling is barbaric and not for her, which is cope and which the reader should recognise as cope. That fixes an ordering the thread had left open: **her defeat comes first.** Whether **Pansy** is still attending depends on where this falls inside her two or three sessions (DIV-121) — having her watch is free and may be worth something.
- The arm afterwards, and how it connects to the Chamber and the B3 final: [the-chamber](the-chamber.md), DIV-069.
