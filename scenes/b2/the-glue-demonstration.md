# The Glue Demonstration

## Purpose

Puts the Disarming Charm's mechanism in front of the reader years before it is needed (DIV-127), by letting a clever idea be **tested** instead of refuted. It is also the first time the reader sees what Flitwick is actually like as a teacher, and it hands Dracaena a joke she will be making for the rest of her life.

## Beats

1. A Duelling Club session. Harry asks the obvious clever question: why not simply stick your wand to your hand, so that nobody can ever Disarm you?
2. Flitwick begins to answer, stops, and offers a demonstration instead. He will glue Harry's wand to his hand and let Dracaena try to take it. **"I'll deal with Madam Pomfrey myself."**
3. Harry goes squeamish. Dracaena: *"Scared, Potter?"* That is what decides him — not Flitwick's authority.
4. She casts Expelliarmus. The charm takes hold of the wand and pulls; the wand cannot leave the hand; **the shoulder gives instead.**
5. Harry to the infirmary. Dracaena laughing — helplessly, hysterically, well past cruelty and into delight.

## Must keep

- **Flitwick does not explain first.** A spoken answer is forgotten by Easter. A classmate carried out of the room is permanent for everyone who was in it.
- **He knows exactly what is going to happen,** and the line about Pomfrey proves it. He is neither surprised nor sorry. He is also perfectly aware that a Ministry-approved instructor would never allow this, and unbothered by that (see [duelling-club](../../threads/duelling-club.md)).
- **The injury is a dislocated shoulder,** because the joint is what gives when the wand will not.
- **She laughs.** Second-year Dracaena is still genuinely unkind, and this is the register — the casual cruelty the thread file names, before the club turns the rivalry into something they both look forward to.
- **The rule is legible from the event.** The reader should come away knowing that the spell grabs the wand and takes what is attached to it, without anybody stating it as a law.
