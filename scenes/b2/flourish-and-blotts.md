# Flourish & Blotts

## Purpose

Narcissa Malfoy, two books before the Top Box. The scene plants everything DIV-116 otherwise has to assert — that she teases her husband in public, that he bears it, that she can take a delicate situation apart without raising her voice — and it hands the reader both Malfoy parents' faces before Harry has either.

It also carries the fake triangle's first public outing (DIV-118), the first instance of the corridor pattern (DIV-104), and the diary.

Comedy throughout, with two bruises underneath that nobody in the scene notices.

## Beats

1. **The soot.** Harry comes out of Knockturn Alley filthy. Hermione throws herself at him and starts dusting him down. **Molly Weasley beams at it and files her as his girlfriend**, and holds the position for three years (DIV-118).
2. **The slight.** Dracaena watches it happen. *Adorable* — his **Mudblood girlfriend** dusting him off with even filthier hands. A pointless exercise, really. One breath, two insults: the blood slur, and the needle she has run since B1 ([painted-life](../b1/painted-life.md), DIV-074). **The kill shot is the hands** — purity logic applied to physical dirt.
3. **Hermione charges.** Not Harry. **This is the first time, and canon has her do the same thing to the same person a year later** — the B3 slap is the repeat, not the precedent. Under DIV-118 *slaps people on his behalf* is already evidence the world misreads, so the triangle's own engine supplies the brawl. **Ron gets hold of Hermione.**
4. **And Harry grabs Dracaena.** Not his friend: the Malfoy. She throws him off, glasses and all, snarling to get past him at Granger. **Nobody in the shop reads it, least of all him.**
5. **Lucius goes for Arthur.** The Muggle Protection Act and the raids are what he shouts about; what set him off is a boy catching hold of his daughter in public to keep her out of a fight (DIV-009).
6. **Narcissa stops it before blows.** She takes her husband's hand: *My, my. Still so full of vim and vigour. Perhaps I should be more* **diligent** *in my duties.* A wink, and the family is called to order. **Hagrid's canon intervention is cut.**
7. **Her look at Harry.** He is on the floor without his glasses while she handles her husband, and she takes her time over the boy her daughter will not stop writing about. All he gets is a voice — the only amused thing in the shop — and no face to put to it.
8. **The glasses come back late.** Hermione finds them bent or smeared, and by the time they are back on, the Malfoys are at the door.
9. **The diary** reaches Ginny's cauldron. How is deliberately left loose (Q-130).

## What it plants

- **The corridor, two years early** (DIV-095, DIV-104). She is vile to Hermione, Harry is angrier than he can say, and he goes to *her* without thinking. September of B4 is the same pattern with a ferret in it; February is where he tells her what the carry was. **She files this one wrong on the day**, as Potter defending Granger, which is what she files everything as — **and she is never given it back** (Q-154, answered). The reader is the only one who ever connects the two. **Harry gets the callback instead**, in B3, over the Monster Book (Q-155).
- **The teasing**, so the Top Box is a callback (DIV-116).
- **Narcissa's face, for Ron, Hermione and Molly**, so at the Cup Ron names her with feeling rather than guessing.
- **Narcissa's interest in Harry**, which is the start date for DIV-040. What she does with the two years in between: Q-039.

## The two bruises

Neither is remarked on, by anyone, ever.

- **The duties are what the curse took** (DIV-030). On a first read the line is flirtation; on a reread, a woman who cannot have more children is making light of it in public and her husband, who has never forgiven himself, has to stand there and take it.
- **Lucius is spared the blush only because his colour is already up** from the row. That is the gag and the hiding place at once.

## Must keep

The slight with the hands in it, Hermione charging rather than Harry, Harry grabbing Dracaena, Narcissa defusing it rather than Hagrid, and Harry never seeing her face.

## Open

- **The exact wording of Narcissa's line.** *Vim and vigour* is the author's; *diligent* is settled over *neglecting*, because diligence is a promise rather than a confession and it flatters him.
- **How far the slur carries** in a public shop in front of Molly and Arthur Weasley, and whether anybody reacts to the word itself. It has to be audible or Hermione has no cue.
- Q-130: the diary's staging stays loose.
- Q-155: which version of the B3 Monster Book callback, and whether Hermione shows that she remembers this day.
