# Sectumsempra

## Purpose

The bottom of B6's nightmare for both of them. The girl who promised to be there for him has broken up with him (DIV-015) and vanished into the Death Eaters. His hurt ends in a spell from the Prince's book that nearly kills her, and scars her for life (DIV-032).

## Beats

1. **The Mirror.** Harry, who has spent months trying to find out where she disappears to, finds her in front of the Mirror of Erised. She goes there to see the Yule Ball: she longs for the dream to come back (DIV-043). For a second, before the duel, Harry sees their wedding in it.
2. **The duel.** Their first since she left the Duelling Club (DIV-028). The Mirror breaks during the fight. He casts Sectumsempra without knowing what it does, and she nearly bleeds out.
3. **"So you hate me this much...?"** She asks it lying in his arms, in a pool of her own blood. It's the final nail in the coffin: she believes he should hate her, and now he has proved he does ([after-sectumsempra](after-sectumsempra.md)).
4. **Kreacher.** Harry sends Kreacher for help, and Kreacher obeys in his own way: he fetches Madam Pomfrey and lets out that Harry cast Dark magic on her.
5. **Pomfrey** runs to the scene and sends Kreacher for Snape, the expert.
6. **Snape** arrives too late to prevent the scars, but in time to dispel the curse. At least one scar runs from her belly to her neck (DIV-032).
7. **The burial.** Snape Obliviates Pomfrey, and forbids Harry from ever going near Dracaena again, on pain of death.

## The burial *(settled)* · DIV-043

Snape is keeping Dracaena out of the spotlight. An inquiry into a Death Eater's daughter nearly killed by Dark magic, with Aurors at the castle (canon), would expose her Mark and her mission.

- **Pomfrey.** After the Obliviation, Snape tells her Dark magic is still at work, so her dressings are never to be changed without him present. That covers him, and keeps the Mark on her left arm out of sight.
- **Crabbe and Goyle.** Before leaving for the site of the duel, he summons them to the Hospital Wing as muscle to guard her. The first aid takes long enough for them to get in place. They don't know what happened. That first day, when Snape brings her in, Harry follows and tries to force his way past them, and gets violent.
- **Harry.** Forbidden to come near her, on pain of death. Snape guards her door himself ([after-sectumsempra](after-sectumsempra.md)).
- **Detention.** Snape gives Harry detention. Officially it's for screaming at Snape and for getting violent with Crabbe and Goyle when they bar his way; they get off scot-free, having acted in self-defence. The details are open (Q-057).
- **The official story** *(settled)*. An old enchanted mirror in a disused room shattered on her. It explains the slashes, the Dark magic and the place, and the evidence is really there. It's an accident with no culprit, so there's nothing for the Aurors to chase. Unspoken irony: it blames the dream, not Harry.
- **What holds it up** *(settled)*. Pomfrey remembers Snape bringing her in. Snape found her on his night patrol. Dracaena tells the same story, since any spotlight threatens her mission. Dumbledore backs it: it's his Mirror, and he knows the truth.
- **Dumbledore** knows, through Snape (DIV-044).
- **The curse** is fought off over the following days with counter-curses and potions, and fades. The scars stay.

## Must keep

The scene itself. It will be hard, and that's the point.

**Her visits** *(settled)*. She has visited the Mirror regularly since she found it, at night, on her way back from the Room of Requirement. She found it wandering the castle, thinking of ways to complete her mission and lamenting her lack of progress.

**The Mirror breaks** *(settled)*. A powerful symbol, and hopeful in a twisted way: the vision is gone, so if you want it now you have to work for it in the real world. Never spell it out: show it broken, and let readers work it out.

**The two visions** *(settled)*. Hers is the past: the Yule Ball. His is a future: their wedding, even at their darkest. She can't allow herself one. It also extends B1, where the Mirror showed him the family he lost. The reader learns hers through her "sweet dream" line in the Hospital Wing ([after-sectumsempra](after-sectumsempra.md)), unless her side is on the page somewhere.

## Notes

The Prince's book is behind both of Harry's B6 wins over her: the Draught of Living Death (DIV-011) and this.

Sectumsempra is Snape's own invention (canon): his favourite pupil, scarred by his own spell in the hands of James Potter's son (Q-043).
