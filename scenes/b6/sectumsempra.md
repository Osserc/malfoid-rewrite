# Sectumsempra

## Purpose

The bottom of B6's nightmare for both of them. The girl who promised to be there for him broke up with him brutally at the start of term (DIV-015) and has vanished into the Death Eaters. His hurt ends in a spell from the Prince's book that nearly kills her, and scars her for life (DIV-032).

## Beats

1. Canon default, kept: Harry finds her breaking down in the bathroom.
2. They duel. He casts Sectumsempra without knowing what it does, and she nearly bleeds out.
3. Snape saves her, but too late to prevent the scars. At least one runs from her belly to her neck (DIV-032).

## Must keep

The scene itself. It will be hard, and that's the point.

## Notes

The Prince's book is behind both of Harry's B6 wins over her: the Draught of Living Death (DIV-011) and this.

Sectumsempra is Snape's own invention (canon): his favourite pupil, scarred by his own spell in the hands of James Potter's son (Q-043).
