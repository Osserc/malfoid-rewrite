# The Breakup

## Purpose

The end of the hollow weeks, and the breakup DIV-015 promises: brutal enough to lead in the end to Sectumsempra.

## Beats

1. **The hollow weeks** lead up to it. She's distant, only physical with him, won't talk, keeps disappearing, and has quit the Quidditch team ([sleeping-beauty](../../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).
2. **The offer.** Flitwick is reopening the Duelling Club. Harry sets up a "date": he tells her, and says he's missed their bouts.
3. **The refusal.** She won't come.
4. **The fight.** They fight, and it ends badly. They're over.

## Why the Duelling Club

The club is where they learned each other ([duelling-club](../../threads/duelling-club.md), DIV-028). Harry offers the best of their past, and that's exactly what she refuses. She has a practical reason too: her evenings belong to the Room of Requirement. And it sets up the Mirror: their next duel is Sectumsempra (DIV-043).

## Must keep

The offer comes from love: he misses them.
