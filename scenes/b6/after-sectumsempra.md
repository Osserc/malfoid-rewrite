# After Sectumsempra

## Purpose

The bottom of the nightmare, seen from both sides. Harry comes to apologise and finds no way back: whatever hope he had that she still loves him dies here. B6 turns much darker on purpose, as canon does ([style](../../core/style.md)). We cast them into hell before we bring them back.

## Two perspectives *(settled)*

**Hers.** She still loves him. Since the breakup ([the-breakup](the-breakup.md)) she has believed Harry should hate her, and has tried desperately to make him: she can't see how he could ever love her after Voldemort co-opted her. Yet part of her still wishes her prince were real. Sectumsempra is the final nail in the coffin. On the floor, in his arms, in a pool of her own blood, she asked whether he hates her this much ([sectumsempra](sectumsempra.md)). Now it's the perfect fuel. In her mind she's mixing him up with Voldemort: both have marked her. Hence "you wanted to mark me, too?", which Harry misses. When she accuses him of wanting only her body, she's firing the ammunition she gathered in the hollow weeks, when she wouldn't let him touch her and twisted his frustration into him seeing her as a thing ([sleeping-beauty](../../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)).

**His.** He thought he could still fix this: apologise, nurse her back to health, kiss her scars better. Her accusation is when he fully grasps the damage he has done. He broke her heart, and it was all his fault. Any hope that she still loved him is gone.

## Beats

1. **Four days.** Snape guards her door and has forbidden Harry to come near her (DIV-043). Harry begs for two days, then waits two more for Snape's guard to slip, and darts in under the Invisibility Cloak as Snape leaves. On the first day, when Snape brought her in, Harry followed and tried to force his way in. Crabbe and Goyle, already summoned by Snape, held him back while Madam Pomfrey gave her a potion. He got violent with them, which helped earn him detention (Q-057).
2. **Her bed.** Behind the curtains, under Muffliato, he finds her bandaged and struggling to breathe, the curse still fighting the counter-curses and potions inside her. He kneels and rests his forehead on her hand.
3. **The perfume.** Pear and vanilla, through the smell of blood: the perfume he chose for her. Flashback to the date ([the-pear-perfume](../b5/the-pear-perfume.md)). She still wears it, months after the breakup.
4. **Awake.** She's hostile: if he won't leave, he can at least show his face. Has he come to admire his work, or to finish it? A coughing fit. He helps her drink her potion, and she lets him.
5. **The scar.** He apologises: he didn't know what the spell did. She opens her gown on the black scar that runs from her belly to her neck. They match now: did he want to mark her, too? He misses the wording. Snape says this one is permanent. Leaving his sign is something he shares with the Dark Lord.
6. **The break.** He protests he's nothing like Voldemort. Her act drops: he tore her apart. Princes are supposed to save their princesses. Why couldn't he let her have her sweet dream? She wishes he'd killed her: then she'd never have found out who he really is.
7. **His hope dies.** She'd rather be dead. There's no coming back from this.
8. **The grip.** He pulls her arms from her face to make her listen: all he wanted was her, and he's been following her because he missed them. His grip leaves marks and reopens minor wounds.
9. **The accusation.** With contempt, she guides his hand to her breast: that's what he wants, so let him do it and leave. That's all she was to him.
10. **Exit.** Horrified, he lets go. Without a word he takes his Cloak, rings her bell for Pomfrey, and leaves. Behind the door she cries. He runs.

## Must keep

- **The perfume.** The reader's proof that she still loves him. Harry registers it, then loses it under her words.
- **"Mark me, too."** The reader catches it; Harry doesn't ([reveals](../../continuity/reveals.md)).
- **"We match now."** Bitter here. Later she likes her scars because they match his (DIV-032).
- **Kissing her scars better.** His dead hope here becomes their game later (DIV-032).
- **"Princes".** The spell that cut her came from the Prince's book.
- **"My sweet dream".** Literal: he caught her watching the Yule Ball in the Mirror (DIV-043).
- **The grip is desperation, not force.** The wounds reopen by accident. She reads it as a violation; the reader mustn't.
- **The reversal.** In the hollow weeks she wouldn't let him touch her. Here she puts his hand on her breast, with contempt.
