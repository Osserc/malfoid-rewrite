# Dumbledore's Counsel

## Purpose

Harry isn't left alone at the bottom. Dumbledore knows what happened, through Snape. He knows her mission, and he has encouraged the couple since B4 (DIV-046), so silence from him would read as cold (DIV-044).

## Beats

1. **No lesson.** Canon has none between Sectumsempra and the cave, and we don't reshuffle. That could be an excuse to leave it alone, but our Dumbledore wouldn't.
2. **The walk.** Harry is angry at Snape, having just learned he overheard the prophecy (canon), and he has spent the whole year angry over the breakup. Dumbledore dangles adventure in front of him. On the walk to Hogsmeade he takes the chance to say something, cryptically, about the whole incident (Q-055, answered).
3. **Rosmerta.** Right after, they greet Rosmerta, which is how Dracaena learns Dumbledore has left the school (canon).

## What he brings *(draft)*

- **Compassion, knowing.** He knows she has been sent to kill him, and still speaks of her with compassion. On a reread after the tower, that hits hard.
- **No absolution.** He can't forgive Harry on her behalf. Only she can.
- **Ariana.** He once fought a duel in anger, his sister died, and he never knew whose spell killed her (canon, revealed in B7). He doesn't tell Harry, but it's why he speaks as he does. The reader understands only in B7.
- **The cave.** Canon's potion makes him relive his worst memory, begging to be hurt instead of "them". It echoes the Ariana undertone without him saying a word about her.
- **The tower.** The same night, he talks to her on the tower while Harry listens, frozen. The two talks mirror each other, so he doesn't speak to her before then.
