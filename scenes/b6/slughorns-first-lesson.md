# Slughorn's First Lesson

## Purpose

The Prince's first win over her, and the hollow weeks seen in class (DIV-015). It also plants her perfume in B6, so the Hospital Wing scene lands ([after-sectumsempra](after-sectumsempra.md)).

## Beats

1. **Amortentia.** Slughorn shows the class a few potions. Hermione names the Amortentia (canon). Dracaena knows it at a glance and doesn't raise her hand (DIV-045).
2. **The smell.** Amortentia smells of whatever attracts you. Harry smells pear and vanilla: her perfume ([the-pear-perfume](../b5/the-pear-perfume.md)). She's standing beside him, wearing it, and he can't tell whether it comes from the cauldron or from her.
3. **The Draught of Living Death.** Harry beats her with the Prince's notes (DIV-011). She and Hermione both notice how much his tricks resemble her technique (Q-042).

## Must keep

Her silence is shown, never explained. Her drive is gone.
