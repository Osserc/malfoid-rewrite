# Kreacher and the Manor

## Purpose

Kreacher's ending, and the only place the elf design is allowed hope. It also gives Hermione's S.P.E.W. somewhere to arrive.

## What canon does

After Harry learns what Regulus asked of Kreacher and what Kreacher did, the trio start treating him as a person. He transforms — clean, devoted, cooking — and leads the Hogwarts elves into the Battle of Hogwarts.

## The problem this has to solve

**Kreacher is already completely Harry's**, through Sirius's will: bound to Grimmauld Place in the ordinary way, with Harry occupying the Black position. So nothing in his ending can turn on being given to Harry, or on Harry being kind to him, because both are already true and neither has changed anything. Canon's warming is a start and cannot be the finish.

An earlier draft had him ask to be severed from the house and re-anchored to Harry, as Dobby was. **Struck as underbaked** (Q-095, retired): it asks Harry to deliberately do the thing that killed Dobby, it makes Kreacher's survival depend on timing, and it leaves the institution exactly where it was.

## The direction · DIV-078

**The answer is Hermione's, and it is a method rather than a kindness.** She has two things nobody else has ever had: a hole in the archive where the history should be, and **a working anomaly she can study** — Dobby, the partial bind, a thousand-year-old oversight eating toast at the Burrow. The curse has a bug, therefore it has seams, therefore it can be worked.

Not a spell that frees an elf — freeing kills them, and she learns that in B4 ([the-kitchens](../b4/the-kitchens.md)). A way for a bound elf to **recover its natural state over time**, as a house-spirit, in a household that has decided never to use what it holds. Slow, partial, procedural, and the first real thing anyone has done about this in a thousand years.

- **Kreacher is the prototype**, at Malfoy Manor, after the war.
- **It is not one click and not one scene.** It is her whole S.P.E.W. questline, undesigned (Q-100).
- **She is not powerful and the method must not need power.** Precision, research and cleverness are what she has, and what she is up against is the oldest dark magic in Britain. She does not beat it. She finds the seam.
- **There is nothing to break.** The binding was cast long ago and what it sits on now is the elves — no artefact, no seat, nothing to destroy. The only instrument still standing is the house, so Grimmauld Place is the seat of Kreacher's binding and of nothing wider (DIV-075).
- **And it is not a repeat of the corridor.** Harry's accident worked once because he and Dobby were the right two people at the right moment on the right seam. Hermione is clever enough to see it cannot be extended, and does not try: she takes it as proof the magic has seams, and finds a different one (Q-100).
- **The cost is houses, and it never goes away.** Whatever the method is, performing it ends the house the elf leaves — which is why Grimmauld dies mechanically rather than sentimentally. Old houses are the only thing that ever made elves and are already disappearing (DIV-035), so every elf saved costs one of the few left. Scaled, it finishes what the ancient wizards started. Scaled or not, it means the one person ever to do anything for the elves is a Muggle-born dismantling the oldest houses in Britain, which is the purebloods' best genuine grievance and does not make her wrong (Q-100).
- **Kreacher's cost is consent.** Leaving Grimmauld means giving up the house of Black, the wall of heads, and everything he has ever been, on the word of a Muggle-born. That is his sacrifice, and it is the right one for him.

## Grimmauld Place dies *(settled)*

Its spirit walks out and the Black house ends — which is also Kreacher's verdict on the Blacks, delivered without a word, and him leaving his old life behind.

## The feud *(draft)*

Dobby and Kreacher meet at Grimmauld, earlier than canon's meeting and with far more in it.

- **Not about manners.** Kreacher is a house that has never been anything else, looking at a thing with no house that is becoming something else. Dobby is what Kreacher would be if Grimmauld ever let him go, and the contempt is horror.
- **He can see Dobby is dying,** alone of everyone, and says so constantly as an insult. Nobody listens, because he is Kreacher. After Christmas, Harry remembers he was told.
### How it starts *(settled)*: the portrait

Harry asks Dobby whether anything can be done about Mrs Black's shrieking portrait, which is fixed to the wall by magic nobody in the Order has beaten.

- **Dobby doesn't think he can.** He tries anyway, out of habit — reaching into the house the way he reaches into Malfoy Manor — and expects nothing, because this is not his house and he has no business in it.
- **And he can, a little.** Not because of any claim: because he is a glitch. He is partially bound, anchored to a person and no demesne (DIV-075), so there is nothing about him for a house to recognise as foreign and nothing to push against. The curse never anticipated him, and Grimmauld's defences are written against elves that belong somewhere.
- **He has to wade through Ur-Kreacher** to do it, which is deeply unpleasant: a century of Black cruelty as texture, pushing back at him the whole way. And Kreacher feels it from the inside — something is *in* him.
- **It costs him a great deal,** and it takes him days to come right, and nobody connects that to anything.
- **He gets the painting off the wall** before Ur-Kreacher, slow and rusty from a decade with nobody in the house, can throw him out.
- **Kreacher's first appearance is a scream:** *TRESPASSER!* — which is exactly, correctly, what Dobby is.
- **And it makes Kreacher's hatred earned.** Dobby has destroyed a fixture of the house of Black, which is the same thing Harry did to the Malfoys, from the other side. Kreacher is not merely being a snob.
- **Hermione is in the room.** An elf doing a thing no elf can do is her first hard datum, years before she knows what to do with it (Q-100).

## The battle

He leads the castle's elves up out of the kitchens (canon). Under our rules that is the Hogwarts elf — a thousand years of keeping children warm — joining in, roused by an elf whose own house no longer exists. Who the castle's household is, and therefore who could have ordered this instead: Q-099.

## The ending · Q-096

- Harry and Dracaena live at **Malfoy Manor**. Not Grimmauld, which is dead; not Godric's Hollow; not a new house. She would not abandon her ancestral house.
- **The Manor will never make another elf.** Nothing has been made since the binding, and Dobby is gone for good (Q-097, answered). So the house stands empty of itself until something foreign fills it: **Kreacher colonises the Manor.**
- A Black elf keeping a Malfoy house, which Lucius has to live with, and which by Kreacher's own snobbery is entirely proper — Narcissa was a Black, so this is a Black house continuing, and he will say so at length. He then spends decades doting on Potter half-bloods and would die before admitting it.
- A grumpy elf butler playing with Harry and Dracaena's children, in the house Dobby was made of, living the life Dobby wanted.
- **The wound stays open.** Kreacher gets Dobby's dream, knows it, and is the only one who ever knew Dobby was dying. If the remembering falls to him — one object kept, one thing he will not explain to the children — the ending keeps its cost without anyone having to say so.

## Must keep

- Kreacher is already Harry's, and that is the problem, not the solution.
- Kreacher knows Dobby is dying, says so as an insult, and is ignored.
- Hermione's method, not Harry's kindness, is what changes anything, and it is not a reproduction of what Harry did.
- It costs the house. Grimmauld dies because of the method, not because of grief.
- Grimmauld dies when he leaves it.
- The Manor, because she would not leave her house.
- Kreacher never finds out what Ur-Kreacher was.
