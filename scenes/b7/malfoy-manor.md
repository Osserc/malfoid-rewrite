# Malfoy Manor

## Purpose

The hinge of B7. It brings Dracaena back into the story halfway through the book instead of at the very end, reunites the couple, turns the Malfoys and gives Ron his return. For Harry it's the first time he drops the plan for her, a rehearsal for King's Cross (DIV-048).

## Why they go *(settled)* · DIV-049

- **The locket.** They have it and no way to destroy it. Harry remembers Dracaena letting slip in B2 that her house is full of Dark things (canon's hook is the hidden chamber under the drawing-room floor; the B2 trip has to keep it, Q-071). They figure something there might destroy even a Horcrux. What: Q-064.
- **Christmas.** She's home for the holidays.
- **The plan.** Harry and Hermione plan it with Dobby as a quick in and out. Ron has left (canon).

## Harry's second motive *(settled)* · DIV-049

He wants to see her one last time. He isn't coming to whisk her away: he doesn't believe she wants him anymore. But he owes her one last apology, and he needs to know whether he's out of her life for good. He won't let the flight from the tower be their last meeting, or worse, a battlefield. She's the best thing that ever happened to him, and now he knows she's been in Voldemort's hands. He has to try.

So once they're inside, he breaks from the plan on purpose and heads for her room. How he knows the way: Q-064.

## Beats

1. Planning: Dobby warns them he can't Apparate with too many people, because he isn't powerful enough. This limit matters later. He is feeble everywhere except here, where the Manor's residual claim on him still holds (DIV-075).
2. Inside, Harry deviates from the plan and goes to her room.
3. Plans change as the infiltration goes. They find the prisoners, and Dobby overexerts himself getting them away. Who's in the cellar: Q-064.
4. Their meeting, and the reconciliation (Q-063). She decides to fight. She asks Harry to get her parents out too: once Voldemort and Bellatrix learn she's gone, they'll kill them, and she won't leave without them (DIV-052).
5. Ron comes back through the Deluminator, straight into the Manor, and fights side by side with Harry against its occupants: his redemption for leaving (DIV-051).
6. *(draft)* **The resonance showcase.** Bellatrix, Voldemort's best witch, gives whoever else is fighting a very hard time, Ron perhaps. Harry and Ron are formidable together after seven years side by side, and it still isn't enough. She's on the back foot only once Dracaena fights beside Harry. They sync because they've spent years duelling each other (DIV-028). This is the first time they're on the same side, and their last duel was Sectumsempra. It's a near-miss, not full resonance: two wands, because one wand in two hands belongs to the final duel (🔒 DIV-006). It breaks on the stained soul and hurts her (DIV-021), which may be why they get out instead of winning. It prefigures the final duel only a little. Bellatrix's taunts can make her niece the third blood traitor, after Sirius and Andromeda. Q-028
7. Dobby's moment. Out of love for Harry, he gets over his fear of his former masters, along the lines of "Harry Potter asked Dobby to save his family!", meaning hers (wording to polish). DIV-050
8. Harry holds off Bellatrix and the others and is the last out, with Dobby. Dracaena is second to last.
9. Once Harry is out, Dobby has nothing left. He starts crumbling into dust, gets out something along the lines of "Did Dobby help his friend Harry Potter?", and is gone (wording to polish). DIV-050
9b. **The true form, in public.** As the body goes, what has been surfacing in him since B2 finally stands up: a translucent figure taller than a man, fair-haired, with Malfoy grey eyes. The house wearing the family's likeness, because that is what it was made of. **Everyone present sees it and nobody knows what it means.** Lucius recognises *a Malfoy* and has no context at all for why. Nothing is explained here. DIV-050, DIV-075
9c. **Hermione explains it afterwards** — the only person alive who can, out of a year in the archives, and the one moment her research changes anything for anyone. DIV-075
10. Dracaena stays at Harry's side from here on (DIV-052).
11. *(draft)* The Malfoys come to some closure over Dobby, realising what they did to him. This may be what turns her parents for good: they decide to fight rather than run, as they had planned (DIV-052).

## Dobby's death *(settled)* · DIV-050

- **Exhaustion, not a knife.** Canon has Bellatrix kill him with a thrown knife. Ours has him spend himself ferrying people out on his own Apparition. His death becomes a choice made out of love, and his limit is set up in the planning (beat 1).
- **He has been dying for five years.** Unhoused since B2, he has been reverting towards an elf's true shape, and the true shape needs a house to sustain it. The reversion is the dying, and it has been spent on Harry's errands all along (DIV-075, DIV-076). The B3 "have you gotten taller?" is the first sign of it.
- **Kreacher knew** and said so as an insult, and nobody listened (DIV-078).
- **He spends his house to empty it.** The residual claim is the only reason his Apparition works here at all; the last trip out is the one with no return.
- **And he was already nearly gone.** Five years of Harry's errands and the Weasleys' absurd improvements, paid for out of Harry's vault and built out of Dobby (DIV-076). The kindness is what wore him down.
- **His death is real,** unlike Kreacher's would be: there is no house left to make him again. Freedom is what makes it final, and he chooses it anyway.
- **The Malfoys too.** He saves the family that abused him, because Harry asked, and to Dobby they're Harry's family.
- **Afterwards.** He leaves no body. Harry scatters his ashes and makes him a tomb anyway, on the cliff at Shell Cottage ([apparate-dancing](apparate-dancing.md)).
- **And he knows to because of Winky.** Three years earlier Dobby asked him to give her a grave — the first an elf ever had — because he thought she should be honoured, and had no idea he was describing his own death (DIV-077, [the-kitchens](../b4/the-kitchens.md)). Nobody asks this time.
- **The epitaph is not canon's** *(settled)*. Canon's stone reads *a free elf*, and under our rules that is Harry carving the cause of death and calling it an honour. Worse, it is not even what Dobby wanted: freedom was never a value he held, only the word to hand for wanting to stop, and what he actually wanted was a family to keep ([house-elves](../../setting/house-elves.md)). So the stone says something he would have liked. **Leading candidate: *Here lies Dobby, my friend*** — Harry's own words, in Harry's own voice, and the word he insisted on in the corridor instead of *master* (DIV-076).
- **The Manor's geist.** His bond with the Manor makes his magic stronger there: he's still something of a Malfoy spirit. What elves are: [house-elves](../../setting/house-elves.md), DIV-075. The Manor has been cold since Lucius cut him loose in B2, and dies properly tonight — which is what Kreacher colonises after the war ([kreacher-and-the-manor](kreacher-and-the-manor.md), DIV-078).

## The cover *(settled)* · DIV-049

The Death Eaters believe Harry and his friends broke in to free the captives and the Malfoys. They have no reason to suspect anything else, or to alert Gringotts. They'd also rather not advertise that the Malfoys defected: the Death Eaters know, and no one else does (DIV-052).

## The sword *(settled)* · DIV-051

Snape never brings Harry the sword, so there's no doe in the Forest of Dean. The sword stays at Hogwarts, waiting in the Hat for Neville (canon, [final-duel](final-duel.md)). The Manor has what the locket needs instead (Q-064). With no frozen pool to pull Harry out of, Ron's return moves to the raid.
