# King's Cross

## Purpose

The turn of Harry's arc. He walked into the forest to die for the purpose Dumbledore gave him. Here he chooses to live, for the girl he loves. It answers his walk to the forest the way her stand answers her B6 despair ([final-duel](final-duel.md)). The Manor was the rehearsal: there he first drops the plan for her ([malfoy-manor](malfoy-manor.md), DIV-049).

## Beats

1. Struck by the Killing Curse in the forest, Harry finds himself somewhere in between (canon). What's left of canon's King's Cross: Q-061.
2. He can go on, or go back (canon).
3. *(settled)* He stays there while Voldemort plays with his body and Hagrid carries it to the castle. Canon's Harry wakes at once and has to play dead through all of it; ours is simply away. Narcissa isn't in the forest, so nobody lies about his death (DIV-052).
4. *(settled)* When she makes her stand, he goes back for Dracaena. Not to finish the job, not to beat Voldemort: he wants his girl, and she's out there alone. That's what keeps him going. DIV-048. How he knows: Q-061.

## Why he goes back *(settled)* · DIV-048

- **Canon's reason is the job.** Harry goes back because the work isn't finished and his return may spare others. That keeps him what Dumbledore made him to the end: a means, whose life matters for what it's for.
- **Ours completes his arc.** He goes from dying for a purpose handed to him by the man he trusted (Snape's memories, the walk to the forest), to dying so the people he loves can live, her first of all (he stops for her, DIV-022), to living for her, because he loves her and she's fighting alone.
- **The job is no reason anyway.** His death protects no one, and he was never really chosen (DIV-026). Voldemort's defeat follows from his return, but it isn't why he returns.
- **The seed is the forest.** He stops for her, which canon's Harry couldn't do for Ginny, and walks in with a glimmer of hope that Dumbledore may be wrong (DIV-022).
- **It isn't a rescue.** She's back on her feet with her wand on Voldemort before she knows he's alive (DIV-023), and her turn has to be her own act (Q-013). He goes back to stand with her, not to save her.

## Must keep

He goes back for her: not for the job, not for Voldemort.
