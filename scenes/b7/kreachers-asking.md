# Kreacher's Asking

## Purpose

Kreacher's redemption, and the only place the elf design is allowed a hopeful ending. It fills the stretch of B7 the outline currently has nothing in (Q-013, Q-065), and it turns the epilogue from decoration into the payoff of Harry's own want.

## What canon does

After Harry learns what Regulus asked of Kreacher and what Kreacher did, the trio start treating him as a person. He transforms — clean, devoted, cooking — and leads the Hogwarts elves into the Battle of Hogwarts.

## The problem this has to solve

Kreacher is already Harry's, through Sirius's will. That is the *ordinary* configuration: he is bound to Grimmauld Place, and Harry is simply the current occupant of the Black position. So "becoming Harry's elf" is not a change, and canon's warming can't be the whole arc.

## Ours · DIV-078

### Autumn: canon's warming, with Dobby in it

Canon's Regulus sequence stands and lands where it lands. Running alongside it is the feud.

- **Dobby follows Harry to Grimmauld** and meets Kreacher — earlier than canon's meeting, and with far more in it.
- **The feud is not about manners.** Kreacher is a house that has never been anything else, looking at a thing with no house that is slowly becoming something else. Dobby is what Kreacher would be if Grimmauld ever let him go, and Kreacher's contempt is horror.
- **Kreacher can see that Dobby is dying.** He is the only one in the series who can. He says so constantly, as an insult — *the Potter elf is half gone already* — and every human in the room hears spite and ignores him, because he is Kreacher.
- **He is right.** After Christmas, Harry remembers he was told.

### Christmas: Dobby dies

[malfoy-manor](malfoy-manor.md), DIV-050. Kreacher is not there. He finds out, and what he feels he expresses as contempt, and nobody reads it.

### February–April: the asking

- **Harry won't offer.** He has done this once and it is dying in front of him. Having learned that his regard is not free, he withholds it — which is a new kind of cruelty and his own to carry.
- **So Kreacher asks.** He comes to Harry and asks to be dismissed from the house and taken by the person: the Dobby configuration, deliberately, with full knowledge of what it did to Dobby, having watched it and understood it better than anyone alive.
- **Harry has to do the thing he watched kill his friend.** B2 was Lucius throwing the sock and Harry catching what fell, by accident. This is Harry throwing it himself, on purpose, and being the only net.
- **Not a house-elf. An Elf.** Dobby's was an accident. Kreacher's is a decision, and he is the first ever made on purpose.
- **Grimmauld Place dies.** Its spirit walks out of it, and the Black house ends — which is also Kreacher's verdict on the Blacks, delivered without a word. He leaves his old life behind and the building stops being anything.
- **And he begins to run down,** exactly as Dobby did, for exactly the same reason: Harry still has no house.

### The battle

He leads the castle's elves up out of the kitchens (canon). Under our rules that is the Hogwarts elf — Hufflepuff's elf, a thousand years of keeping children warm — joining in, roused by a Black elf who no longer has a house. Two houses, one of which no longer exists.

### The epilogue · Q-096

**What saves Kreacher is that Harry stops being homeless.**

- Harry and Dracaena live at **Malfoy Manor**. Not Grimmauld, which is dead; not Godric's Hollow; not a new house. Dracaena would not abandon her ancestral house, and the Manor is standing empty of itself since Dobby.
- So the thing that killed Dobby — a person-anchored elf running on one person with nothing behind him — becomes survivable the moment that person has somewhere to live. **Dobby died of Harry's homelessness.** Kreacher lives because Dracaena brought a house.
- **Kreacher restores the Manor.** A Black elf keeping a Malfoy house, which is only possible because he is anchored to a person, and which Lucius has to live with. By Kreacher's own snobbery it is entirely proper: Narcissa was a Black, so this is a Black house continuing, and he will say so at length. He then spends forty years doting on Potter half-bloods and would die before admitting it.
- **A grumpy elf butler playing with Harry and Dracaena's children**, in the house Dobby was made of, living the dream Dobby was denied.
- **The wound stays open.** Kreacher gets Dobby's dream, knows it, and is the only one who ever knew Dobby was dying. If the remembering falls to him — one object kept, one thing he will not explain to the children — the ending keeps its cost without anyone having to say so. Q-096.

## Must keep

- Kreacher knows Dobby is dying, says so as an insult, and is ignored.
- Harry refuses to offer; Kreacher asks.
- Kreacher knows exactly what he is asking for.
- Grimmauld dies when he leaves it.
- The Manor, not Grimmauld or Godric's Hollow, because she would not leave her house.
- Kreacher is never told, and never finds out, what Ur-Kreacher was.
