# Charity Burbage

## Purpose

The nightmare at its most literal: her teacher, killed at her family's table. Not a beloved mentor — Dracaena's failure here is that she can't manage even the lesser thing (DIV-012).

## Beats

1. Burbage hangs captive above the Malfoys' table (canon default).
2. She pleads with Snape (canon default), and with Dracaena, her best student, believing she reached her. She didn't: the interest was always in the material, never in her (DIV-012). Dracaena owes her anyway — Burbage stood at the only door out of the script — and the whole debt lands at once, here.
3. The Death Eaters taunt Lucius and Narcissa about their daughter's Muggle Studies. This adds to canon's mockery of the family over its impure relatives.
4. Voldemort kills her (canon default).

## Must keep

The plea to Dracaena, and her parents' humiliation.

## Open

What Dracaena does, or fails to do. Burbage needs setting up on the page in B3–B6 (Q-020).
