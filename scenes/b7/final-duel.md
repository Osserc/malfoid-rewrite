# The Final Duel

## Purpose

The payoff of the series: the romance as the force that defeats Voldemort. It also answers her B6 despair (DIV-015). Then, she lost faith in a prince who was alive. Here she keeps it for one she believes is dead, because he told her never to give up hope (DIV-022).

## Beats

All beats are *(settled)*, part of DIV-023, unless marked 🔒 or *(draft)*.

### Her stand

1. Harry is shown dead. He's still in the in-between, so he feels none of Voldemort's games with his body (DIV-048). Narcissa isn't in the forest: she and Lucius are at Hogwarts (DIV-052). *(draft)* Voldemort has Harry's wand broken and shown with his body (Q-029).
2. While Voldemort taunts Neville, Dracaena steps forward and challenges him to a duel. Tears in her eyes and fury in her heart, she takes up the mantle of the boy she loves and fights in his stead.
3. Voldemort taunts her, and she'll have none of it. He's impressed by her skill, but she's outmatched.
4. Their spells start ricocheting. In the confusion Neville kills Nagini with Gryffindor's sword (canon), and the brawl breaks out.
5. She's wounded. McGonagall, Slughorn and Shacklebolt, canon's three duellists, step in to cover her and are overwhelmed.

### The return

6. She regains her strength and stands, wand pointed at Voldemort. In the chaos Harry, back from King's Cross for her ([kings-cross](kings-cross.md), DIV-048), gets up and walks towards her.
7. Voldemort, his assailants beaten, turns and freezes at the sight of Harry behind her. She looks round and sees him there, smiling.
8. Wandless, Harry raises his arm and puts his hand over hers, holding her wand with her.
9. 🔒 Harry and Dracaena face Voldemort together.
10. 🔒 Both use Dracaena's wand.
11. Voldemort's canon panic, but this time both of them are staring him down. A taunt about the love he despised and underestimated, along the lines of "Who's going to die for you today, Potter? Malfoy?" Their answer: "You are." (Wording to polish.)

### The duel

12. Voldemort gloats. He killed Harry once and will again, and he turned Dracaena away from him. Unfazed, they duel him in resonance (DIV-021), and he's shocked at how powerful they are.
13. The fight is hard. The resonance is volatile and shows cracks, but it holds. Voldemort uses every trick he has and can't get past them, and turns the battlefield against them (Neville's line, below).
14. He tries Legilimency. Dracaena wraps Harry's mind in hers, and Voldemort finds nothing but her love for Harry. It's a callback to B5 (canon), when he can't stay in Harry's mind once it fills with love.
15. He's on the back foot, then cornered.
16. 🔒 Their Expelliarmus meets his Killing Curse, and their "love" rebounds it onto him. It's their signature spell. He dies for good.

### Neville's line

All settled, part of DIV-025. It runs alongside beats 13–16.

- **N1.** Voldemort can't get past them, so he turns the battlefield against them. With the Elder Wand at full power (Q-011) he raises every body in the Great Hall as an Inferius, good and bad alike. He sees only bodies, and any will do (who has died by then: Q-033). He calls back the Dementors Dracaena drove off earlier in the battle (DIV-027) and sets the whole horde on Harry and Dracaena, who are locked on him and can't defend themselves.
- **N2.** Neville stands against them with his own wand and the sword of Gryffindor, holding the creatures off the duellists. The sword burns through the Inferi (why it burns: Neville's enchantment, or *Goblin silver* below). He keeps going as his injuries mount.
- **N3.** Surrounded, he goes down on one knee and hears the voice of Godric Gryffindor, along the lines of "If you fall here, all will be lost. So stand your ground, heir of Gryffindor!" (Wording to polish.)
- **N4.** He gets back up and rallies the DA. They raise walls of flame against the Inferi, and together they shield Harry and Dracaena from the horde and buy them time to finish Voldemort.
- **N5.** He sees Bellatrix in a duel (with Lucius and Narcissa, DIV-053) and doesn't go after her, because his friends need him. He too chooses love and justice over vengeance.
- **N6.** He survives.

## Mechanics

- No wand technicalities decide the duel (DIV-024). Together they are simply stronger.
- They are never disarmed when they're together. They're each other's strength.
- Harry's death in the forest protects no one (DIV-026). Voldemort's spells take hold on everyone in the hall, and every part has to be played.

## Proposals (not yet agreed)

- **Her stand has a job.** Voldemort's attention on her is what lets Neville get to Nagini. Without her challenge the snake lives and Voldemort can't die.
- **One loses, three lose, two win.** She fails alone. Three of the best adults, fighting side by side, fail too. Two fighting as one win. That shows resonance is about unity, not numbers, without anyone explaining it.
- **Taunts as wedges.** Every taunt aims at a crack in the sync: the Mark (which he can burn), Sectumsempra, the broken promise.
- **"Die for you."** Voldemort knows love only as sacrifice, because that's the only kind that has ever beaten him (Lily). He expects her to die for Harry. The new magic is love that lives.
- **Walls and embrace.** Her walls broke in 1996 (DIV-015). An embrace has nothing to break.
- **Silence.** Voldemort gets all the words. They never speak to each other or answer him, except "You are", said together. Canon's explaining while circling is cut.
- **The tale flips.** The prince is the one asleep, and she's the one who walks up to the dragon.
- **Two armies.** Voldemort fights with things that have no will: the dead and the soulless. The duellists are shielded by the living, who chose to be there.
- **Friendship's job.** Friendship can't resonate (DIV-021), so it can't win the duel. What it can do is buy love the time to win it.
- **Godric through the sword.** In canon the sword already chooses who is worthy, so the voice comes through it, once only. That stretches existing lore rather than adding a new rule. "Heir of Gryffindor" mirrors the Heir of Slytherin (B2): Voldemort is an heir by blood, Neville by choice.
- **The Patronuses Harry taught.** Against the Dementors, the DA shield him with the spell he taught them in B5. Canon has a precedent: Luna, Ernie and Seamus cast Patronuses for Harry during the battle.
- **The other boy.** The prophecy could have meant Neville. The boy who wasn't chosen holds the line for the one who never really was (DIV-026).
- **Goblin silver.** The sword takes in only what makes it stronger (in canon, the basilisk venom). Neville pulls it from the burning hat, so it takes in the fire and burns of its own accord. That gives the burning hat a reason to stay (Q-032).
- **Unseen.** Neville's line gets its own POV section, cut into the duel. Harry and Dracaena never see it, and only learn afterwards who held the line.
