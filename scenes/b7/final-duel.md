# The Final Duel

## Purpose

The payoff of the series: the romance as the force that defeats Voldemort. It also answers her B6 despair (DIV-015). Then, she lost faith in a prince who was alive. Here she keeps it for one she believes is dead, because he told her never to give up hope (DIV-022).

## Beats

All beats are *(settled)*, part of DIV-023, unless marked 🔒 or *(draft)*.

### Her stand

1. Harry is shown dead (canon; Narcissa's part: Q-032). *(draft)* Voldemort has Harry's wand broken and shown with his body (Q-029).
2. While Voldemort taunts Neville, Dracaena steps forward and challenges him to a duel. Tears in her eyes and fury in her heart, she takes up the mantle of the boy she loves and fights in his stead.
3. Voldemort taunts her, and she'll have none of it. He's impressed by her skill, but she's outmatched.
4. Their spells start ricocheting. In the confusion Neville kills Nagini with Gryffindor's sword (canon), and the brawl breaks out.
5. She's wounded. McGonagall, Slughorn and Shacklebolt, canon's three duellists, step in to cover her and are overwhelmed.

### The return

6. She regains her strength and stands, wand pointed at Voldemort. In the chaos Harry gets up and walks towards her.
7. Voldemort, his assailants beaten, turns and freezes at the sight of Harry behind her. She looks round and sees him there, smiling.
8. Wandless, Harry raises his arm and puts his hand over hers, holding her wand with her.
9. 🔒 Harry and Dracaena face Voldemort together.
10. 🔒 Both use Dracaena's wand.
11. Voldemort's canon panic, but this time both of them are staring him down. A taunt about the love he despised and underestimated, along the lines of "Who's going to die for you today, Potter? Malfoy?" Their answer: "You are." (Wording to polish.)

### The duel

12. Voldemort gloats. He killed Harry once and will again, and he turned Dracaena away from him. Unfazed, they duel him in resonance (DIV-021), and he's shocked at how powerful they are.
13. The fight is hard. The resonance is volatile and shows cracks, but it holds. Voldemort uses every trick he has and can't get past them.
14. He tries Legilimency. Dracaena wraps Harry's mind in hers, and Voldemort finds nothing but her love for Harry. It's a callback to B5 (canon), when he can't stay in Harry's mind once it fills with love.
15. He's on the back foot, then cornered.
16. 🔒 Their Expelliarmus meets his Killing Curse, and their "love" rebounds it onto him. It's their signature spell. He dies for good.

## Proposals (not yet agreed)

- **Her stand has a job.** Voldemort's attention on her is what lets Neville get to Nagini. Without her challenge the snake lives and Voldemort can't die.
- **One loses, three lose, two win.** She fails alone. Three of the best adults, fighting side by side, fail too. Two fighting as one win. That shows resonance is about unity, not numbers, without anyone explaining it.
- **Taunts as wedges.** Every taunt aims at a crack in the sync: the Mark (which he can burn), Sectumsempra, the broken promise.
- **"Die for you."** Voldemort knows love only as sacrifice, because that's the only kind that has ever beaten him (Lily). He expects her to die for Harry. The new magic is love that lives.
- **Walls and embrace.** Her walls broke in 1996 (DIV-015). An embrace has nothing to break.
- **Silence.** Voldemort gets all the words. They never speak to each other or answer him, except "You are", said together. Canon's explaining while circling is cut.
- **The tale flips.** The prince is the one asleep, and she's the one who walks up to the dragon.
