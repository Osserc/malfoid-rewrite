# Before the Forest

## Purpose

Their goodbye, and the hope that carries her through the final sequence (DIV-023). It reverses DIV-015: this time, whatever happens, she won't fall into despair.

## Beats

All beats are *(settled)*, part of DIV-022.

1. Harry has seen Snape's memories and is walking to the forest to die. He and Dracaena are back together by now (how: Q-013).
2. In canon he can't stop for Ginny. Here he stops for her.
3. He tells her never to give up hope.
4. He has a faint hope of his own. He has few reasons for it, but Dumbledore is one man and may be wrong, and didn't he always tell them to keep faith and be brave? With that glimmer he marches to his death.
5. She doesn't know exactly what's going to happen. She trusts him and prepares for the worst.

## Must keep

He stops for her. "Never give up hope." She trusts him without knowing what he's going to do. Whatever happens, she won't succumb to despair as she did in B6.

## Notes

His hope doesn't cheapen the sacrifice, as long as he'd go without it. The sacrifice counts because he's willing, not because he's certain. His hope also makes what he tells her honest.

The payoff is King's Cross, where he goes back for her ([kings-cross](kings-cross.md), DIV-048). Stopping for her is the seed.
