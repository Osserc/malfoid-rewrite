# Apparate dancing

## Purpose

The first light scene after the Manor, and the first dance since the Yule Ball. It does four things at once:

1. **A cute scene**, after the darkest stretch of the series.
2. **A feat for Harry.** Chaining Apparitions mid-dance, both of them, without a Splinch.
3. **Love in magic.** It works only because he wants to go somewhere with her ([magic](../../setting/magic.md)).
4. **She joins the trio.** She tags along on the quest from here, with a real limit: she can only get away with Harry.

## The problem *(settled)* · DIV-054

She can't Apparate: she skipped the B6 lessons ([dracaena-malfoy](../../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)). Side-Along would get her out, so it isn't that she can't come. It's pride and a real logistical concern at once.

- **Ron's point.** Under the teasing, he thinks she's a liability. Danger will keep mounting, and she's dead weight if someone has to stay near her at all times to get her out. If they're separated and need to escape, she's stranded, and Harry will stay behind with her, because of course he would. That's the crux.
- **Her pride.** Side-Along is how you move children and the wounded: hauled along, squeezed, with a crack. The other way out is lessons from Granger, in something a pure-blood should have mastered long ago.
- **No lessons in B7.** Teaching her unsupervised is too dangerous, and they can't afford a Splinching.
- **Why it has to be Harry** *(draft)*. Ron is clumsy enough Apparating himself, and carrying someone else would be a disaster. Hermione is magically weak ([society](../../setting/society.md)) and can't easily take someone along, least of all in a pinch when she's already spent. Canon fits: when she drags both boys out of the Ministry, Ron gets Splinched.

Harry's answer doesn't lift the limit. She isn't carried, she's partnered, and it's always Harry: she still has to be near him to get away (Q-068).

## Why Harry dances *(settled)*

- **He wants to.** The technique works only with her, because he wants to go somewhere with her: "I wished we could go somewhere together." The rules: [magic](../../setting/magic.md).
- **He's afraid of hurting her.** He isn't over Sectumsempra. The pull gets gentler with every jump. These are his velvet gloves, and they come off later ([harry-dracaena](../../threads/harry-dracaena.md), Q-069).

## Beats

1. **Settled in.** A few days after the raid, at Shell Cottage, they find out she can't Apparate. Ron gloats: at last he's better than Malfoy at something. She kicks him in the shin. Hermione reminds him he still Splinches. Under the teasing Ron is worried, and so is Hermione: they need to come and go as a group, at once.
2. **The lessons.** She says she had no time for them. Ron: because she was busy plotting to kill Dumbledore. This time Hermione kicks him. Dracaena drops her eyes and rubs her forearm through her sleeve.
3. **Hermione's offer.** She offers to train her. She reads Dracaena's face: lessons from a Mudblood, in something a pure-blood should have mastered long ago. Incredulity, despair and shame at once. In B6 Dracaena called her that plenty, to hurt her, and the memory is fresh. Hermione would like some payback for the years of abuse, but knows better, and treats her with kid gloves ([hermione-granger](../../characters/hermione-granger.md)).
4. **The invitation.** Harry has been sitting by Dobby's tomb on the cliff ([malfoy-manor](malfoy-manor.md)). He gets up, goes to her, kneels and asks her to dance. She protests it's hardly the time or the place; his soft look disarms her. "Please?" She accepts, pouting.
5. **The first jumps.** A slow waltz. It's been an eternity since they last danced, at Hogwarts, and she finds her feet in steps she's known since childhood. "Hold on to me." *Swoosh*: the shore. She's furious he didn't warn her. *Swoosh*: the cottage, under her parents' eyes. *Swoosh*: back where they started. The pull gets gentler each time.
6. **The challenge.** She almost leans in, then decides it won't be that easy: he'll have to work for it. "Let's see if you can keep it up." She grips his side and ups the tempo, as she did at the Yule Ball ([yule-ball](../b4/yule-ball.md)). Now he has to keep Apparating them both around the clearing without hurting either of them. She knows how dangerous and hard the game she's sprung on him is. It's what she loves most about him: he never runs away, he always stands up, and he always meets her challenge.
7. **The Malfoys.** From the cliff, Lucius: "Now he's just showing off." Narcissa finds Harry charming. Lucius asks, as a joke, whether he should worry. Not at all: their daughter looks at Granger as if she'd kill her for touching her boy, so she might poison her own mother for it too. He's relieved she has found her bark again: the months as prisoners in their own home were hardest on her. Hermione drags Ron away. Lucius decides he'll have a talk with the boy, soon (Q-067).
   - *(draft)* Fleur dances with Bill beside the Malfoys. It doesn't steal the couple's moment.
8. **The end of the dance.** She tires after a few more minutes, and Harry is secretly grateful: he couldn't have kept it up much longer. By now she's at ease, sliding through space as if it were a dance step, with Harry watching over her. The last jump ends in a dip, their faces almost touching. She stops his kiss with a finger: they've given their audience enough of a show. She pulls herself up against him, trying her best to drag him down. Exhausted, he holds firm: he has decided he'll never let her go again. One last *swoosh* to the cottage.
9. **"Problem solved."** She's smug. Ron: Harry solved it for her. Hermione's elbow. Dracaena takes it in good spirits and goes to her parents, smiling.
10. **How did you do it?** Ron grumbles that she hates them, and that she nearly killed them both with that stunt. Hermione has reservations, but if Harry can manage Apparating her along with them, she's fine with it. Then Ron asks what's been on his mind all along: how did he do it? Hermione doubts many wizards alive could. Harry winks: he wished they could go somewhere together.

## Afterwards *(settled)* · DIV-054

- **No lessons in B7.** She never learns during the book. Every Apparition with her is a dance step, to Ron and Hermione's dismay.
- **The limit.** She has to be near Harry to get away (Q-068).
- **The sound.** On his own, Harry still cracks.
- **After the war** she learns, and still makes him dance her ([dracaena-malfoy](../../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)).
- **The timer.** While they stay, Dracaena brews a batch of her mother's medicine, which Hermione's new sealing spell preserves. Once it's done, they leave to continue the quest. It also turns things between the two girls: Dracaena ends the stay in Hermione's debt ([narcissa-malfoy](../../characters/narcissa-malfoy.md)).

## Must keep

- She can't Apparate, and Ron's teasing hides a real worry: she's a liability.
- Harry asks her to dance on one knee.
- *Swoosh*, not a crack.
- She raises the bar, as at the Yule Ball, and he meets it.
- "I wished we could go somewhere together."
