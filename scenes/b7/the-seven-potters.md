# The Seven Potters

Canon's flight, with Moody's death fought for instead of reported. Mundungus, Hagrid, Lupin and the rest have no files yet; they are themselves.

## Purpose

**It pays two debts at once.** The reader has been owed a demonstration of what Alastor Moody can actually do since he threw Barty Crouch Jr out of his own head in a headmaster's office (DIV-086), and B5's Ministry only half-settles it. And it is the last rung of his arc: **the man who spent a year telling Harry he was a soldier dies getting him carried out like a boy** (DIV-099).

**It also solves a problem rather than making one.** Canon's exit from this chase is the holly wand acting on its own, which is unsettled here (Q-029) and which fed an allegiance chain we have already purged (DIV-024). Moody's stand takes over the saving; the Elder Wand hunt's trigger moves to Q-011.

## Beats

1. **The briefing, and the promise, said flat.** *I'll get you to the destination, Potter, if it's the last thing I do.* **To the room, as the brief** — he is on a different broom and he does not do sentiment. It only becomes a vow in retrospect.
2. **The ambush, as in canon.** Voldemort comes for Moody's pair on canon's own logic: Potter will be riding with the best fighter they have.
3. **Mundungus bolts**, and Moody takes it in stride. **He brought Dung and knew exactly what he was** — he needed bodies, and he made a trade. *The man whose whole doctrine is never to take what you have not checked is abandoned by the one man in the formation he did not bother to check.*
4. **Then he turns and fights, and he is a terror.** He kills Death Eaters. **This is the only time in seven books the reader sees him work with nothing on him** — no curse, no impostor, no trunk.
5. **Harry's Expelliarmus gives him away** and a Death Eater calls it — *it's him!* — **and Moody makes Voldemort deal with him.** Voldemort cannot turn his back on Mad-Eye Moody throwing curses at him.
6. **That window is how Harry and Hagrid get out.** **Harry is still identified**, which the rest of the book needs.
7. **Voldemort kills him**, and he has done exactly what he said he would at the briefing. *o7*
8. **The eye is carried, not used.** Still not recovered; Harry takes it off Umbridge's door as in canon and **keeps it until he can bury it properly.**

## Must keep

- **No artifact.** The eye does not see through anything for anybody. A see-through-walls object answers Gringotts, the snatchers and the Manor before they are asked, and this book runs on *will they be found*.
- **He does not get a speech.** The promise is made before, not during. In the fight he says nothing worth quoting.
- **It is not a rescue he chooses out of love.** It is the brief, executed. That he is also, finally, protecting a child is something the reader sees and he never says.
- **Harry is identified.** Whatever the choreography, the Expelliarmus does its canon job.

## What it pays off

- **The arc, ended.** The prefect party with his ego intact (DIV-098), the apology where it first cracked (DIV-100), the Ministry where he watched what he had done (DIV-099, Q-140), and this. **He keeps making the same mistake and it keeps costing somebody else — and the one time the correction lands, it costs him.**
- **The vigil at the Burrow.** Canon grieves a hard old man who died badly. Ours grieves the man who made Harry what he is, did real harm doing it, and then spent his life keeping a promise — all three at once, with no way to sort them.
- **The graves.** Harry buries Winky, the first elf grave in Britain (DIV-077). Harry buries Dobby (DIV-050). Harry buries Mad-Eye Moody's eye. **He is the one who buries what everybody else leaves lying**, and nobody ever remarks on it.
