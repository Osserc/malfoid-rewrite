# The Only Two of Us

## Purpose

The smallest possible delivery of a large thing: **she has been sharing a room with forty people for three years and has only ever seen one of them.** Harry says it without knowing what he is saying, which is his whole mode (DIV-116, DIV-117), and she understands it instantly, which is hers.

It also does two jobs of plumbing. It plants the wand-tip shield in front of a crowd (Q-144), and it is where her discovery that the technique is unreproducible gets its correction: **she should have seen it at the Duelling Club years ago.**

## Beats

1. **A pause in the session.** The room is drilling in pairs; the two of them are off to one side watching, which is the only time either of them ever stands still.
2. **She is still turning over the DA discovery** — that the pin-shield is not something the school does badly, it is something the school does not do (DIV-125, DIV-109).
3. **Harry, flatly.** *Are you really that shocked they can't do it? I've never seen anyone at the club manage it either.*
4. **The blush.** Three years of Flitwick's piste, every week, full of people failing or declining to copy her, and she never once noticed — **because on that piste there were only ever two people.** He has just told her something about herself that he does not know he has told her, and she cannot pretend not to have heard it.
5. **She covers.** *(open — see below)*
6. **He puts an arm around her waist.** No comment, no ceremony, no indication he thinks anything has happened.
7. **The room has stopped to stare.** She shoos them back to work, imperiously, **and does not disentangle herself.**

## Must keep

- **He names it without knowing what he is naming.** If Harry understands what he has said, the scene is dead.
- **The blush comes before the snark**, not instead of it. She is caught first and only then recovers.
- **The arm is unremarked by both of them.** It is not a gesture; it is where his arm goes now.
- **She shoos them off and does not let go.** The whole character is in those two actions happening at once.

## Open

- **Her line in beat 5.** This is a prime spot and wants the right one. Candidates:
  - ***I wasn't watch—*** and she stops. **The lead candidate**, because it rhymes exactly with Harry in the robe shop two years earlier — *she's not, she's just—* — where he also breaks off one word before the true thing (DIV-119). Both of them stop mid-sentence rather than finish it, which is the series in miniature.
  - *Obviously they can't. That is rather the point of its being mine, Potter.* Haughty, deflects, concedes nothing — safe, and less interesting.
  - *Did I ask for your assessment of the room?* Pure deflection.
- **POV.** Harry's is the lean: from inside her head the blush has to be explained, and from outside the reader gets to do the work.
