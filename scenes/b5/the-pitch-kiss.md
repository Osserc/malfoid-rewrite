# The Pitch Kiss

## Purpose

Canon's version of this scene needs Draco to insult Harry's dead mother, which Dracaena, deep in the dream (DIV-013), would never do. Ours gets to the same place — Harry, George and Fred banned for life — with nobody acting out of character, and takes the joy out of Quidditch for her on the way (DIV-067). It is also where the new rules (DIV-066) first pay off in front of the reader, and the last time she is happy on a broom.

## Beats

1. **The match.** Slytherin lead, but by less than 30, which under DIV-066 makes the Snitch the one thing that can take it off them. Her job is to keep him away from it until the lead is safe.
2. **The duel.** She doesn't. She flies with him rather than against him — matching him, neck and neck, instead of blocking — and loves every second of it.
3. **The steal.** Harry takes it. Gryffindor win by the width of the Snitch. A real triumph, on his own field.
4. **The kiss.** She comes down elated. She barely registers the loss, and what she does feel is a little blue that it's over. She kisses him in front of the whole stadium. The school has known since the Second Task (DIV-038); it has never seen the loser this pleased that the winner won.
5. **Montague.** Her own side read it as her handing the match over. He says it where Harry can hear, loudly, to others rather than to her face: she's gone blood-traitor, like the Weasleys, and worse than that.
6. **The brawl.** Harry gets there first. Naming the Weasleys gives George his own reason, and he takes it. Angelina and Katie hold Fred back.
7. **"Go on, Potter."** Swept up, still on her broom, she cheers him on out loud, and only afterwards works out what she has just done in front of several hundred witnesses.
8. **The ban.** Umbridge bans Harry, George and Fred for life. Fred did nothing. She will not touch a Malfoy, so Dracaena walks away from it untouched, which is its own punishment later.

## Must keep

- She did **not** throw the match (DIV-067). She lost because she wanted the duel to go on, which is a different thing and the one thing she cannot say out loud.
- The trigger is about **her**, never about Harry's mother.
- Fred is banned for doing nothing.
- The kiss is not a reveal. Their relationship is public from B4.
