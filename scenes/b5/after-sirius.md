# After Sirius

## Purpose

The high point of the dream, and the promise that makes DIV-015 so cruel.

## Beats

1. Harry has just lost Sirius.
2. She comforts him.
3. She promises to be there for him, whatever happens.

## Must keep

The comfort and the promise.

## Notes

Canon defaults make this heavy:

- Her aunt Bellatrix killed Sirius, who was her mother's cousin.
- Her father was arrested in the same battle.
- Lucius's failure there is what Voldemort punishes her for with the Mark.

So she comforts Harry across all of that.

How much she knows by then about her father's arrest. Q-022: did anything she told Lucius help set the trap?
