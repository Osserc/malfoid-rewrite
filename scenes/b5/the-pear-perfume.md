# The Pear Perfume

## Purpose

The dream at its height, in its last months (DIV-013). It plants the scent that, in the Hospital Wing, tells the reader she still loves him ([after-sectumsempra](../b6/after-sectumsempra.md)).

## The errand runs *(settled)*

From April 1996 the Inquisitorial Squad exists (canon: it forms right after Dumbledore's flight), Umbridge runs the school, and there are no more official Hogsmeade weekends. Dracaena flexes the Squad's privileges to take Harry out of the castle on "errands" whenever she likes. Officially he's her errand boy, carrying her bags as punishment for his constant misbehaviour. These are their secret Hogsmeade dates, and they fool no one. An echo of B3, when she made him her servant ([potions-servant](../b3/potions-servant.md)).

The April date and the lack of later Hogsmeade weekends come from fan timelines; the books don't state them.

## Beats

1. **The task.** Cresswell & Sons, on one of their "errands". She sets him an arduous task: choose her a perfume. She enjoys watching him squirm in front of the clerk.
2. **Pear.** He picks something fresh and innocent: it just smells nice.
3. **The trap.** She scoffs, for the clerk's benefit: a farm boy's idea of what a lady would like. He plays along. He knows the trap: it's how she fishes for compliments, and how she hands them out.
4. **She takes it.** She never said she disliked it. Leaning on his arm, she says it fits: he'll find her in any crowd, since nobody of her standing would wear it. She buys it.

## Must keep

- **The scent.** Pear, a hint of vanilla, and minor notes Harry can never pin down.
- **Her own brew.** From then on she wears it whenever they're together. Being the potioneer she is, she learns to brew it herself, adds a new note to every batch, and teases Harry when he can't name it.
- **She still wears it in B6.** Harry smells it in the Amortentia ([slughorns-first-lesson](../b6/slughorns-first-lesson.md), DIV-045), and in the Hospital Wing after the breakup ([after-sectumsempra](../b6/after-sectumsempra.md)).
