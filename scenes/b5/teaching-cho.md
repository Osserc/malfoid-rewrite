# Teaching Cho

## Purpose

The B5 Cho runner (Q-134) at its most entertaining, and the one time Dracaena's lurking becomes an intervention. **Cho asks Harry for a lesson and gets one from somebody else.**

It also puts the pin-shield on the page in front of the whole DA, doing what it is for (Q-144), and it is DIV-013's register exactly: she is not jealous, she is *instructing*, and she will say so with a perfectly straight face.

## Beats

1. **Cho asks Harry to show her something**, one to one.
2. **Dracaena materialises and takes his place.** *It boggles the mind that anybody would want Potter, of all people, to teach them a spell.*
3. **She teaches it. Thoroughly.** By casting it at Cho, from across the room, repeatedly.
4. **Everything Cho throws back comes straight off the wand-tip shield** and returns, and it costs Dracaena nothing at all — the technique working as designed, in public, with the whole room watching (DIV-109).
5. **She stops the instant it stops being fun**, pronounces the lesson concluded, and leaves.
6. **Harry thinks she was helping.** Nobody else in the room thinks that.

## Must keep

- **It is deniable, because it is technically a lesson.** She never mentions Cho, never mentions Harry, and never says anything that could be quoted back at her. It is the Inquisitorial Squad voice applied to a teaching room: *someone has to make a proper witch of her* (DIV-013).
- **Harry does not understand.** Not once, not afterwards.
- **Cho is not humiliated into tears.** She is comprehensively outclassed, which is funnier and does not make Dracaena cruel in the register the book can't afford (DIV-070, DIV-071).
