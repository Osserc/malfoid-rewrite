# Madam Malkin's

## Purpose

Their first meeting, and the origin of the Sleeping Beauty motif. It's the only time either of them is seen without a name: not the Malfoy heiress, not the Boy Who Lived. It's also the first time her pattern shows: when she feels exposed, she reaches for snobbery.

## Beats

1. Sunlight on her hair reminds Harry of *Sleeping Beauty*. He once watched the film in secret from behind the living-room door while the Dursleys had it on at night, and Dudley didn't like it. Harry was spellbound.
2. That same day magic turned out to be real, so his only question is whether she's the real princess.
3. She asks who he is. He blurts out whether she's Sleeping Beauty.
4. She's confused, but flattered. She's had plenty of compliments, never one this sincere and awestruck.
5. He looks her in the eye: she doesn't have purple eyes, but hers are pretty too.
6. It sours: she finds out it's a Muggle story.
7. Flustered, she changes the subject and sneers at Hagrid. The damage is done.
8. Harry leaves thinking the "princess" is cruel. Neither has given a name.

## Must keep

The blurted question, the purple eyes line, the Muggle reveal, the Hagrid sneer, no names exchanged.
