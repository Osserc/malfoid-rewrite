# Uncle Severus

## Purpose

Her first real show of skill on the page, and where it comes from. The flashback sets up four things:
- Something is wrong with Narcissa, and it takes a very complex potion to hold it back (DIV-030).
- Snape and Dracaena are bound, and he favours her because she reminds him of Lily (DIV-031).
- Her skill comes from a huge head start, with the Half-Blood Prince himself as her master. That makes her potions oddly like Harry's in B6 (DIV-011).
- She has a special relationship with her mother: she learns potions to make mama well.

The lesson also starts Hermione's hatred of her, because in Potions Hermione is second best.

## Beats

**The lesson** (canon default, rewritten around her):

1. Snape quizzes Harry, who can't answer.
2. Each time, he has Dracaena answer instead, to show off his pupil. She gets the Draught of Living Death (DIV-011).
3. Hermione blurts out the answer to the third question unasked, and loses points. What she says is right by the book but imprecise in practice. She's only read about it, and doesn't connect the dots.
4. "Anything to add, Miss Malfoy?" Dracaena corrects her from experience: she's had years at the cauldron to meet things like this. She wins points for Slytherin, and Hermione is wrecked.

**The flashback** (from the author's sketch):

5. Around 1985, Malfoy Manor. Snape doesn't think of it as a home. He's come to deliver Narcissa's medicine, and Lucius pays him: a rare artifact Snape asked for, plus a pouch of Graphorn horn, immensely expensive because Graphorns are so hard to get near (canon, *Fantastic Beasts*). He knows it by touch, from the ridges.
6. Snape thinks about Lucius: his mentor at his Sorting, then his patron by owl and his client, and among the few he can call a friend (no more young Snape advising Lucius: see [severus-snape](../../characters/severus-snape.md)). "Especially not since" *her*. Unnamed.
7. Dracaena, about five, peeks out and asks if he's the one who makes mama's medicine. She runs up and hugs his legs: mama always feels better when she drinks it.
8. She asks if she could make it herself. Lucius says it's far too complex, and Snape is about the only one he'd trust with it. Snape nearly refuses, then sees in her the eager curiosity *she* used to show. He kneels to her level and offers to set her on the path. Whether she succeeds is up to her.
9. She'll brew plenty, so mama never runs out and stays well forever.
10. He'll send her one of his old potions books. If she gets through it, he'll consider more instruction. "Thank you, uncle Severus!"
11. Lucius teases him with the name. Snape laughs for the first time in a long while, and finds himself looking forward to a pupil.

## Must keep

- "Mama's medicine," and why she wants to learn: so mama never runs out.
- The hug. Snape kneeling to her level.
- "Uncle Severus."
- Lily is never named: only "her".
- Hermione loses points, then Dracaena outdoes her.

## Open

- Where the flashback sits in the lesson. Candidate (Claude): it's triggered by what she adds at the third question. Canon's third question is about monkshood and wolfsbane.
- What exactly Hermione gets imprecise, and how Dracaena corrects her. Candidate (Claude): she adds that aconite goes into the Wolfsbane Potion, which holds a werewolf's curse in check without curing it, just as mama's medicine does. Snape hears his own teaching in her answer and remembers the Manor. It also seeds Lupin in B3. Aconite in the Wolfsbane Potion is our own inference from the name.
- Harry's canon "ask Hermione" cheek: keep or cut.
