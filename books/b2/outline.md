## Decided beats

🔒 locked; everything else here is settled.

- Flourish & Blotts: Harry comes out of Knockturn Alley filthy and Hermione throws herself at him and checks him over. Molly Weasley reads it as a girlfriend and is delighted — the first instance of the fake triangle, and she holds the position for three years. DIV-118
- Flourish & Blotts: Dracaena calls Hermione his Mudblood girlfriend, Hermione charges her, **Harry grabs Dracaena** and is knocked down, and Lucius squares up to Arthur over the children. **Narcissa breaks it up** with a joke about her marital duties, and takes her first look at Harry while he is on the floor without his glasses. Hagrid's canon intervention is cut. DIV-120, [flourish-and-blotts](../../scenes/b2/flourish-and-blotts.md)
- After Lockhart's disastrous session, Flitwick takes over the Duelling Club. Harry and Dracaena become regular, evenly matched opponents. DIV-028, [duelling-club](../../threads/duelling-club.md)
- Dracaena drags Pansy to the Duelling Club. Pansy lasts two or three sessions and tells her she isn't coming back — the first preference she has ever stated out loud. Dracaena takes it perfectly well, and never asks what she means to do instead. DIV-121, DIV-122
- Looking for something of her own, Pansy works through the clubs canon never puts on the page and finds Ron Weasley at the chess club, winning. She asks whether she can watch; he gives her pointers; by the end of the year she is learning the game off him by owl. **Nothing romantic is in it.** DIV-121, [ron-pansy](../../threads/ron-pansy.md)
- The sock. Canon's trick stands; freeing Dobby starts to kill him, he begs Harry to keep him, and Harry accepts as a friend rather than a master. Nobody witnesses the only working remedy in a thousand years. DIV-076, DIV-075, [the-sock](../../scenes/b2/the-sock.md)
- Lucius discards the sock carelessly and Dobby catches it; he knew what it meant, does not raise a hand to Harry, and leaves him a warning he can't read. The Manor goes cold from that night and he never connects the two. DIV-076, [house-elves](../../setting/house-elves.md)
- Dobby goes to the Burrow — lodging, not a house of his own, since he is anchored to Harry. He has Harry's vault, and starts building the Weasleys improvements they can't refuse. DIV-076
- Harry asks why a duellist doesn't glue his wand to his hand. Flitwick demonstrates instead of answering: Dracaena Disarms a glued Harry and dislocates his shoulder. She goes to the infirmary with him, pokes the arm, and suggests doing it again sometime. DIV-127, [the-glue-demonstration](../../scenes/b2/the-glue-demonstration.md)
- **The Chamber, together.** Dracaena is down there; they fail to fight the basilisk; Harry gets the sword in and cannot finish it one-handed. He reads Expelliarmus in her wand's draw, glues the sword to his hand, and her Disarming drags the blade through the basilisk's head — through his shoulder to get there. Fawkes answers the venom, Pomfrey the arm; they stab the diary together and she hauls him out. **He does not survive the night without her.** DIV-129, DIV-127, [the-chamber](../../scenes/b2/the-chamber.md)
- For Dracaena, Potter has just killed her family's elf — in the same week she helped him kill a basilisk. The best and the worst thing he has ever done to her, in that order. Q-098, Q-101, Q-164, Q-165
