## Decided beats

🔒 locked, *(draft)* draft; everything else here is settled.

- Dracaena milks the injury for weeks, delays the Gryffindor match to practise, and makes Harry her Potions servant. The sling keeps her out of her class's Boggart lesson; she faces hers later, alone with Lupin, and freezes ([remedial-boggart](../../scenes/b3/remedial-boggart.md)). She takes no part in the fake-Dementor prank: Crabbe and Goyle pull it on their own, and she disapproves. DIV-017, [potions-servant](../../scenes/b3/potions-servant.md)
- Days before the final, she jeers at Hagrid over Buckbeak's sentence to keep up appearances, almost in tears, baits Hermione into slapping her and takes it. Harry sees her face. DIV-069, [the-slap](../../scenes/b3/the-slap.md)
- The Quidditch final: Flint fouls to hold Gryffindor under the Cup margin, she's the only Slytherin playing clean, the Seeker duel runs the length of the match, and she loses fairly — to a move she'd have thought beneath her. Her house turns on her over the course of it, and Harry gets one close look at what her anger is like aimed at someone else. Her team desert her; Harry comes back and offers her his hand, the gesture she offered on the train. DIV-059, DIV-066, DIV-068, DIV-069, DIV-070, DIV-071, [the-quidditch-final](../../scenes/b3/the-quidditch-final.md)
- 🔒 Dracaena saves Buckbeak and gets him to Sirius, who escapes. DIV-003, [buckbeak-rescue](../../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)
- Dracaena spies on Harry's Patronus lessons and studies the charm for him. The lap-pillow scene becomes his Patronus memory, though he won't count it as happy until the lake. DIV-018, DIV-027, [lap-pillow](../../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)
- Her fake lead sends the Dementors to the lake. She can't cast the Patronus, and confesses; Harry re-evaluates her, and his Patronus saves everyone with a first flicker of resonance; Hermione's idea; Sirius recognises her. DIV-018, DIV-021
- 🔒 No Time-Turner: the climax has to be rebuilt. DIV-002, Q-003
- *(draft)* Hermione drops Muggle Studies, and Dracaena calls it conceding defeat. DIV-012
- *(draft)* Dobby tells Harry, at length and at speed, about the first meal he has ever eaten: Molly's cooking at the Burrow over the summer, cooked by hand, for him. Harry cannot work out why it matters so much. Out of the yoke and fed, Dobby looks better than he ever has, and Harry asks whether he has got taller — which is the first sign he is dying. DIV-076, DIV-050, Q-102
- *(draft)* Dracaena finds Dobby alive at Hogwarts, which by everything she knows is impossible, and he tells her he is glad Harry freed him because her father was a horrible master. She cannot take it, and ruminates on it for months. It is the first half of the case against her father; Buckbeak is the second. Q-098, Q-101, DIV-076
