## Decided beats

🔒 locked, *(draft)* draft; everything else here is settled.

- Harry's birthday at Privet Drive: he wrestles the Monster Book shut and thinks of his rival, fondly, alone, without noticing that it is fondly. Two years to the day since Madam Malkin's, and nobody points at it. Q-155, DIV-120, DIV-009
- The hippogriff lesson, before Buckbeak: Hagrid says to stroke the spine gently, Harry is already primed, and has a private laugh at Dracaena as a grumpy cat — a white one over Crookshanks, who is already on the page. Two lines, interior. A joke, not a motif: her animal is the dragon. The laugh curdles within minutes. Q-155, DIV-120, DIV-017, DIV-027
- Diagon Alley before term: Harry forgot robes, so the three of them go to Madam Malkin's and meet Lindsay Madison again. She ribs him about his young lady, Hermione asks sincerely why everyone keeps saying that, and Lindsay asks after the princess. Harry vents in forensic detail and then won't let her agree with him. DIV-119, DIV-117, DIV-118, [the-robe-shop](../../scenes/b3/the-robe-shop.md)
- Dracaena milks the injury for weeks, delays the Gryffindor match to practise, and makes Harry her Potions servant. The sling keeps her out of her class's Boggart lesson; she faces hers later, alone with Lupin, and it is Harry, telling her he wishes he'd never met her. She freezes, understands none of it, and tells no one ([remedial-boggart](../../scenes/b3/remedial-boggart.md)). She had been doing the Dementor impression at Harry herself that autumn; afterwards she stops, and she takes no part in the fake-Dementor prank — Crabbe and Goyle pull it on their own, and she disapproves. DIV-017, DIV-070, [potions-servant](../../scenes/b3/potions-servant.md)
- The Defence exam: she goes through Lupin's obstacle course admirably and then never comes out of the Boggart trunk, in front of her whole year. Lupin takes her out and hands her to Pansy without a word; Daphne gets her first deniable ammunition and spends it on the queue; Ron gloats, Hermione digs at Harry rather than at her, and Harry can't place any of it. We never see inside the trunk. DIV-017, DIV-093, DIV-094, [the-trunk](../../scenes/b3/the-trunk.md)
- Days before the final, she jeers at Hagrid over Buckbeak's sentence to keep up appearances, almost in tears, baits Hermione into slapping her and takes it. Harry sees her face. DIV-069, [the-slap](../../scenes/b3/the-slap.md)
- The Quidditch final: Flint fouls to hold Gryffindor under the Cup margin, she's the only Slytherin playing clean, the Seeker duel runs the length of the match, and she loses fairly — to a move she'd have thought beneath her. Her house turns on her over the course of it, and Harry gets one close look at what her anger is like aimed at someone else. Her team desert her; Harry comes back and offers her his hand, the gesture she offered on the train. DIV-059, DIV-066, DIV-068, DIV-069, DIV-070, DIV-071, [the-quidditch-final](../../scenes/b3/the-quidditch-final.md)
- 🔒 Dracaena saves Buckbeak and gets him to Sirius, who escapes. DIV-003, [buckbeak-rescue](../../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)
- Dracaena spies on Harry's Patronus lessons and studies the charm for him. The lap-pillow scene becomes his Patronus memory, though he won't count it as happy until the lake. DIV-018, DIV-027, [lap-pillow](../../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)
- Her fake lead sends the Dementors to the lake. She can't cast the Patronus, and confesses; Harry re-evaluates her, and his Patronus saves everyone with a first flicker of resonance; Hermione's idea; Sirius recognises her. DIV-018, DIV-021
- 🔒 No Time-Turner: the climax has to be rebuilt. DIV-002, Q-003
- The Dementor garrison is on the grounds all year, over McGonagall's loud objection and Dumbledore's acquiescence. In August she gives Flitwick the urn of ashes she has kept since the war and he distils it into a Lustrum enhancer — the first she has ever had — and she wears it; the castle sees a new necklace and nothing more, and Hermione can find nothing on it. When the Dementors swarm the Quidditch match she could end the garrison where she stands, and scorch fourteen children off their brooms and burn whoever in the crowd has the worst hands with it, so she doesn't — and leaves scorch marks on the railing. At the Three Broomsticks in December, Hermione overhears her tell Flitwick to hold on to it because she doesn't trust herself with it. It is the table where Harry learns about Sirius, so nobody has attention to spare. DIV-079, DIV-080
- Harry's Patronus at the lake doesn't just drive the swarm off, it maims it. Wounded and starving, the horde storms the castle. The staff hold the walls, and McGonagall breaks the siege in her battle garb, laughing — the night the school learns what "Hellfire" meant. The fight is short, Harry and Dracaena have no POV on it, and the aftermath is the cover for her flight to Sirius. DIV-079, DIV-080, DIV-081, [the-laughing-witch](../../scenes/b3/the-laughing-witch.md)
- Fudge loses the prisoner, the hippogriff and his garrison in one night, and answers with a hearing. Where he, Macnair, Dumbledore and Snape are through it: Q-003
- Balthier Thynne and Nola Laurel, fifth-year Slytherins, come on the page: he tells Harry not to be ashamed of fainting, and he watches the siege with the fire that killed his father in his head. DIV-082, [house-unity](../../threads/house-unity.md)
- *(draft)* Hermione drops Muggle Studies, and Dracaena calls it conceding defeat. DIV-012
- *(draft)* Dobby tells Harry, at length and at speed, about the first meal he has ever eaten: Molly's cooking at the Burrow over the summer, cooked by hand, for him. Harry cannot work out why it matters so much. Out of the yoke and fed, Dobby looks better than he ever has, and Harry asks whether he has got taller — which is the first sign he is dying. DIV-076, DIV-050, Q-102
- *(draft)* Dracaena finds Dobby alive at Hogwarts, which by everything she knows is impossible, and he tells her he is glad Harry freed him because her father was a horrible master. She cannot take it, and ruminates on it for months. It is the first half of the case against her father; Buckbeak is the second. Q-098, Q-101, DIV-076
