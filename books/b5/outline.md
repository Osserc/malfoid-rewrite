## Decided beats

🔒 locked, *(draft)* draft; everything else here is settled.

- The summer before: Snape teaches Dracaena Occlumency, and sees her memories of Harry. DIV-020, DIV-031, Q-043
- Dracaena is dreaming: happy with Harry, "keeping tabs on him" (Lucius's strategy), with the Inquisitorial Squad as cover. DIV-013
- From April, when the Squad forms (canon), she takes Harry to Hogsmeade on Squad "errands": their dates, run as Squad business. He picks her perfume. DIV-013, [the-pear-perfume](../../scenes/b5/the-pear-perfume.md)
- Lucius tells Voldemort he has planted his daughter as a spy. DIV-020
- Before term: Umbridge drives the Dementors to Little Whinging and holds them there herself, using the same *Arceo* every senior official is issued and simply meaning it the other way. Fudge's insistence at the hearing that they cannot leave Ministry control is wrong rather than dishonest — nobody has ever held them. DIV-084, DIV-085
- The prefect party at Grimmauld: Sirius tries to slip Harry a Firewhisky, Moody butts in fondly about the taste he gave him last year, and Molly goes off. The Order photograph is aimed at the room instead of at Harry — Moody in a nostalgic mood, naming how each of them was taken apart — and Harry is the one who leaves. Lupin still dispatches the Boggart. DIV-098, [the-prefect-party](../../scenes/b5/the-prefect-party.md)
- Moody is on Sirius's side of the argument about how much Harry is told, because he made a soldier of the boy and it is the one thing he thinks he did right. Lupin is the voice of reason, they are overruled, and neither man is actually looking at Harry. The bill arrives at the Ministry. DIV-099, Q-140
- Moody apologises for the ferret: *I failed you both*, professional shame and not the Imperius. Harry offers him the excuse — Lucius Malfoy's own defence — and he declines it. Then he asks Harry to pass it to her. **Timing held in both directions; the author's lean is before term.** DIV-100, Q-139
- At the Department of Mysteries Moody is a terror, as canon never lets him be — and it changes nothing. Sirius dies a few feet away, and he works out what his own argument has cost. DIV-099, Q-140
- Early on, Umbridge shuts down the Duelling Club, one of her first acts. DIV-028, [duelling-club](../../threads/duelling-club.md)
- Running all year, in front of everybody: nothing about Harry and Dracaena is secret from the school, and she does not pretend otherwise. They quarrel constantly, she docks him points for invented infractions, and she tells anyone who asks that she is disciplining him and will make a proper wizard out of Potter yet. It vexes Umbridge and nobody can lay a finger on it. DIV-013, [sleeping-beauty](../../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)
- Also running all year, for laughs: Cho keeps seeking Harry out — the train, the Owlery, canon's beats kept — and Dracaena is lurking behind every attempt. The school works it out; Harry never does, and spends the encounters wishing she would come and extract him. DIV-041, Q-134
- *(open)* Her office, late in the year: Umbridge boasts of sending the Dementors with the Inquisitorial Squad in the room, and canon puts Dracaena in that room. What she does with it: Q-109
- The first match: Harry steals it off her lead and she kisses him on the pitch anyway. Montague calls her a blood-traitor, Harry and George beat him for it, and Umbridge bans them and Fred for life. DIV-066, DIV-067, [the-pitch-kiss](../../scenes/b5/the-pitch-kiss.md)
- She plays the rest of the season angry, with Harry in the stands. Gryffindor win the final and Ron comes good, but Slytherin take the Cup on aggregate: her first Cup, won without ever beating him. DIV-066, DIV-067, [quidditch](../../threads/quidditch.md)
- *(draft)* Some Slytherins join the DA, one of them a friend of Dracaena's. DIV-055, [house-unity](../../threads/house-unity.md)
- After Sirius dies, Dumbledore tells Harry to treasure the love he has left, and not only his friends'. DIV-046
- Hermione tracks the elf conspiracy across the year: elves enter the record already in service, with no origin, no habitat and no young, and she finds the shape of the cover-up without ever finding the deed. The history, and no remedy. DIV-075, [house-elves](../../setting/house-elves.md)
- Harry asks Dobby about Mrs Black's portrait. Dobby does not think he can touch it, tries anyway out of habit, and finds he can — because he is a glitch and Grimmauld's defences are written against elves that belong somewhere. He has to wade through Ur-Kreacher to do it, it costs him days, and he gets the painting off the wall. Kreacher's first appearance is a scream of *TRESPASSER!*, which is correct. DIV-078, DIV-075, [kreacher-and-the-manor](../../scenes/b7/kreacher-and-the-manor.md)
- The feud begins. Kreacher can see Dobby is dying and says so as an insult, and nobody listens. Hermione has her first hard datum that the curse has seams, years before she can use it. DIV-078, Q-100
- After Sirius dies, she comforts Harry and promises to be there for him. DIV-014, [after-sirius](../../scenes/b5/after-sirius.md)
- After the book: she is Marked, Voldemort shatters her walls, and her faith dies. DIV-015
