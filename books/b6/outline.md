## Decided beats

🔒 locked; everything else here is settled.

- Dracaena is back in the cage; her promise is broken. DIV-015
- Start of term: the hollow weeks. She's distant, only physical with Harry, won't talk, keeps disappearing, and quits the Quidditch team. DIV-015
- First Potions lesson: Harry smells her perfume in the Amortentia, and she knows the potion but doesn't raise her hand. He beats her at the Draught of Living Death using the Prince's notes. She and Hermione both notice his tricks resemble her technique. DIV-011, DIV-045, Q-042, [slughorns-first-lesson](../../scenes/b6/slughorns-first-lesson.md)
- A few weeks in, Flitwick reopens the Duelling Club. Harry offers it as a date; she refuses, and their fight ends in the breakup, brutal enough to lead in the end to Sectumsempra. DIV-015, DIV-028, Q-031, [the-breakup](../../scenes/b6/the-breakup.md)
- She can no longer cast a Patronus. DIV-027
- Sectumsempra, in front of the Mirror of Erised, where she sees the Yule Ball and he, for a second, their wedding. The Mirror breaks in the fight. In his arms, bleeding, she asks whether he hates her this much. Snape saves her too late to prevent the scars, which she bears for life, and buries the incident. DIV-032, DIV-043, [sectumsempra](../../scenes/b6/sectumsempra.md)
- That night Harry follows her to the Hospital Wing and attacks Crabbe and Goyle when they bar his way. He screams at Snape, and gets detention. He's banned from Quidditch, one way or another, and eventually tells his friends. Q-057
- Four days later Harry slips into the Hospital Wing. She casts him as the Dark Lord's like, and his last hope dies. [after-sectumsempra](../../scenes/b6/after-sectumsempra.md)
- On the night of the cave, on the walk to Hogsmeade, Dumbledore says something cryptic to Harry about her. DIV-044, [dumbledores-counsel](../../scenes/b6/dumbledores-counsel.md)
