## Decided beats

- At the World Cup the veela don't touch Harry: he's already in love without knowing it. DIV-037
- Harry never asks Cho, and Cho is nothing to anybody. Dracaena saw the veela leave him cold and has decided the girl is Hermione, who is always next to him. DIV-041
- It is the real Moody all year, under the Imperius, with Crouch Jr asleep in the trunk holding him down. His teaching is genuinely his, which is why Harry's help was real and why Voldemort needed nobody to fake it. DIV-086, [alastor-moody](../../characters/alastor-moody.md)
- Dracaena draws on Hermione in a corridor and Crouch takes Moody over to beat her half to death down it as a ferret: his one unforced act of the year, and the reader's best clue. **It is an assault, not a prank** — she comes back bleeding and in shock — **and McGonagall draws her wand on an Auror** to stop it. Harry carries her to the hospital wing unasked, furious with her the whole way, and she will not let go of him. Once she is mended he tells her what she did was hideous. She answers nothing and carries it to the Ball. DIV-095, DIV-086, DIV-079, DIV-074, Q-117, Q-135
- Days after the lake she raises Moody at last, and Harry answers with the corridor rather than with an argument: he was furious with her when he picked her up, and did it anyway. Moody himself stays unresolved until B5. DIV-104, [which-one-is-real](../../scenes/b4/which-one-is-real.md)
- After the First Task, Moody debriefs Harry like a new Auror and pours him a drink out of the famous flask. It is whisky, Harry is unchanged, and then *CONSTANT VIGILANCE* — the offer sincere and a test at once. It spends canon's loudest clue, plants the word *check* for the Butterbeer, and teaches Harry that he is safe with Moody, which is true and is not. DIV-097, DIV-096, [the-flask](../../scenes/b4/the-flask.md)
- Moody builds him in four rungs across the year, not one scene: the drink in November, the flask thrown back at the lake in February, the stamp on the Conjunctivitis Curse in the spring where the doctrine lands, and nothing at all before the maze. He calls him *junior Auror* throughout. DIV-102, DIV-096
- Moody and Sirius reach the same verdict on Dracaena independently — the death of him or his greatest asset; the jackpot or an early grave, and worth it either way. Harry takes Sirius's. DIV-101, DIV-019
- The Duelling Club stays open, and the visiting students join. Harry beats a Durmstrang student who has taken an interest in Dracaena, too aggressively, and Rita Skeeter gets her ammunition. DIV-028, DIV-029, [durmstrang-challenger](../../scenes/b4/durmstrang-challenger.md)
- The Goblet binds Harry, and the binding has teeth: refusing costs him his magic, which means Privet Drive for good and never seeing her again. Dumbledore tells him so plainly in November, along with what the prize really is, and cannot spring the trap because the one counter is what the Goblet forbids. DIV-087, [triwizard](../../setting/triwizard.md)
- Sirius plants the idea of asking Dracaena to the Ball. DIV-019
- Before the Ball, Dracaena's owls home tip off Lucius, and Narcissa takes Harry's side. DIV-040, [the-green-dress](../../scenes/b4/the-green-dress.md)
- 🔒 Harry asks Dracaena to the Yule Ball and she accepts: the turning point. DIV-004, DIV-013, [yule-ball](../../scenes/b4/yule-ball.md)
- Their first kiss is at the Ball. Right after it, Rita Skeeter prints that they went together. DIV-040
- After the Ball, its memory gives Dracaena her first corporeal Patronus. DIV-027
- 🔒 Dracaena is Harry's Second Task hostage, and the dream begins. DIV-005, DIV-013, [second-task](../../scenes/b4/second-task.md)
- On the eve of the Second Task, Dumbledore has a word with her. DIV-046
- Harry goes down on a gillyweed extract Dracaena brewed. At the lake she claims him and kisses him in front of the school. DIV-038, DIV-039, [second-task](../../scenes/b4/second-task.md)
- Fleur tests Harry's heart with her charm, then hands Dracaena a public win. DIV-037, DIV-038, [the-little-basilisk](../../scenes/b4/the-little-basilisk.md)
- Krum joins the Duelling Club because he is curious about Harry, and at the weighing of the wands tells him why he ended a match Bulgaria was losing: the captain called it, and he agreed. DIV-066, DIV-090
- Hooch calls a special flying lesson for the first years, with a world-class player in the castle. An exhibition, not a contest; nobody duels. DIV-091, [the-flying-lesson](../../scenes/b4/the-flying-lesson.md)
- The Third Task is a fight. Cedric beats Fleur, Harry beats Krum, and Harry beats Cedric in front of the Cup, wearing her ribbon, in front of the whole school. DIV-089, [the-third-task](../../scenes/b4/the-third-task.md)
- Beaten, Cedric Summons the Cup; Harry takes it out of the air; Cedric catches his leg. Touch is not claim, so he arrives as the spare over an inch. DIV-089
- In the graveyard the charge lands in Harry as the victory resolves and is drawn out of him for the ritual: the strongest he will ever be, arriving while he is bound to a headstone and useless. DIV-087
- The Diggorys visit. Harry gives them closure, with Dracaena running the room; then she answers his guilt without telling him he did nothing wrong: "I'm glad it was you who made it out alive." DIV-092, [the-diggorys](../../scenes/b4/the-diggorys.md)
- He decides he is a calamity, on a premise that is almost certainly false — the real final was Harry and Krum, and Cedric was never the likeliest winner. DIV-092, DIV-089
- Moody throws Crouch out in Dumbledore's office when Crouch rides him too hot, and that is what saves Harry. Fudge takes Crouch into custody and has him Kissed days later, destroying the only witness who could corroborate Harry. DIV-086

- The hospital wing: Sirius shoves her to the bedside, Dumbledore shelters her, and she guards Harry all night. "Sleep well, my prince." DIV-058, [the-hospital-wing](../../scenes/b4/the-hospital-wing.md)
- After the Third Task, in the hospital wing, Harry keeps Lucius's name from Fudge, with Dracaena there. She defends him to the Minister's face, and it reaches her father. DIV-047, DIV-058, [the-missing-name](../../scenes/b4/the-missing-name.md)
- The last nights of term: Harry's night terrors, her failed potion, the Malfoy room and her lap. He finds her hoard. DIV-059, DIV-060, DIV-061, [the-malfoy-room](../../scenes/b4/the-malfoy-room.md)
- The train home: the winnings dressed as an investment, her idea (DIV-062); the second rift with Ron and Hermione (DIV-063); Lucius finds them asleep and declines to see it. [the-train-home](../../scenes/b4/the-train-home.md)
- End of the year: Dumbledore, who noticed the missing name and welcomes it, tells Harry she's in a very tough spot, with her father a Death Eater. DIV-046, DIV-047

- Over the summer she writes to him, and Dobby carries the letters. DIV-065
