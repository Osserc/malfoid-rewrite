# Apparate dancing

"You can't be serious! Am I finally better at something than *Malfoy*?!"

"Oh sod off, Weasel."

"Ron, should I remind you that you still Splinch on the regular?"

"Yeah, but at least I *can* Apparate! And she- OUCH!"

Ron's gloating was squarely interrupted by a vigorous kick on the shin by the target of his lampoons, who was in no mood for jokes. Not even Hermione had it in her to stand up for Ron, as she saw Dracaena's expression as he subjected her to his barbs. Ron could be so insensitive sometimes... yet she couldn't help but worry that this was yet another complication that the girl's presence created. They needed to be able to come and go as a group instantly, and Dracaena's inability to Apparate would be a huge problem. She shot a look at Harry, who was sitting by Dobby's tomb on the cliff. When was he going to intervene?

"I didn't have the time for those stupid lessons..."

"Yeah, because you were too busy plotting to kill Dum-"

"RON!"

This time the kick came from Hermione, as Dracaena lowered her gaze to the ground and started rubbing her forearm through the sleeve of her light blue shirt. As much as she would have liked to get some payback for years of Malfoy's constant abuse, she knew better. Harry would not have stood for that, not after he had got her out of her gilded prison, at the price of Dobby's life.

"Listen, Malfoy, we need to find a solution. I can help you train, if you want?"

The look in Dracaena's eyes at the prospect of her, a pureblood witch, having to take *lessons* from a Mudblood on something she was supposed to be a master of by now was a mixture of incredulity, despair and shame. Thankfully Harry stood up before either of them could say something they would have regretted later, and made his way towards Dracaena. At the sound of his clothes rustling as he moved, she spun around, a timid smile on her face. As he stood before her, he dropped to one knee and asked, "Would you dance with me, Dracaena?"

"P-Potter, this is hardly the time or the place for-" she started, but Harry's soft expression disarmed her. She couldn't say no to those stupid, beautiful, kind green eyes...

"Please?"

"F-fine, if you insist..." she pouted, and held out her hand for him to take.

With one fluid motion he rose, took her hand in his, and gently placed his other on her hip. It seemed like an eternity since they had last danced together, happily, within the confines of Hogwarts. As they began a slow waltz, Dracaena started to regain her confidence. These were moves she had practised since childhood, and being in the arms of her prince brought her a great deal of comfort.

"Hold on to me," he said, his loving eyes looking straight at her.

*Swoosh*. Suddenly, she felt the ground go missing beneath her feet. It was just an instant, but she felt her whole body being *pulled*, decisively yet not violently, and then the world reappeared. Gone was the forest behind Harry, replaced by the sight of the waves ceaselessly caressing the shore. She felt her knees go weak, but Harry held her steady, and kept dancing.

"What was that, Potter?!"

"We Apparated."

"WHAT?! You could have told me!"

"We're doing it again now."

"You bas-"

Another *shwoosh*, and the pull got a little gentler. Like someone had made her slide abruptly across the ground, like an overeager dance partner. This time she was greeted by Shell Cottage, and the figures of her parents standing watch from afar, observing the scene.

"Do not do that again!"

"I think I'm getting the hang of this, actually."

"Potter, I swear to-"

*Shwoosh*. A delicate yet firm pull, and the scenery changed again. Ron was shaking his head with an exasperated smile, while Hermione was staring at them dancing with her mouth agape.

"That wasn't so bad now, was it?"

He was smiling warmly at her, with the same infuriatingly kind expression he wore when he knew he had managed to sneak his way into her heart after dodging her barbs, and he was sitting contentedly in her most guarded place and she couldn't help but lean in and-

*It's not going to be that easy, you'll have to work for it.*

"Feeling confident, are we? Let's see if you can *keep it up* then."

And just as she had done all those years ago, she edged closer to him, gripped his side and upped the tempo. Last time, it had taken all his concentration and trained Quidditch reflexes to avoid stomping on her feet as she almost dragged him across the Great Hall. And this time he had to keep Apparating both of them around the clearing, without hurting either. She might not know how to perform the magic she required of him, but she was aware of how dangerous and tough the game she sprang on him was. But it was one of the things she loved most about him.

He never ran away. He always stood up. And he always met her challenge.

One, two, three, four, *tap* and *shwoosh*, now they were on the cliff; one, two, three, four, *tap* and *shwoosh* and they were on the shore.

Not that either of them knew that: both were gazing into each other's eyes, Dracaena with the determination of wanting to see her partner pushed to his limits and exceed her expectations, and Harry with the confidence he would meet them and show her he would never let her down again.

__________________

Far above them, Lucius Malfoy was looking at his daughter and Harry, trying not to smile.

"Now he's just showing off," he drawled, doing his best to appear bored and mildly upset, as usual.

"I find him a very charming boy," his wife offered, leaning on his shoulder.

With a tilt of his head, he asked her jokingly, "Should I worry?"

"I think your daughter might poison me if I so much as touch him. Did you see how she looks at that Muggle-born?" Then she looked up at him, and added, "You're fine. For now."

Lucius chuckled, relieved that his wife had found her bark back. The past months they had spent as prisoners in their own home had been very tough on all of them, Narcissa having borne the worst of it.

"Looks like she finally took the hint."

They saw Granger drag the Weasley boy away, and then brought their eyes back to the dancers in the field. And Lucius decided he'd have a talk with the boy, soon.

__________________

Despite her wishes, Dracaena had to start winding down after a couple more minutes. Her exhaustion was starting to mount, and Harry was secretly grateful for her lack of exercise; he wasn't sure he'd be able to keep up his performance much longer. By this point, Dracaena felt perfectly at ease letting Harry Apparate them around, as it felt like she was simply sliding through space in a particularly fluid dance step, with Harry as her watchful guardian.

Their dance finally came to a close with one last Apparition, with Dracaena letting herself fall backwards and Harry catching her, their faces almost touching each other's. His lips parted, but before he could lean in further Dracaena pressed a finger to them, and gestured towards their audience.

"I think we have given them enough of a show for now," she said coyly, and started lifting herself up by pulling against him.

Despite the exhaustion, Harry braced himself and allowed Dracaena to use his weight to regain her footing. The effort nearly brought him to his knees, as Dracaena was trying her best to drag him down. But he decided he would never let her go again, and so he stood firm.

Harry grinned at her, still holding her hand, and then one last *shwoosh* and they were in front of the cottage.

Dracaena regarded Ron and Hermione with a smug look, and proudly proclaimed, "I think we've solved this particular problem, haven't we?"

"You mean Harry solved it for you. Must be nice hav- OW!"

Hermione elbowed him in the ribs before he could finish, but to her surprise Dracaena took it in good spirit, and left to join her parents with a heartfelt smile.

"Why do you coddle her so much, Hermione? She hates us!"

"Thank you for doing that, Hermione," said Harry with a guilty smile. "I know she can be a handful."

"Yeah, Harry, we noticed. If I didn't know better, I'd say she was trying to kill you both with that stunt."

"While I wouldn't put it like that, Ron, I do have some... reservations. But if you think you can handle Apparating her with us, I'm fine with it."

Ron threw up his hands with a "bah", like a person who knew he had lost the argument. But then he turned to Harry, and asked him the one question that had been on his mind during the entire performance.

"How did you do it?"

"Do what?"

"Apparating like that. It's supposed to be extremely difficult, and you did it while dancing!"

Hermione suddenly perked up, visibly interested. "Yeah, I don't think there are many wizards alive who could do that! What's the trick?"

With a slow wink, he candidly said, "I wished we could go somewhere together."
