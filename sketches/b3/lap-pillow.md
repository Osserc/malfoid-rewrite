# A happy memory

"Well well well, if it isn't Potter!"

Harry groaned and fell deeper against the bench he was sitting on. He'd recognize the owner of that haughty yet silky voice anywhere, even among a crowd of people chattering away. He would probably never get used to hearing it carry sneering remarks at his expense every time they crossed paths. Exhausted and munching on the still twitching remains of a Chocolate Frog, Harry mumbled a weak "what do you want, Malfoy?"

The young girl gracefully vaulted over the bench and took a seat a short distance away from him, fixating her silvery eyes on his face, as she was wont to do. An evil smile crept on her face as she spotted a smudge of chocolate on the corner of the boy's lips.

"Did you mother not teach you any manners, Potter?"

There it was. Any hope that Harry had of having some time to unwind after yet another failed attempt at driving the Boggart-Dementor away was dashed by the girl who had been nothing but a thorn in his side ever since, remindinh him of the creature's favorite torture implement.

"My parents are dead", he replied flatly, turning his gaze away from Dracaena's. Had he lingered a bit more, he might have caught the Slytherin girl's mouth briefly turn into a tiny "oh", of surprise and regret, before her owner regained her usual arrogance. And most importantly, he would have noticed her quickly withdrawing a hand holding a richly decorated green handkerchief, reaching for his stained lips.

"A-ahem... having trouble with the Patronus Charm, I take it, judging by how you're scarfing that thing down."

Harry's neck snapped towards her. How did she even know what he and Lupin were up to? He wasn't exactly hiding that he was receiving "special lessons", but he did not expect anyone to actually find out. Who would hang around the History of Magic classroom at this time of the day? Well, Malfoy apparently. Or, more realistically, either one of her goons, Crabbe and Goyle.

"So what? Are you here to taunt me? To rub it in my face that I can't even chase away a Boggart pretending he's a Dementor?"

A delighted giggle came from Dracaena's throat, relishing in Harry's dismay.

"I'm actually here for another reason. I read up on the Patronus, and I know you need a strong, happy memory to conjure it properly."

He didn't even need to hear the rest of her drivel to know what she was about to say, he heard it for years from Dudley. He knew it was coming, and braced himself, but he wasn't sure he'd be able to take it. Not after experiencing the Dementor's evil magic such a short while ago.

"That must mean you don't have any!", she declared triumphantly, "how does it feel Potter, I wonder, knowing you spent so many years without anything to smile about? Knowing that nobody wants you?"

"... shut up..."

"What was that, Potter? Dementor got your tongue?"

"SHUT UP!"

Suddenly, he was upon her, his hands grabbing the ornate cresting besides where the girl's head was resting, and he found himself snarling at her, screaming at the top of his lungs.

"DO YOU HAVE ANY IDEA WHAT THOSE THINGS DO TO ME WHENEVER THEY'RE NEAR?! I HEAR VOLDEMORT KILL MY PARENTS, OVER AND OVER AGAIN!"

All the rage and grief he felt since the Dementors' arrival was finally exploding, and he just couldn't stop. He didn't even notice Dracaena's reaction due the veil of tears now clouding his eyes. The girl recoiled from him and drew her legs close to her chest, and her usually composed face was a mask of pure terror. She'd never seen Harry like this; sure she got him angry on the regular, but this was... something else. It was as if he... hated her. Yes, hate was the right word. Until now, she saw him angered, annoyed, and fed up. But she never felt so despised by this boy.

"FIRST IT'S MY DAD! HE STEPS IN TO PROTECT ME AND VOLDEMORT JUST... KILLS HIM. THERE'S A LOUD 'THUD' WHEN HE FALLS DOWN. THEN IT'S MY MOM'S TURN. HE TELLS HER TO LEAVE BUT SHE PLEADS WITH HIM TO SPARE ME AND YOU KNOW WHAT HE DOES, MALFOY!? HE LAUGHS"

He knew he said too much, and that Malfoy would use it against him later, but he just couldn't stop. It felt oddly pleasant, letting it all out, even if it was in front of the last person he'd wanted to show weakness to.

"AND THEN HE SAYS 'AVADA KEDAVRA' AND THERE'S A GREEN FLASH AND THE SOUND OF MY MOM HITTING THE GROUND... AND THEN... and then..."

It was too much, and the words died in his mouth before he could finish recounting the harrowing experience. He managed to regain his senses, and distanced himself from Dracaena, settling back down onto the bench, his head in his hands.

"Just... just go away Malfoy. You got what you wanted, didn't you? Now leave me alone."

He expected her to hit him with another hateful retort, but this time there was only silence. He wasn't even looking at her, so he did not notice that her eyes were wet, and her lips were trembling. Hesitantly, she scooted over towards him on the bench. It took her a few tries to muster the courage to address him.

"I'm s-s-sorry, Potter. I d-didn't know..."

What the hell was going on? Was she apologizing? *The* Malfoy, apologizing to him, of all people? A few more moments passed, and before Harry could come to his senses she spoke again.

"Y-you know... I also couldn't defeat my Boggart."

The sudden revelation from Dracaena shook Harry from his torpor, and he turned towards her, and finally saw the effect his outburst had on her. She looked far from the proud and composed witch he knew. She looked... small. Like the little girl she was.

"Yeah. When *Rags* called me, I thought I would have gotten some kind of creepy crawly or something", she said while mimicking a bug's scuttling with her fingers, "but instead... I got father."

She shuddered at her recollection, before continuing, "he had this... disgusted expression, l-like he was looking at walking garbage and, and I just... froze. In front of everyone."

Was she trying to... make him feel better? The girl took a long, slow, deep breath before looking him in the eyes.

"A-anyway, back to the actal point. I think I can help you with the Patronus. I-if you want me too..."

She smiled weakly, tapping her thighs and just said "come here".

"W-what are you up to?"

Now that Harry was a bit more composed, Dracaena managed to summon her usual spunk and sounded a lot more confident, albeit her voice was still a bit shaky.

"Your head Potter, your head! Put it here!", and without waiting for him, she half-dragged him down and sat his head on the makeshift cushion.

Incredulous, Harry did not react when Malfoy started caressing his hair and cheeks with gentle hands, while looking right into his eyes.

"Mama alwas does this to me whenever I am upset, and I always calm down afterwards", she leaned down towards him, and her silky hair tickled him, "I-I thought that... maybe... it could help you as well."

Harry chuckled a bit, unintentionally, at the thought of the stuck-up witch who loved tormenting him referring to her mom as "mama", and confessing their embarrassing little ritual.

"Really? You call your mother 'mama'?"

Dracaena's pale cheeks suddenly turned to a burning pink, but she didn't stop, nor did she comment. A few more seconds slipped by before Harry managed to blurt out the question that had been occupying his mind.

"Why are you doing this?"
"What do you mean?"
"Being... nice to me. You're never nice to me."
The girl appeared a bit resentful at that, as if it was a false accusation, "l-listen Potter, I'm just... worried about our duel, ok? What am I going to do if the big scary Dementors show up during our Quidditch match and I win because you faint again? I want to beat you fair and square, no excuses. I want you to know I'm better than you."

Something did not sit quite right with him, but he decided against speaking up. Although he remained wary, there was a strange quality to Dracaena's smile that gave him pause. It was gentle and sweet, without any of the usual venom that accompanied it.

"I think this is enough, Malfoy."

Dracaena startled and immediately raised her head, distancing herself from his. He awkwardly got up, missing the softness a bit, and when he turned around Malfoy was already standing. She looked extremely embarrassed, possibly even more than him.

"S-so, did you like it?"
"What?"
"I mean, did it work?"
"I, I guess it did *something*..."

The girl clapped her hands once, "good! Now don't go fainting anymore! I need you to be in top shape when I demolish you!", and darted away without giving him a chance to respond, "oh, and if you tell anyone I'll hex you!". There was a certain skip to her step that only appeared after she beat him during dueling club.

"What was that about?"

He had faced three-headed dogs and ancient monsters before, but this had to be the strangest encounter he had ever had. Would this really help against a Dementor? And what was Malfoy even plotting?
He didn't even notice he was absent-mindedly touching the cheek Dracaena was stroking while making his way to Gryffindor's common room.
