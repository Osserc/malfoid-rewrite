# The Quidditch final

Harry barely registered the thunderous round of applause that greeted the Gryyfindor team as he and his team mates entered the Great Hall. He also barely noticed that Ravenclaw and Hufflepuff joined the cheer against Slytherin, fully concentrated as he was in locating the one person he wanted to see.

Dracaena Malfoy sat facing away from her own House's table, a quivering yet ferocious smile on her lips as she regarded Harry, her long hair tied into a practical ponytail. She did her best to appear in control, but her own complexion betrayed her nervousness, as she was even paler than usual. He stared resolutely into her eyes as he made his way to his own table, making a point of sitting across her.

Not that Harry was any better in regard to nerves: he knew Dracaena was going to be a formidable opponent. He saw plenty of what she was capable of both during practice and her own matches, and she was nothing like the overconfident rookie he faced the previous year.

He was so focused on Malfoy that he did not touch his breakfast despite Wood's urging, and Angelina had to haul him off his seat when the Gryffindor captain called his team to the field.

His meal was not the only thing he missed: his ears did not even pick up on Cho Chang sending him off with a "Good luck, Harry!", as his attention was dominated entirely by Dracaena, who never once took her eyes off him despite a certain pinkness coming to her cheeks.

She sustained his gaze without flinching, as did he. Neither of them was going to let the other claim even this small victory.

___________________________

They left their locker room in silence, Angelina placing a hand on Harry's shoulder before passing him. "Don't worry Harry, you'll have your lead before you know it." Her tone was confident, but Harry could see her shake slightly as reached her spot behind Wood.

60 points. If they were to win the cup, Gryffindor needed to be a whopping 60 points ahead of Slytherin before Harry caught the Snitch. Otherwise, the House of the serpent would have still won the cup based on their previous performances.

He trusted his team mates, and against any other opponent he would have remained serene. He felt confident he would have been able to hold off Diggory or Cho for longer; but Dracaena was a different beast altogether. The girl was fast and vicious, a way tougher opponent that the other two Seekers, and Harry wasn't sure he would have been able to pull off a stall this long. Especially after witnessing the deadly aim of Slytherin's beaters and their especially powerful Bludgers. He was confident he could evade them, but what about Katie, Alicia and Angelina? Would Fred and George be enough? Would they ever be able to put enough points between them and Slytherin before they fell apart?

His train of thought was cut short when he bumped into George, who had stopped walking after he planted himself in front of his Slytherin counterpart. The Weasley boy turned around with a mischievous smile.

"This afraid of Malfoy, Harry?"

A delicate giggle coming from his side made him snap his head around. Dracaena Malfoy was laughing her cute little chortle with a gloved hand covering her mouth.

*Hang on a second... that looks familiar...*

Dracaena noticed him looking at her glove, and held out her hand for him to inspect. There was no doubt about it: it was his own glove. The one she won from him a few weeks earlier in a dueling bout. He was sure she'd have burned it or something, it sounded like something she'd do. A nice little pyre for yet another piece of him she managed to tear away.

"A bit too big for my liking," she recited with a melodious voice she must have practiced a hundred times before, "But I wouldn't pass on the chance to beat you with your own stuff."

He saw that she had to fasten it tightly to fit her slender hand, and suddenly all his tension vanished, replace by pure whimsical joy.

"Two can play this game, Malfoy."

Barely containing his laughter, Harry raised his own right hand, palm outstretched, showing her how he, too was wearing a mismatched glove. It was a rather thin one, of a very dark green color, with soft silver threads woven into the fabric. Delicate hands had stitched an elegant cursive "D" into the palm, and Harry watched delighted as Dracaena's cheeks flushed in embarrassment. He had donned that glove on a whim that morning, and he did not expect that decision to pay off this much.

"Captains, shake hands!" said Madam Hooch.

Flint and Wood complied, and from their strained smiles and contract muscles everyone could see that they were trying to crush each other's fingers.

"Mount your brooms!" said Madam Hooch. "Three... two... one..."

The crowd erupted and drowned the whistle.

___________________________

Harry's bout of euophoria lasted very little, as he witnessed the Slytherin team play the dirtiest game of Quidditch he had ever participated in.

It all started right after Angelina quickly scored a goal for Gryffindor, as Marcus Flint slammed into her at full speed with a "Sorry! I didn't see her!", and Fred retaliated by hitting him on the back of his head with his club, which made him crush his nose on the handle of his broom. An incensed Madam Hooch issued a penalty for each team, and thankfully Wood managed to block Slytherin's, while Alicia scored hers.

They were up twenty to zero, but they needed forty more points before closing the match, and each score until then only made Malfoy's job easier. And from then on, the match got tougher and tougher for Gryffindor, as Slytherin amped up the rate of their fouls.

Harry knew what they were doing. Flint was a shrewd captain, and knew his own chasers were not as good as the opponent's, so he must have opted to hamper their performance with as much rough play as possible. He may have banked on his tougher, bigger players being able to withstand whatever retaliation Gryffindor was capable of, and would later capitalize on their fatigue. He seemed to have set aside winning the match itself, and opted for the safest way to win the Cup instead: close the game before Gryffindor gained their ninety points.

That bet looked to be the winning one: Gryffindor's forty to zero lead was quickly shortened to a mere twenty point lead, as Slytherin's chasers relentlessly targeted Wood's goals. Only the heroic efforts of the Weasley twins managed to beat the greens back enough to allow Katie to score again, widening the lead to thirty.

*This is not looking good,* Harry thought as he eyed at Dracaena. Seventy points on a Snitch were more than enough for her to grab it, and he was going to have to hold her off for who knows how long.

Their first bout came as Harry narrowly avoided Derrick and Bole trying to sandwich him, and he saw Dracaena speeding away, chasing a faint golden shimmer. Flint had just scored again, giving his Seeker a chance to not only secure the cup, but win the game itself for them.

In a panic, Harry accelerated, riding Malfoy's tail to catch up to her. He saw her stretch to grab the tiny sphere, but Harry had swerved earlier to her right, anticipating the Snitch's turn, which caused her to slam into him when she suddenly found him before her. The sudden contact took her by surprise and she grabbed him to avoid falling off her own broom.

"Don't you dare drop me Potter!", she screamed.

Harry had half a mind to just leave her to her own devices, remembering Wood's enraged shout when he opted to dodge Cho in the Ravenclaw match, but in the end he held on to her, helping her stabilize.

"HARRY WHAT DID I TELL YOU ABOUT BEING A GENTLEMAN?! JUST LET HER FALL!"

Wood's voice rang through the stadium, which elicited a great laugh from the stands. Dracaena however seemed in no rush to distance herself from Harry, and spent a few more instants clutching his flying gear breathing heavily before parting.

"Thanks," she muttered. "And sorry. I didn't see you coming."

Somewhat skeptically, Harry quipped "Taking a page out of Flint's book?"

The mention of her captain's poor sportsmanship made her snort, and she shot him a resentful look as she settled back on her broom, taking a deep breath now that she lost sight of the Snitch, allowing herself to reset.

"I hate it, you know."

She uttered those words in a low whisper, but they were clos enough that he could hear her. Harry regarded her quizzically, unsure. "It's working though, isn't it?"

"I don't want to win like this!"

"Why?"

"What do you mean 'why'?"

"Aren't you Slytherins all about cunning and strategy?"

"Some of us," she sniggered resentfully, "Have dignity, Potter. Something that is apparently lost on most."

And she sped off, gaining altitude to better survey the field in her hunt for the golden orb, leaving Harry to his own musings about this newfound Slytherin honor.

___________________________

Despite the relentless fouling from Slytherin, Gryffindor managed to claw their way closer to the sixty points lead they required. The score was now ninety to forty for Gryffindor, and Harry was quickly running out of steam.

Meanwhile Malfoy was a menace on the pitch: he had to constantly keep an eye on her, as the Snitch was now sufficiently slowed by the accumulated score for her to be able to spot it very quickly. They already clashed three more times, and each save from Harry was closer than the last. Dracaena was clearly upset that her job was to close the game as soon as possible with no regards for winning the actual match, but was she playing her part admirably, throwing herself behind the Snitch at every chance. And he had to admit that she was a sight to behold.

Of all his opponents, Dracaena was by far the most technically gifted flier he had ever faced, completely different from how she was the previous year. The biggest change he could see was her form. A year ago she was cocky and undisciplined, blindly following the Snitch as it led her around the pitch; this time, she was *stalking* it. Instead of chasing at full speed, she held back a bit to account for its sneaky swerves and feints, and whenever it turned she was able to shorten their distance by cutting through the angle between where the Snitch was going and her current position.

It was a more refined version of the same technique Harry had always used in his own encounters with the Snitch. But whereas Dracaena reacted, Harry predicted. He lost count of how many time Wood screamed at him to *please, be a normal Seeker* whenever he went off its trail only to then cut it off, but even the Gryffindor captain had to give up and let him play to his strengths. And his ability to somehow read the small twitches that the enchanted orb showed was the only thing keeping Gryffindor in the running, as Malfoy had been flinging herself at her prey relentlessly, only to be always greeted by Harry blocking her path after cutting a particularly brazen angle.

Harry could only marvel at how effortlessly Dracaena was steering her broom as if it were second nature. He always found the Nimbus series's flying charm to be a bit too opinionated for its own good. As long as you flew like a conventional high level player, the broom was excellent: it took care of all the little details while you could focus on scouting and dueling, like angling during a turn. For a cerebral player like Dracaena it was a match made in heave. He, however, always had to fight against it to pull off his own reckless stunts.

*No wonder she wiped the floor with Diggory and Cho.*

Meanwhile, his Firebolt was more than happy to let him do whatever he wanted without interfering, to the point he had to mind any stray thought from influencing it. Their last bout had been especially salient example. On the backfoot and unable to afford letting Dracaena out of his sight, he was almost tricked by her when she dove madly. Startled, his immediate thought of giving chase caused his broom to angle sharply downward, and it was only thanks to his keen eyes and trained reflexes that he noticed her leaning slightly towards her side, betraying her intention to change course at the last second, allowing him time to react accordingly when she inevitably swerved, in an attempt to shake him off.

She suddenly sprang upward and Harry caught sight of her smile, radiant and joyful. It was at that moment that her hair tie came off, and he did not realize that his hand shot off and grabbed it before it could fall to the ground, his broom deftly turning just a little bit to allow him the extra reach he needed before readjusting is course. As if the broom read his mind and knew what he was trying to do. The old Nimbus would have probably ignored it.

It was only after he cut her off yet again with a powerful ascent that he noticed the small emerald tie in his hand. He smiled to himself and tucked it safely away in his pocket, and he resumed his watch.

___________________________

"ANOTHER GOAL FROM JOHNSON! GRYFFINDOR NOW LEADS A HUNDRED TO FORTY! THEY CAN WIN THE CUP IF POTTER GRABS THE SNITCH NOW!"

*Finally.*

The announcement hit Dracaena like a particularly mean Bludger. She looked furious as she turned around and faced Harry. She had been able to focus entirely on the offensive all game, but now that Angelina scored, she could no longer afford to keep her eyes off Harry. She needed to tail him to be able to chase after him as soon as he moved to catch the Snitch if she was to have any chance of stealing it from him. She knew how keen his eyes were and how quickly he could actually grab the Snitch.

"Tired, Potter? Or do you still have some fight in you?" she panted as she slowly flew close to him.

The Slytherin's taunt dragged him out of his focused state, and as he regarded her his mouth curved into a devilish smile. Malfoy's hair was, quite frankly, a mess. It seemed to love the feeling of the wind sweeping it around, and had almost taken the shape of a lion's mane now that it was free of its tie's tyranny.

"Thinking of trying out for Gryffindor's cheer team, Malfoy?", he smirked, nodding at her hair with a move of his chin. He did not even notice the roars coming from the Slytherin stands as Bole managed to hit Alicia with a well placed Bludger.

Without missing a beat, Dracaena closed her fists as if holding imaginary pom-poms, brought a hand to her hip and shot the other arm upward with a playful "Go Gryffindor!", accompanied by a small movement of her hips.

"How do you even know what that is? That's Muggle stuff!"

Dracaena's smug smile was all he got out of her before Lee Jordan's furious voice shook him out of his stupor.

"HARRY STOP FLIRTING WITH YOUR GIRLFRIEND AND CATCH THAT SNITCH!"

"JORDAN! CUT THAT OUT!", interjected professor McGonagall.

Harry laughed at the insinuation, but his smile faded away as he saw Dracaena's face drain of colour as she stared at the Slytherin stand. Her House mates were no longer cheering, electing instead to scowl at her display. Harry saw Malfoy's hands tighten in angry fists as she clutched her thighs, but did not fly away, preferring to hover close to her rival instead.

An "OUCH, THAT WAS DIRTY!" came from Jordan as he witnessed Bole colliding full force with Angelina and almost knocking her off her broom.

Already at her breaking point, Dracaena finally snapped and sped towards her Beater, and started stabbing him with her finger, as Harry flew to Angelina's side to make sure she was alright, as was treated to the close up sight of an incensed Dracaena Malfoy.

"You inbred idiot troll! This is my match! Mine! You will not take it away from me!", Dracaena shrieked, as Bole stodd half confused and frightened of the tiny girl spitting insults at him.

For once, Harry was happy he was not the target of her tirades, but his relief was short lived as he noticed just how much her anger was deforming her face. She had seen her upset, but this was completely different, as if she really wanted to just march up to the Slytherin Beater and strangle him with her bare hands. It was an ugly expression, even more spiteful that he thought possible. Finally she relented as Madam Hooch awarded a penalty to Gryffindor, blocked by Bletchley, and she followed Harry back in th sky. He didn't even realize neither of them had so much looked at the Snith to fly to their team mates.

"Maybe your captain should have replaced with him an actual troll," he suggested, trying to defuse the situation, "You know, one of those guarding the Fat Lady. I saw one who seemed to understand English, the smaller one with the weird bowler hat."

"Ha-ha, how funny," she snarled, but the corners of her mouth curled a bit into a smile, "Thinking of a career as a clown? Hoping I'll just fall over laughing and give you the game away?"

He grinned, and feeling a bit guilty at having forgotten his task he resumed scanning methodically the field for any traces of shimmering metal, doing his best to ignore Dracaena, which was a lot harder than he anticipated. He was surprised to notice just how much he was enjoying her banter.

Then he saw it. A golden glimmer, lazily dancing behind Bletchley, the Slytherin goalkeeper. Almost in a trance, he surged forward, forcing a surprised yelp from Dracaena who scrambled to follow him too little too late. It was a straight line from where they were to the Snitch, and Harry was relieved that he would not have to wrestle with his Firebolt for such a simple task. Bletchley instinctively screamed as he dodged out of the way as Harry closed on him, which allowed the Snitch to realize it was being targeted. It scrambled to dive downward, and as Harry anticipated his movement and was stretching out his arm to-

"OH NO, FLINT SCORES AGAIN! A HUNDRED TO FIFTY FOR GRYFFINDOR!"

It took all his mental fortitude to avoid snatching the orb, and his fingers scraped the cold metal as they close on the air. He saw Dracaena rushing the now free snitch, and he had to muster all his remaining strength to swerve and use his from to obscure the Snitch from her vision. Fortunately his efforts were rewarded by an angry, frustrated shout, and he turned around to face the Slythering Seeker with a smug smile.

Dracaena was fuming, having had to stop herself to avoid tackling Harry again. A devious idea came into Harry's mind.

"*I can end this whenever I want*," he mouthed at her, aiming to provoke her.

The plan succeeded flawlessly. Dracaena let out a blood curdling scream as he vented her frustration.

___________________________

"ONE HUNDRED AND TEN TO FIFTY FOR GRYFFINDOR THANKS TO THE FANTASTIC KATIE BELL! THAT GIRL IS ON A ROLL TODAY!"

*I have to end this.*

He knew it. His ability to concentrate was running on fumes after spending the entire match micromanaging his Firebolt to perform ever bolder and riskier maneuvers just to keep up with Dracaena, and he was at the end of his rope.

Dracaena was now glued to him, close enough to graze each other with their shoulders, something that seemed to bring Lee Jordan a great deal of enjoyment.

But this time, neither he nor Dracaena paid him any mind, as they were both busy slowing the other down and surveying the pitch for signs of the Snitch. Dracaena swept a lock from her eyes and as she turned towards Harry's side, the boy caught a faint golden reflection in her silvery orbs. Judging from the look of alarm on her face she must have noticed its owner too, and Harry wasted no time in turning around and there was the Snitch, floating dreamily.

Leveraging his superior acceleration he gave chase, but Dracaena was hot on his heels thanks to the headstart her positioning gave her. She kept herself a little higher than him, ready to swoop down when the time was right. He saw her do it in her previous match, she was a master at turning the downward sweep into an impressive burst of speed to overcome her opponent. His Firebolt was too fast for that, but it certainly would have been enough to reach him and slow him down with her own body weight. And if she managed to send him off course, she might have even be able to catch the Snitch herself.

Desperate, Harry tried the same trick he employed against Ravenclaw. He baited her dash raising a hand, and at the same time he gracefully slid off his side. With the corner of his eye Harry saw Dracaena angling slightly downward, gaining momentum. She must have anticipated his trick and acted accordingly, aiming to occupy the space below his broom, where his body would have been and foil his attempt at ending the game. He caught a glimpse of her triumphant grin as she descended.

*Good, she took the bait,* he thought, and allowed himself a furtive smile.

She braced, but the impact she expected never came.

Instead of swinging helplessly like he did in the previous match, Harry used his right hand to interrupt his falling maneuver mid-movement, bent forward and flattened himself against the underside of his broom. Without a body to stop her advance, Dracaena could do nothing but watch in horror as she sped further downwards, away from her rival, who was now riding his broom upside down. Her frantic attempt at adjusting course were fruitless, and she could only watch helplessly as Harry was now flying upside down.

It was the toughest acrobatics he ever pulled off, and he mentally thanked his mysterious benefator for the Firebolt, who seemed more than Happy to oblige his decision to ride it upside down. Now free from his pursuer, he accelerated towards the snitch, reached out with the hand bearing the silver letter on it and...

"POTTER CATCHES THE SNITCH! GRYFFINDOR WINS THE CUP! GRYFFINDOR WINS THE CUP! ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY TO FIFTY!"

___________________________

The wave of euphoria that washed over him made the next seconds a blur.

He did not notice the stadium erupting in cheers for his team, and it was only when Wood crashed into him, throwing his rms around his neck and sobbing into his shoulder, that he reacquired a semblance of self-awareness.

Then it was the Weasley twins that slammed into the huddle, followed shortly by the three Chasers, who were chanting "We won the Cup! We won the Cup!"

The addition of the three girls sent the entire team slowly descending to the ground, just as the entire Gryffindor audience swarmed the pitch and raised its team into the air.

Now being carried by a multitude of arms, Harry was free to look around, and from his vantage point he saw Hagrid, crying into his gigantic handkerchief; professor McGonagall sobbing without any restraint into her hat; Ron and Hermione, beaming at him; and finally, Dumbledore holding the Quidditch Cup.

Then he turned around and saw the Slytherin team angrily stomping away, leaving a distraught Dracaena alone on the field on her knees, staring at her hands, her back convulsing from heavy whimpers.

A great shadow came over him.

*This isn't right.*

He did not even see Dumbledore bestowing the Cup upon Wood, the captain hugging it before turning to face Harry and thrusting it towards him.

"This is yours, Harry! You won us this Cup!", tears streaming down his face.

But Harry did not take it. He was staring at the girl crying in the middle of the pitch.

He still had his Firebolt in his hand.

With an agile movement, he jumped onto his broom and took off with a leap, rolling away from the crowd, then he started running towards the weeping girl.

___________________________

"Dracaena! Dracaena!"

She looked up with a tear-streaked face.

He crouched before her, panting, and extended his hand to her, the one that wore the glove with silver "D" on it.

"Let's go!"

She gave him a shocked look, and for a second she looked like she was somewhere else, eyes fixated on his hand.

She raised a skittish arm and made to accept his hand before retracting it in a rush, as if she were afraid she would have been stung.

Judging from her lips twitching, he could see she was trying her best to come up with a jeer, a scowl, anything to protect her wounded pride.

She opened her mouth, but no words left her once she raised her head and saw Harry's smile.

There was neither pity nor sympathy in it.

Just a delighted, happy, unrestrained smile.

Slowly but decisively, she reached for his hand with her oversized glove, and she yelped in surprise as he propped her up with strength she did not know he possessed. She was still clutching her Nimbus 2001.

"Come on, let's race! Last one beyond the lake is a flobberworm!"

He laughed heartily as he started running while pulling her along.

"Potter, what are you doing!?", she squealed as he jumped on his broom, and she did the same to avoid falling to the ground and dragging him down with her.

"MR POTTER! MISS MALFOY! COME BACK THIS INSTANT!"

Professor McGonagall's furious voice was the last thing they heard as they took off and sped towards the lake.

___________________________

They were still holding hands as they settled down at a more moderate speed, both their chests heaving with exhaustion, with Dracaena rather uncomfortable as it forced her in a rather awkward position to keep her hand in his.

"Is this your way of cheering me up?"

Harry turned around, giving her an excited smile.

"I wanted to fly with you some more!"

"Why, to rub it in?", she grumbled.

He drew closer to her, enough for their knees to touch.

"This was the most fun I ever had flying!" he exclaimed in between pants. "And it's thanks to you!"

He watched her fight back a smile and lose, and she finally let herself breathe. The lump in her throat dissolved as she saw Harry look at the horizon, an ecsatic expression on his face.

There was no winning against that grin.

Having reached the far shore, he beckoned downards and they both angled their brooms towards the ground.

Dracaena was the first to touch grass, and she tried her best to gather her voice.

"Looks like you're the flobberworm this time, Potter."

Harry theatrically dropped to the ground, doing his best imitation of the creature.

She couldn't help but laugh.

It started as a soft giggle which soon turned into a roaring fit of laughter which made her keel over, and she soon joined the boy on the ground.

Then, the ruckus died down and they laid side by side, arms open wide and grinning happily.

"How did you do that?", she finally asked, turning towards him and propping herself up with he elbow.

He beamed at her, as if he was dying to talk about it.

"I knew you were waiting for it. You always watch me fly," he rattled out, sitting up, "You were so close to me and I knew I had to trick you in some way, and that was the best I could come up with on the spot."

The girl sat up too, and looked at him intensely, drinking in every word.

"I didn't even know I could fly upside down," he continued, "I think I hurt myself when I gripped the handle," and he beckoned at his left arm.

"That was reckless," she whispered, "I would never have done something like that. It's so... uncouth."

Slowly, she reached out with her hand to give it a timid squeeze, and she let out a small giggle at his wince of pain. He just smiled in response, and a few more seconds of silence stretched between them. Then she spoke again.

"You won't get away with it next time," she declared proudly, trying her best to sound threatening and failing miserably.

Harry let out a delighted gasp at her attempt, and then a look of surprise dawned on his face. With a little "oh!" he started rummaging in his pocket, and took out the hair tie he rescued earlier.

Dracaena's eyes went wide.

"Is that- when did you-"

"I don't know, I just... grabbed it. It would have been a pity if you lost it. You looked great flying with your hair like that," holding out his hand.

They both blushed, averting their eyes. Then she started gathering her hair, and turned her back to him showing him a full view of her nape.

"Can you tie it for-"

The loud sound of a whistle had them both look up at once. Madam Hooch had finally caught up with them riding her own broom, and was gaining on them.

"Twenty points from Gryffindor! Twenty points from Slytherin! And you're both in detention! Separately!"
