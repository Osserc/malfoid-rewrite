# Uncle Severus

Despite its undisputable majesty, Severus Snape never thought of Malfoy manor as something that could be called a home. He couldn't help but think that it was nothing more than a monument to glories past, a statement more than a place to live. He very much preferred his own run down little house, in an incospicuous muggle neighborhood, where he could blend in with the locals and pursue his own research, undisturbed by anyone. He did, however, enjoy his regular outings to Lucius's, even if the motive behind it wasn't as festive as he'd hoped.

"Thank you as always, Severus. I wouldn't trust anyone else with this."
"Think nothing of it, Lucius", he said with an almost imperceptible curl of his thin lips, "it's the least I can do."
"It's not the least at all", replied the Malfoy patriarch, handing him a richly decorated lockbox, "here, the artifact you requested. I hope it will meet your expectations, this was exceedingly hard to find. Oh, and one more thing."

Lucius produced a small satchel, and as soon as he touched it he figured out the contents. To the untrained hand they would have felt like a misshapen rock, but he knew this was an authentic Graphorn horn by its characteristic ridges, clealy perceivable through the soft fabric.

"It seems I am to remain forever in your debt", mused Snape in his usual half-mocking tone.
Lucius Malfoy just chuckled, and gripped his arm lightly, "I should be the one to say that. This is but a gift for my old friend, who still comes to my aid after all these years."

He was indeed among the few people Snape could really call "friends": he would always be grateful how he took him under his wings when he first got to Hogwarts and was sorted into the noble Slytherin house. He remembered how he casually got him rare books, and he never made it feel like charity; how he sought his council for an advanced potions assignment; and even how he taught him some very specific curses that were impossible to find even in the Restriced Section of the castle's library. Oh, Severus knew he was still carefully cultivating his garden of clients, to be leveraged after graduating. But he did not mind. That was, after all, par for the course for the upper echelons of the wizarding world, and Lucius was a master at playing the game even as a young boy, and then a man. Still, Severus could feel that beneath that facade, Lucius did consider him somewhat of a close friend, and he was grateful for that. There weren't many of those left, if any, especially considering he never had that many to begin with. Especially not since Li-

"Are you the one who makes mama's medicine?"

A young child was peeking from behind an impressive ebony door sporting magically moving carvings of a writhing mass of snakes, each with gleaming emeralds for eyes. She could not have been older than five, and she was wearing a long, silvery dress, just like a doll. Her pale blonde hair fell loose behind her head, and not a strand of it was out of place, as if it had just been combed. She was the spitting image of Narcissa Malfoy, only smaller.

Lucius shot him an apologetic look before introducing her, "my daughter, Dracaena."
"I am indeed, Miss Malfoy."

The little girl run up to him, forgetting she was the heiress of the Malfoy lineage, and hugged him at his legs with all the strength her tiny body could muster.

"Thank you mister! Mama always feels better when she drinks it!"

The sudden effusion took both adults by surprise.

"Curious. She's never like this with strangers..."

Dracaena was now looking up at the sallow man with eyes that almost sparkled from excitement.

"Hey, you think I could make her medicine myself?"

Severus almost spat out a curt rejection, but the words died in his throat as he regarded the starry-eyed child looking up at him. It was the same childlike enthusiasm and curiosity *she* used to show...

"Dracaena, your mother's potion is extremely complex, and Severus here is one of the handful of people capable of brewing it. And about the only one I'd trust wi-", Lucius did not finish his thought as he saw his friend kneel down to look his daughter in the eye.
"It is not something that a novice would be capable of doing. But if you really are interested, I can set you on the path. After that, whether you succeed or not will be up to you."
The child in silver let out a squeal of delight, and puffed up her chest in pride. "I will do it! I will learn! Then I'll make plenty of mama's medicine, that way she'll never run out and she will be good forever!"

The potion master got up slowly, thinking. He looked at Lucius for any kind of hint as for what to do, but the man just shrugged with a weak smile.

"If you would... I'll have one of my old potions books sent to you. If you manage to get through it I will... consider... further instruction. Is that acceptable?"
A happy "yes!" came out of Dracaena, "thank you so much uncle Severus!", and she sped off at a trot, skipping happily down the hallway.

Severus felt a tiny smile creep up his face, as Lucius chuckled, "I can never win against that child. I wager she's going to regale Narcissa with tales of the upcoming alchemical prowess. And thank you for indulging her. She can be very persistent."
"You believe being a potioneer befits the heiress of the Malfoy family?"
"It befits a great man I happen to know, *uncle Severus*."

For the first time in a long while, Severus Snape let out a small, heartfelt laugh. He was strangely looking forward to having a pupil interested in the art.
