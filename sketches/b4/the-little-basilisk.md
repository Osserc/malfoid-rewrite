# The Little Basilisk

Harry Potter was in trouble.

Not that trouble was a stranger to him: in one way or another, dangerous and wondrous situations always seemed to know how to find him. But nothing could have prepared him for the relentless assailant who had been hounding him for the last couple of days.

*Fleur Delacour*.

The part veela champion of the french school seemed to have taken a shine to him ever since he saved ("a most gallant rescue, Arry!") her sister Gabrielle in the Second Trial of the Triwizard Tournament. Not that Gabrielle was in any danger, of course. Harry had been the only champion to take the sirens' song too seriously and insisted on saving the little girl when Fleur was nowhere to be seen, alongside... her. He did not expect to see her of all people down in the lake, and the mere sight of her sleeping form was enough to stop him in his tracks, as if he had been Stunned. He remembered how her pale blonde hair floated freely around her, enveloping her form like an otherwordly halo. He couldn't see her silvery eyes, but the expression she was wearing in her enchanted sleep was regal and dignified. Majestic even. She was even more beautiful than when they danced at the Yule Ball together, in front of everyone to see. And despite his heart's frantic protests to get Dracaena out of there right that instant, he could not leave Gabrielle there, in the depth, waiting for a sister who might never come.

In hindsight, Harry deeply regretted his choice of rescuing both girls. He suspected it was only thanks to Ludo Bagman that most of the judges agreed to reward his "bravery" ("more like utter stupidity, Potter"), which allowed him to remain in the running for the tournament. It was bad enough that people thought he was an attention seeker of the worst kind, now he had to endure every student's jabs. The entire school body seemed to relish in poking fun at the "valorous knight" who insisted on bringing both damsels back to shore, and could not distinguish fantasy from reality. And Fleur, of all people, was not helping.

She was always able to find him and made a point of praising him and grabbing his arm, much to the annoyance of Hermione, Ron, and of course a certain Dracaena Malfoy ("oh non, ze petit basilic!"). The french champion was hell bent on provoking the Slytherin girl, acting extra saccharine especially in front of her. Harry remembered clearly how upset she was that Fleur had tried to kiss him on the cheeks when he emerged with her sister from the murky depths of the lake ("sank you so much, mon chevalier!"), so he closed his eyes and indulged in the memory for a moment. Her anger had been a sight to behold.

_________

He surfaced from the lake gasping for hair, the gillyweed extract having already run out and leaving his lungs in flames. The two people he was carrying were not cooperating, but he was at least grateful they weren't making his job harder, but as soon as their noses felt the presence of open air they woke with a sudden gasp.

"Gabrielle! Gabrielle!"

He felt one weight leaving his hands, as Fleur reached out and grabbed her sister from Harry's grasp. The *other* weight, however, was less courteous. As soon as she came to, Dracaena gripped him in a vice and refused to let go, buring her face in his neck.

"Malfoy, you're hurting me."

"You deserve it, stupid, stupid Potter!"

It took the superhuman might of Hagrid (whose arms received plenty of nasty scratches from Dracaena's nails, desperately trying to escape his firm grip to get back to Harry) to separate them and allow Madam Pomfrey to administer first aid. After a couple of minutes all the late comers from the abyss were wrapped in magically heated blankets, with a cup of hot chocolate in their hands. Dracaena was nestled under Harry's own cloak, and looked like a fearsome dragon protecting her hoard, very much like the Horntail he faced months earlier.

Then it happened.

As Fleur approached Harry, and her arms were trying to wrap him in an embrace, Dracaena angrily shoved her away with more strength that she should have been able to exert. He would always remember her threat:

"BACK OFF YOU SLAG! HE'S MINE!"

And then she kissed him, furiously, in front of the whole school.

_________

After that, Fleur took great pleasure in barging in between Harry and Dracaena. She wore her signature enchanting smile but even Harry could figure out that there was a hidden war going on around him, and he was the cause. Not that he minded that much, truth be told. Dracaena took the challenge to heart and was even more forward with him. Sure, she never missed a chance to berate him in public, but it was their game at this point, and he greatly enjoyed how she always managed to dress compliments in scathing remarks in ever more creative ways. Until it finally came to an end on the third day.

The youngest Triwizard champion was enjoying breakfast when Fleur came sauntering over to him, and unceremoniously pushed Neville out of her way to sit besides Harry.

"Bonjour Arry, mon cher! Had good dreams, oui?", as she started slathering a toast with a very generous serving of butter.

Harry's eyes frantically scanned the room for signs of Dracaena to get him out of this mess, but she was nowhere to be found. Before he could mutter anything in response, Fleur leaned towards him, and suddenly her voice changed. It was very mellow and sweet.

"My trickz really do nozing to you, do zey?"

Her eyes were sparkling, and Harry could see no hint of malice in them. If anything, they were filled with nothing but joy.

"I can zee zat you like her very much. You could not rezist me ozerwize", then as she regained her usual playful demeanor, "say 'aah~'", and she held the buttered slice of bread in front of his mouth.

A loud slam of fist on wood suddenly reverberated along the Great Hall. Harry's neck snapped towards it, hoping to see Dracaena, and sure enough there she was, with a clenched fist resting on the heavy door. He mouthed a silent "save me" to her, pointing at the french girl at his side, who also turned to look at her "rival", and a mischievous smile formed on her lips. She dropped the toast and stood up, dragging Harry along.

"Arry mon cher, it's ze petit basilic! Quickly, get behind me!", then to she proclaimed to Dracaena "away, villainess! You shan't have my Arry!"

Fleur stood between Harry and the advancing girl, as if to shield him from a dangerous beast. Dracaena's eyes were filled with icy fury as she stopped before the two, and although she was shorter than Fleur, she appeared way more dangerous. A single word left her lips with a hiss:

"Leave."

The french champion raised a hand to the sky, and moved the other to her heart, as if pretending to faint.

"Ah! Zis is no little snake, but a fierce dragon! I can't best her!"

Gracefully spinning around, winking at Harry with a "remember your Fleur, Arry! You will remain in my heart forever!", as if it were a line she rehearsed dozens of times before, she retreated towards her own schoolmates at the Ravenclaw table.

"Potter."

A sudden force grabbed his tie and jerked him around, and he was face to face with Dracaena.

"Oh thank goo-" he tried to say, but his words were smothered by Dracaena's lips closing his own.

After what felt like an eternity, Dracaena pulled away, but did not let go of his tie. "We're leaving", and started dragging him towards the doors of the Great Hall, as their audience erupted in chatter, clearly amused by the spectacle.

_________

"Malfoy, can you stop now? My neck hurts."

Once away from prying eyes, she let go of his tie and turned around angrily. She was wearing a formidable scowl and was biting her thumbnail as she was wont to do when something annoyed her, usually him. But her anger did not extend to her eyes.

"That devious woman. Now I'll have to send her something nice."

"What was that about?"

She raised her gaze to look him in the eye, and started jabbing at his chest with a pointed finger.

"That whole pantomime was an excuse t- ah, what do you know Potter? I bet you were enjoying it anyway, weren't you? Oh, to have the exotic veela from France fawn all over you before everyone, another woman falling at the feet of the Boy who Lived! What is this, the third one now?"

Harry could not avoid chuckling at her little show of jealousy. There was a time where she feared her frown and the venomous words that would soon come out of her lips, but he knew her well enough by now that he could only find her endearing. Dracaena pouted.

"You know, you could have made more of an effort to push her away."

"I'm sorry, I'm not too good at this."

"Well good thing I am capable enough for both of us then. Honestly Potter, you're so hopeless, I bet no other girl would put up wi-."

Suddenly Harry wrapped her in a tight hug, and he could feel Dracaena melt into his embrace and press herself against him.

"Do it first, next time", she grumbled.

He couldn't help but smile at her antics, "do what?"

"Figure it out!"

She pushed him away and started angrily walking towards the Potions dungeon, but after a few steps she spun around, and flashed him a luminous smile.

"What are you waiting for? Come on!"

The boy hurried up and took his place besides Dracaena, and she wasted no time in grabbing his hand, and leaned towards him as they walked. He gripped hers back, and he swore he could hear a purr of delight coming from her.

"You know, Fleur said something strange to me earlier. She said 'my tricks really have no effect on you', or something like that. What did she mean?"

He titled his head towards Dracaena's and could see her cheeks taking on a very cute shade of pink. "I don't know Potter, do I look like an encyclopedia to you? Ask you *other* girlfriend, I'm sure she'll love to regale you with everything she knows about veelas", she said, trying her best to sound annoyed.

"Since when are there two Dracaena Malfoys at Hogwarts? How could I miss her?", he said playfully, as he made a show of looking around.

"You couldn't handle two of me, Potter", she giggled, before briefly standing on her tippy toes to steal a quick kiss from his lips.

"Ugh, could they NOT?"

"Really, it's getting out of hand..."

Dracaena quickly turned around and stuck out a tongue at the approaching Ron and Hermione, who vocalized their displeasure at her effusions.

"Jealous, mud- muffin head?", she corrected herself mid-sentence, before sticking even closer to Harry, humming to herself.

Harry turned his neck slightly and shot his friends an apologetic smile, before resting his head on Dracaena's, and taking hold of her hip with his arm.

Not even the prospect of a Potions lesson could dampen his mood, as he could hear Dracaena humming happily at his side.
