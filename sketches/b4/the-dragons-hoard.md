# The dragon's hoard

Harry was grateful for Sirius's presence at his side, supporting him with the massive frame of his dog form as he made his way out of Dumbledore's office and towards the hospital wing. He didn't think he had it in him to walk the whole way there on his own, especially not through the flight of stairs that led to the office he had just left. As the effects of Fawkes's ministrations started to fade, he felt his exhaustion mount and his bad leg was on the verge of giving way, but thankfully his godfather was with him every step of the way, watching him like a hawk and ready to hold him up as he stumbled.

The huge dog had his work cut out for him when they finally made it to Pomfrey's, as Harry was swarmed by the small crowd that had taken up residence there, all waiting for him. Amidst the cacophony of "Harry!" shouted his way, Mrs Weasley and Hermione were the first to close the distance, and it was only thanks to Sirius that they did not bring the spent wizard to the ground in a tackle. Only one person did not move, Sirius noticed, and she was staring at Harry with great worry and apprehension, her knuckles white as she clenched her robe. *Dracaena Malfoy*. Harry was looking at her as well, but before he could speak, Dumbledore raised his voice, addressing the clamouring room:

"I understand your eagerness, but Harry here has been through a terrible ordeal tonight. He has had to relive it for me, and I will not ask more of him. What he needs now is rest, and peace, and quiet. You may remain if he wishes so, but I ask you to refrain from questioning him. He will tell you when he is ready, which may be never," then he gestured to the bed, and the huge dog gently nudged Harry towards it, helping him along the way.

As the boy was settling into it, Bill and Ron started placing some chairs around the bed, to allow everyone to sit next to Harry. Harry's eyes were still fixed on Dracaena, who was now standing alone in the centre of the room, barely holding up her composure.

"Headmaster," said Madam Pomfrey, as she regarded the colossal canine helping set up the seats by pushing them around, "may I ask what-?"

"This dog will be remaining with Harry for a while. I can assure you he is extremely well trained. Harry, I will wait while you get into bed."

Unnoticed by the people swarming Harry's bed, Sirius crossed the room to reach the girl who was struggling to remain quiet. Unlike his friends, she did not utter his name nor did she lunge at him. She stood there, watching him, with anguish and worry in her eyes, but not daring to make a move. While Pomfrey went to fetch a Sleeping Draught to grant Harry a peaceful sleep, and the boy lay down in his bed, Sirius acted. With all his might, he bulldozed the Slytherin girl forward, causing her to yelp loudly and stumble towards the bed, and she had to grip it to avoid falling to the ground. Suddenly, all eyes were on her, her own darting frantically around the room, unsure of what to do.

"Dear, are you-", started Mrs Weasley, before being interrupted by a weak and soft voice coming from the bed.

"Dracaena...", rasped Harry, and the Malfoy girl's head snapped to him.

Harry held up his right hand towards her, and beckoned weakly at it with his head. There were the remains of a nasty gash on his forearm, a reminder of the dagger wound he had received earlier that day.

"I'm sorry... I think I lost your ribbon...", he apologised with a feeble smile.

It was too much for the girl, who finally let out all the feelings she was desperately trying to push down. Before anyone could stop her, she climbed onto the bed and flung herself towards Harry, pressing him against the cushions holding up his head.

"YOU STUPID, STUPID IDIOT! WHY DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT THE RIBBON?!", she wailed as tears were now streaming freely down her cheeks. "WHO DID THIS TO YOU?!"

"It was a gift...", offered Harry in his defence, as he hugged her back with his good arm.

"Miss Malfoy! I will throw you out if you don't let go of my patient right this instant!", bellowed Madam Pomfrey as she witnessed Dracaena's assault while carrying a purple potion after bustling out of her office.

"I AM *NOT* LEAVING!"

"Dumbledore, do some-", but Pomfrey's words were drowned by the low, threatening growl coming from Sirius, who rejoined Harry at his bedside. Dumbledore exchanged a look with the dog before intervening.

"Miss Malfoy, I will not ask you to leave, but please let Harry lie down. He needs his rest," then, taking Madam Pomfrey aside, he said in a whisper, "Miss Malfoy may need to be here more than Harry does."

"Come now, dear, you can sit beside him, here, all right?"

Reluctantly, Dracaena allowed Mrs Weasley to pry her from Harry, who was smiling happily as he watched the girl slinking back towards the chair. Their hands trailed along each other's arms as she was pulled away, and they ended up resting atop each other's.

Grumbling about "proper procedures", Madam Pomfrey brought a goblet to Harry's mouth, "You'll need to drink all of this, Harry. It's a potion for dreamless sleep."

Harry disentangled his hand from Dracaena's, took hold of the goblet and drank a few mouthfuls. Dracaena steadied the cup as soon as she noticed his hand lose strength, as Harry was feeling himself becoming quite drowsy, and placed the goblet on the nearby bedside cabinet, before putting her hand back on his.

"Sleep well, my prince...", she whispered, as she gently caressed his grimy cheek.

Steadily, Harry's vision blurred, and the last thing he saw before falling asleep was Dracaena, smiling at him and her fingers intertwined with his.

Harry had not managed to get any sleep after he was discharged from Pomfrey's. Without the aid of the Sleeping Draught, his nights were plagued by nightmares of what happened in the graveyard: Cedric's murder, Voldemort's resurrection, the torture, the duel... and the other boys in his dormitory noticed. He woke screaming in the night, multiple times, for two days straight. Although they tried their best to appear supportive, their patience was starting to wear thin.

As for Harry, he looked extremely tired, with heavy bags under his eyes evidencing his lack of sleep. He came to dread the idea of closing his eyes, in case he saw the horrors again. When he received the message on the morning of the third day, he welcomed it.

"*Midnight. The dungeons, two corridors from the Slytherin common room.*"

It was Dracaena's handwriting.

He told neither Ron nor Hermione. His two friends did not forgive Dracaena for trying to keep them away from him when he lay in bed in the hospital wing, and they did their best to prevent them from being alone together during the last two days. If they knew she was trying to lure him away from the tower in the middle of the night, they'd have tried their best to foil their plan.

And he missed her, dearly. He longed for some time alone with her, without his friends trying to not leave them alone out of some misguided fear. Dracaena herself took the hint, and soon enough she stopped trying to elude their constant surveillance, and buried herself in the library.

So when he found the tiny paper snake bearing the message that managed to sneak into his bag, he was elated. That night, he donned his Cloak of Invisibility when he thought Ron was asleep and sneaked out of his dormitory.

The castle at night was surprisingly silent, despite the recent happenings, but he welcomed it.

He was so eager to meet Dracaena that he arrived at the meeting spot an hour early, and his trepidation quickly gave way to anxiety.

He started pacing in front of the dead end he had been directed to, trying to calm himself down.

What if it was indeed a trap? What if Crouch had an accomplice in the castle? What if-

"*Potter! What are you doing here so early!?*"

He turned around so fast he almost lost his balance. Dracaena's voice was a low hiss, and came from... below?

"*Step aside, or you'll fall down!*"

Suddenly, he felt the ground cave under his feet, and he jumped sideways to avoid getting sucked in by the shifting stone. He marvelled as the stony surface was rearranging itself into a spiralling staircase that led into the bowels below, which reminded him of the Chamber of Secrets. But as he started the descent, he noticed that unlike the Chamber, the stone was neither slick nor grimy, and in fact it was spotless, as if it was regularly maintained. Candles emitting a pleasant green light were lined at regular intervals, which gave him a clear view of the steps as the ceiling closed again to conceal the secret entrance.

As he was making his way downstairs, however, he could not help but think of the graveyard. The green light was eerily similar to the Killing Curse that took away his parents, and Cedric a few days earlier. But as he heard Dracaena's voice, he steeled himself, twisting his torso a bit to avoid scratching his arms against the rather narrow walls.

"Come in, come in!" she invited, as a richly ornate door came into view, already opened. He deftly removed his Cloak, and made his way into the secret chamber, closing the door behind him, which shut with a series of heavy clicks.

The first thing that Harry thought was that the room was impressively green, even more than the Slytherin common room that he had visited two years before. A big, enchanted fireplace hosted a flickering green blaze, which cast playful shadows across the circular room. Despite its overall small size, the room was home to a variety of comforts: a queen-sized canopy bed, a large mirror hanging over the fire where Dracaena was working, a round sofa with plenty of cushions and even a bathtub. Whoever made this room their home was used to luxury, and despite being smaller than the common room, it looked far cosier. Or at least it would have, had it not been for that dreadful green glow that kept reminding him of his nightmares.

Dracaena was attending a happily bubbling cauldron, and judging from her annoyed stomps and *tch*s she wasn't having much success with whatever she was attempting. It was an odd sight for Harry: he had never witnessed her fail a potion before. But what surprised him more was her attire.

If someone had told him that Dracaena Malfoy slept in pyjamas like a commoner, he wouldn't have believed them; he always figured her for the type to wear an elegant nightgown or something like that. Instead, she wore a comfy set of trousers and shirt, with long sleeves. He initially thought they were white, but on closer inspection the fabric showed an intricate lattice of very thin lines, and they seemed to dance slowly as the green glow shone upon them. He was so enthralled by the vision that he did not even notice the green light being replaced by a softer purple glow at a flick of Dracaena's wand. As he glanced upward at the large mirror hanging over the fireplace, he could see the violet light complement her lovely silver eyes wonderfully, and Harry did not mind at all that her pale hair, usually neat and composed, hung loose and dishevelled. She was even more beautiful than when she went all out for the Yule Ball, and suddenly he was somewhere else.

*They were on a roomy sofa, the bright morning light coming in from the window and bathing Dracaena's hair in its glow. She was sitting in his lap, both wrapped in a blanket and holding a cup of hot chocolate. He took a sip and gave her a kiss on the nose, despite her protests, and left a brown mark on its tip. The girl laughed, and nuzzled closer to him-*

"Nice place too, a bit too green for my tastes though. It could stand some red. Or gold," he teased her, looking around. "Wait, is that...?"

He did not see it when he first entered the room, but that was unmistakably a Gryffindor banner spread across a wall. Under the green light it would have passed for a Slytherin standard, but on closer inspection the difference was clear. There were no snakes embroidered in the cloth, which bore the lion instead.

"No Potter, don't look!", but Dracaena's warning came too late. Harry's eyes went down, and found a small stand directly below the banner, sporting an odd assortment of everyday objects. Among them a quill, a Chocolate Frog card with Dumbledore on it, a worn Seeker's glove and... his old scarf? A memory came rushing to him: their last duel of their second year. She had won that bout, evening their score, and she had claimed his Gryffindor scarf.

"Did you keep all the stuff you nicked from me?"

"What was I supposed to do, throw them away?"

He gave her a big smile as he reassured her, "Truth be told, I have a small treasure trove myself of your things. Not as grand as this one though; I don't have a whole Slytherin banner in my room."

"This is why I told you to come later! I didn't have time to clean up, or change into something else!"

"I think I prefer the snake pyjamas, you look adorable in them."

She did not dignify his remark with a proper response, settling for a "humph!", but she did nothing to hide her grin from him.

"So, this room... what's the story behind it?"

Glad to have a way out of her embarrassment, Dracaena quickly plunged into the chamber's secret history. Apparently nobody knew who built it, perhaps it was another one of Salazar Slytherin's ideas, but what was for certain was it came into the Malfoys' possession around three centuries before, when Brutus Malfoy was at Hogwarts. According to what her mother Narcissa had told her, Brutus found the secret room and charmed it so that no one could open it without knowing its password, which was then passed down the Malfoy family for generations.

"Mama told me the secret last year. I suppose the current furniture was chosen by father, it looks a bit too... austere for her liking."

"So, are you going to tell me?"

"Tell you what?"

"The secret," he smiled, knowing full well what her reaction would be.

"Potter!", exclaimed Dracaena as she blushed furiously, "I'm not letting you barge into my own room as you please! This is an emergency, all right?"

Now it was his turn to react, cocking an eyebrow, "Emergency?"

"I know you can't sleep. Nightmares from... that night. I spent the last two days racking my brains trying to figure out a solution, but I found nothing I could do in a matter of days. So here we are."

"Dracaena-"

"Listen Potter. You are not looking good, and not even your roguish smile can hide those huge bags you have under your eyes", she said as she made her way to the sofa and sat on it, "and if I can't put you to sleep with a potion, I can at least be here to comfort you, should anything happen to you in your sleep."

Just like she had done the previous year, she patted her thighs, invitingly. "Come, I'll watch over you."

Harry shifted uncomfortably on his feet, "It... it might be pretty bad."

"I am *not* letting you go through this alone. Now hurry up, we don't have all night."

Reluctantly, Harry joined her on the sofa, and lay down, his head on her soft thighs. Dracaena leaned down on him, and took his head in both hands, and he felt something soft slide over his hair. And suddenly, his vision went dark.

"It's my sleep mask. I have work to do, so I can't put the lights out," she said apologetically.

As Dracaena's delicate hand kept playing with his hair, he fell into sleep with the light crackle of the fireplace, and the sound of the turning of the pages of a book.

He had no nightmares that night.

Lucius Malfoy was standing at King's Cross station, wondering why his devil of a daughter was nowhere to be found.
