# After Sectumsempra

The door of the Hospital Wing crept open, and Snape's figure emerged, his robes in an even more pitiful state than usual. His greasy hair framed a face that was the very image of exhaustion, but the emotion that showed even more vividly was anger.
Directed at him.
Suddenly, Harry sprung into action, darting into the room before the Potion master slammed it shut, under the cover of his Invisibility Cloak.
It took him four days before he managed to get past Snape's guard and get into the room.
He begged for the first two.
He cried and pleaded to be let in, to see her, to explain, to apologize.
But the Death Eater would not relent.
Then he decided to sneak in, and it took two more days for Snape to lower his guard enough to allow him to elude his surveillance.
And now that he was in, he found it hard to approach the bed where he knew Dracaena lay.
Heavy blue curtains hid Dracaena from sight.

*You have to do it. You have to tell her.*

"*Muffliato*", he muttered, delicately parted the veil and stepped up to the bed.

His heart nearly gave out, as did his knees, and he had to grab onto the bedframe to avoid falling.
There she was, her chest heaving with great difficulty while soaked bandages wrapped the wounds all over her body.
The wounds *he* gave her.
He reached out with a trembling hand and held hers, gently, as if he was afraid that his mere touch could break her all over again.
Her face was contorted into a suffering grimace.

*You did this.*

He knelt down and placed his forehead on the back of her smoldering hand, the result of his curse and the mix of counter-curses and potions battling it from within her body.
If this was her state after four days, he dared not imagine what she felt immediately after he cast that blasted spell.

Suddenly, a familiar smell among the stench of blood and sweat caught his attention.
It was a delicate pear scent, a hint of vanilla, and a slew of minor fragrances he could never quite recall.
He could, however, perfectly remember where it came from.

__________________

They were at Cresswell & Sons, during one of those "secret Hogsmead dates" of theirs, not that they were fooling anyone.
Officially he was there as Dracaena's errand boy, forced to carry her shopping bag as punishment for his constant misbehaviour; the truth, of course, was that his girlfriend was abusing her position in the Inquisitorial Squad as a front to bring him with her whenever she wanted with plausible deniability.
Dracaena was gleefully watching him squirm in front of the witch clerk, struggling to make his choice.
His blonde captor burdened him with a most arduous task: picking out a perfume for her from among the store's offerings, and he eventually settled for something that he though would "just smell nice".
It was something fresh and innocent, the kind of scent that-

"Really, Potter? Pear, of all things? This is pathetic, it's a farm boy's idea of what a high class lady would like", she shrugged, shooting the clerk a look that said it all.
"Well, sorry if it wasn't up to her highness's refined tastes", retorted Harry with a knowing smile.

He knew this was a trap he was meant to fall into. The Malfoy heiress seemed to have developed even more of a sadistic streak since the previous year, and he was perfectly aware that this was her way to fish for compliments, as well as hand them out.

"Did I say I dislike it?"

She treated him to her enchanting smile, her as suave as an angel as she leaned on him, holding his arm.

"I suppose it is fitting you'd chose something like this, so that you'll find me even among a crowd. Merlin knows no one in my position would wear this otherwise", then she turned to the clerk and announced "I'll take this one."

From then on she made a point to always wear it when they were together, and being the gifted potioneer she was, she even managed to make it herself. She liked adding her own spin to it with each new batch, and loved trying to get Harry to detect which particular flavor she chose to include at the time, only to poke fun at him when he inevitably failed.
To think she would still wear it, even after all these months apart...

__________________

"You're the last person I want to see right now, but if you're not going to leave, at least have the decency to show your face."

Her voice was hoarse and ragged, and though she struggled to let the words out, the hostility permeating them was unmistakeable. But he didn't care. She was *alive*.

"Dracaena!"

His Invisibility Cloak fell to the wayside as he stood up, clutching the girls's hand even tighter.

"Are you here to admire your handiwork, Potter? Or did you come to fini-"

A coughing fit interrupted her, not as bad as the ones she had on the first day, but it was a hard, rasping noise that made more tears cloud his eyes.
He immediately reached for the potion on the nightstand, helped her sit up, and brought it to her lips. He had witnessed Madam Pomfrey administer it while Crabbe and Goyle held him back, a few days back, as he was trying to force his way in.
He thought she would fight back, tried to push him away, but she just let him help. Was she that weak?

"I... I am so sorry for this... I-I didn't know what that spell was, I thought it would on-"

He couldn't even finish his sentence as Malfoy shot him a look of pure disgust.

"I will carry some of these scars for life, Potter."

At any other time, Harry would have had quite a different reaction at the sight of the girl of his dream unclasping the sash holding her hospital gown together. This time, he had a look of pure horror as he could see the dark outline of a jagged, pulsating black scar worm its way from her abdomen all the way to her neck. She gently caressed the curse mark he gave her, and addressed him with the voice she used with those who disrespected her and were due a polite, yet deadly tongue lashing.

"Look, we match now, don't we? I bet this is what you were after, hm? You wanted to mark me, too?"

The Boy who Lived was too enthralled by the grim spectacle that he paid no mind to her wording. He could only stare at the result of his handiwork on Dracaena's fair skin. The same pure white skin he used to kiss and stroke tenderly, now reduced to a dark canvas of his own making.

"Snape says this one will be permanent", she said almost innocently, while tracing the scar. "I see that leaving your sign is something you share with the Dark Lord."

The mention of Voldemort roused him from his shock, and his eyes darted towards her face. Her expression was one of disdain, anger and... was it disappointment?

"No, no no no. Dracaena, I'm not like him. You know I'd never-"
"Do I, Potter?", her furious voice suddenly rang with renewed vigor, as she dropped her act and now let her real emotions run wild, "what I do know, is that you tore me apart!"

Her voice started to crack, and she could no longer hold back her tears. Both grief and rage now danced across her face, as she struggle to let our her thoughts.

"I guess I should be thankful that Harry Potter did not *mean* to slice open my stomach!"

The effort took most of her energy, and she fell back on the soft bedding, an arm folded over her eyes to hide her tears.

"Princes are supposed to save their princesses..."

*Princes...?*

Then he remembered. How often she referred to herself as his "princess", and how she talked about the Muggle fairy tale she was reading and how Harry was her own knight in shining armor, vanquisher of dragons and evil wizards! And now he had become the villain of their story.

"Why couldn't you let me have my sweet dream? I know we have to wake up, in the end. But I wish... I wish you'd just killed me, Potter. That way I wouldn't have to suffer like this. I would never have discovered who you really are."

She'd rather be dead. He felt his heart sink into his chest. Until now, he really thought he could fix this somehow. If only he could talk to her, tell her how sorry he was, how it was all a big mistake... he held onto the tiny hope that she would forgive him. And then he'd nurse her back to health, and kiss her scars better, and this would have become nothing more than a bad dream.
But there was no coming back from this, was there?
He destroyed the most beautiful thing that ever happened to him.
He shattered Dracaena's heart.

*No, I can't let it end like this.*

He wanted to look her in the eye. He wanted wipe away her tears, and tell her he was sorry, so sorry that he did this. He grabbed her arms and pulled them away from her face. Her eyes were red and swollen, half vacant, as if her spirit was lost somewhere else, far, far away from where he was. But he had to try. He had to make her understand.

"Dracaena, please, you have to listen to me! All I wanted was you! Why do you think I spent all this time trying to figure out what you were doing? Because I missed you! I missed... I missed us! Our little outings, our stupid fights in front of Umbridge, our-"

His hold was strong enough that his fingers started leaving red signs red signs on her pale skin, and a wince of pain escaped her chapped lips, as some of the minor wounds reopened as a result of his pressure.
She finally looked into his eyes, and they were filled with such contempt that he felt all his strength leave his body.

"Of course. I know what you want. Go ahead, do it. And then leave."

She feebly guided his hand atop her breast.

"That's all I was to you, wasn't I?"

Horrified, Harry left her arms, and stumbled backwards.
How stupid did he have to be to think that a "sorry" would have made up for his crime?

*I have no right to stand beside her.*

Wordlessly, he picked up his cloak, wrapped himself in it, and rang the bell on Dracaena's drawer.
Then he left.
As he closed the door behind him, he heard the muffled cries of the love his life.
Struggling to keep his own down, he started running.
