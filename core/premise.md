# Premise

Draco Malfoy is a girl: **Dracaena Malfoy**, only child of Lucius and Narcissa, Slytherin, Harry's rival and his tsundere love interest. The rewrite covers all seven books. It is not a cosmetic genderswap: the change is there to be used, and plot beats, mechanics and setting change wherever that serves the story.

## Pillars 🔒

Set in stone by the author. Full entries are in [the ledger](../continuity/divergences.md).

- **DIV-001**: Draco is Dracaena Malfoy, Harry's tsundere love interest.
- **DIV-002**: Time-Turners don't exist.
- **DIV-003**: B3. Dracaena regrets getting Buckbeak condemned. She saves him herself and brings him to Sirius so he can escape.
- **DIV-004**: B4. Harry and Dracaena go to the Yule Ball together. It's the turning point of their relationship.
- **DIV-005**: B4. Dracaena takes Ron's place in the Second Task, as the one Harry would miss most.
- **DIV-006**: B7. Harry and Dracaena duel Voldemort together. Both use Dracaena's wand, and their "love" rebounds the Killing Curse onto Voldemort through Expelliarmus.

## Directions 🔒

The principle is locked; the design is still open.

- **DIV-007**: Spellcasting is technical. Wand motions draw "radicals" that combine into spells.
- **DIV-008**: The pureblood ideologues have an actual point.
- Canon elements that break the plot or the ethics get dealt with, not ignored. See [problems/](../problems/_index.md).
