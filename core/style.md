# Style

## Point of view

*(settled)* Multiple POVs, not only Harry's (Q-017). Canon already opens B4, B6 and B7 outside Harry's head.

Caution: her chapters shouldn't explain her hostility away. If we see too much of her soft side, the hostility reads as an act. Chapters from Hermione or Narcissa, seeing her from outside, help keep the balance.

## Language

British English spelling and terminology (canon default).

## Tone and rating

_Open._ Not afraid of the hard beats (Sectumsempra, Burbage).

*(settled)* B6 turns much darker, on purpose, as canon does: the shadow war becomes a real one.

## Prose rules

**Ration the weighted kisses.** The pecks they steal off each other are free, and they're frequent: they're how the two of them talk. The weighted ones are spent only where something changes, and never twice on the same note. A second public kiss three days after the lake would have repeated the claim instead of raising it, which is why the Great Hall gets a bite and the corridor gets a peck that undoes it ([the-little-basilisk](../scenes/b4/the-little-basilisk.md), DIV-038). B4's weighted count is two: the Yule Ball and the lake.

Otherwise _open._
