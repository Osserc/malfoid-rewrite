# Timeline

Dates follow canon unless a `DIV` says otherwise. Each entry gives its date, the event and its references. Only list events that matter to our plot, or that we've changed.

## Before 1980

_Marauders era, the first war. Not yet worked out._

- **1689**: The Statute of Secrecy is signed (canon). The long decline of the magical population begins. DIV-035
- **c. 1970–1981**: The first war. Wizarding births dip, and every pupil at Hogwarts during the series is born in it. DIV-035

## 1980–1991

- **1980-06-05**: Dracaena born (canon default: Draco's birthday). DIV-001
- **1980-07-31**: Harry born.
- **1981-10-31**: The Potters are killed; Voldemort falls.
- **Early 1980s**: Lucius escapes Azkaban by claiming the Imperius Curse (canon). A Death Eater still at large tries to curse him for it and hits Narcissa, who can never have another child. DIV-030
- **Soon after the curse**: Snape starts brewing Narcissa's medicine, which holds the curse at bay without lifting it. DIV-030, DIV-031
- **c. 1985**: Dracaena, about five, meets Snape at the Manor and asks to learn to brew her mother's medicine. He sends her one of his old potions books; private lessons follow until Hogwarts. DIV-031
- **Mid-1980s**: Harry secretly watches *Sleeping Beauty* from behind the Dursleys' living-room door. DIV-009

## B1 · 1991–92

- **1991-07-31**: Diagon Alley. Harry and Dracaena meet at Madam Malkin's. DIV-009
- **1991-08**: Dracaena reads the Muggle books Narcissa obtained for her. DIV-010
- **1991-09-01**: Hogwarts Express. Harry rejects her; her grudge begins. DIV-009
- **1991-09-01**: The Sorting. Ron goes to Hufflepuff without a moment's hesitation, Hermione to Ravenclaw. DIV-033
- **1991-09**: First Potions lesson. Snape has her answer the questions Harry can't, the Draught of Living Death among them. Hermione blurts out the third answer and loses points; Dracaena outdoes her. DIV-011, DIV-031

## B2 · 1992–93

- **1992-12**: Lockhart's Duelling Club ends in disaster (canon). Afterwards the students petition to keep it open, and Flitwick takes over. Harry and Dracaena become regular, evenly matched opponents. DIV-028

## B3 · 1993–94

- **From about 1993**: The post-war cohorts reach Hogwarts, and each Sorting runs a little longer than the last. DIV-035
- **1993-09**: The hippogriff lesson. She milks the injury for weeks: the match delay, the Potions servant. Lupin runs the Boggart lesson House by House; she sits out Slytherin's session in her sling, and faces hers alone with Lupin once it comes off. DIV-017, DIV-042
- **1993-10-31**: Black slashes the Fat Lady, assuming Ron is a Gryffindor. DIV-036
- **Later in the year (canon timing)**: Black breaks into Hufflepuff and stands over Ron's bed. DIV-036
- **During B3**: Hermione drops Muggle Studies, and Dracaena calls it conceding defeat. DIV-012
- **1994, during Harry's Patronus lessons**: she spies on them and studies the charm for him, getting only mist herself. The lap-pillow scene. DIV-018, DIV-027
- **1994-06**, the night of Buckbeak's execution: her fake lead sends the Dementors to the lake. She frees Buckbeak, ends up at the lake, fails to cast a Patronus and confesses. Harry casts his, with a first flicker of resonance. She flies Buckbeak to Sirius, who escapes. Sequence provisional (Q-003). DIV-003, DIV-018

## B4 · 1994–95

- **1994-08**: Quidditch World Cup. The veela don't touch Harry: he's already in love without knowing it. Dracaena notices, and assumes it's Cho. Arthur and Lucius are unmoved too. DIV-037, DIV-041, Q-050
- **Between the visitors' arrival (1994-10-30) and the Yule Ball**: at the Duelling Club, Harry beats a Durmstrang student who has taken an interest in Dracaena, too aggressively. Rita Skeeter gets her ammunition. DIV-029
- **Before the Yule Ball**: Sirius plants the idea of asking her. DIV-019
- **December 1994, before the Ball**: Dracaena's owls home. Lucius suspects, and Narcissa confirms it and takes Harry's side. DIV-040

- **1994-12-25**: Yule Ball. Harry asks, Dracaena accepts. Their first kiss. DIV-004, DIV-013
- **Right after the Ball**: Rita Skeeter prints that they went together. DIV-040
- **After the Yule Ball**: its memory gives her her first corporeal Patronus. DIV-027
- **1995-02-23**: the eve of the Second Task. Dumbledore has a word with Dracaena as he puts the hostages to sleep. DIV-046
- **1995-02-24**: Second Task. Dracaena is Harry's hostage, and the dream begins. He goes down on her gillyweed extract; at the lake she claims him in front of the school. DIV-005, DIV-013, DIV-038, DIV-039
- **The next three days**: Fleur's test, then her pantomime in the Great Hall. DIV-037, DIV-038
- **1995-06-24**: Third Task. Afterwards, in the hospital wing, Harry keeps Lucius's name from Fudge, with Dracaena there. DIV-047
- **End of B4 (June 1995)**: Dumbledore, who noticed the missing name and welcomes it, tells Harry she's in a very tough spot. DIV-046, DIV-047
- **End of B4 (June 1995)**: Lucius orders Dracaena to cut ties with Potter. She refuses, throwing Harry's silence about him back at him, and he comes up with the "keeping tabs on Potter" strategy. DIV-013, DIV-040, DIV-047, Q-022
- **Summer 1995**: Snape teaches Dracaena Occlumency, and sees her memories of Harry. DIV-020, DIV-031, Q-043

## B5 · 1995–96

- **1995-09**: Umbridge shuts down the Duelling Club, one of her first acts. DIV-028
- **1995–96**: Lucius meets Voldemort regularly, never at the Manor, and tells him he has planted his daughter as a spy. DIV-020
- **From 1996-04**: the Inquisitorial Squad forms after Dumbledore's flight (canon). She uses its privileges to take Harry to Hogsmeade on "errands": their secret dates. He picks her pear perfume. DIV-013, [the-pear-perfume](../scenes/b5/the-pear-perfume.md)
- **1996-06-05**: Dracaena turns 16.
- **1996-06**: Department of Mysteries. Sirius dies; Lucius is arrested (canon). In his office Dumbledore tells Harry to treasure the love he has left (DIV-046). Soon after, she comforts Harry and promises to be there for him. DIV-014
- **Summer 1996**: She is Marked and meets Voldemort for the first time. He shatters her Occlumency with ease, and her faith dies. Lucius is in Azkaban (Q-030). DIV-015

## B6 · 1996–97

- **1996-09**, start of term: she's distant and hollow, only physical with Harry, keeps disappearing, and quits the Quidditch team. DIV-015
- **1996-09**: First Potions lesson with Slughorn. Harry smells her perfume in the Amortentia, and she doesn't bother to name it. Harry beats her at the Draught of Living Death using the Prince's notes. She and Hermione both notice his tricks resemble her technique. DIV-011, DIV-045, Q-042
- **Late 1996-09** (tentative): Flitwick reopens the Duelling Club. Harry offers it as a date; she refuses, and their fight ends in the breakup. DIV-015, DIV-028, Q-031, [the-breakup](../scenes/b6/the-breakup.md)
- **1996–97**: she can't cast a Patronus. DIV-027
- **Spring 1997**: Sectumsempra, at night, in front of the Mirror of Erised, which breaks in the fight. Snape saves her too late to prevent the scars, which she bears for life, and buries the incident. DIV-032, DIV-043
- **The same night**: Harry follows her to the Hospital Wing and attacks Crabbe and Goyle when they bar his way. In the days after he begs Snape, screams at him, and gets detention. Q-057
- **Four days later**: Harry gets past Snape and visits her in the Hospital Wing. [after-sectumsempra](../scenes/b6/after-sectumsempra.md)
- **The night of the cave**, end of term: on the walk to Hogsmeade, Dumbledore says something cryptic to Harry about her. DIV-044

## B7 · 1997–98

- **1997-07**: Malfoy Manor. Burbage pleads with Snape and with Dracaena before she's killed. DIV-016
- **1998-05-02**: Battle of Hogwarts. Her Patronus drives off the Dementors for a while (DIV-027). After Snape's memories, Harry stops for Dracaena on his way to the forest and tells her never to give up hope (DIV-022). With Harry shown dead, she challenges Voldemort. Harry rises and joins her, and together they defeat him (DIV-006, DIV-023), while Neville and the DA hold off the risen dead and the Dementors (DIV-025)
