# Timeline

Dates follow canon unless a `DIV` says otherwise. Each entry gives its date, the event and its references. Only list events that matter to our plot, or that we've changed.

## Before 1980

_Marauders era, the first war. Not yet worked out._

## 1980–1991

- **1980-06-05**: Dracaena born (canon default: Draco's birthday). DIV-001
- **1980-07-31**: Harry born.
- **1981-10-31**: The Potters are killed; Voldemort falls.
- **Early 1980s**: Lucius escapes Azkaban by claiming the Imperius Curse (canon). A Death Eater still at large tries to curse him for it and hits Narcissa, who can never have another child. DIV-030
- **Mid-1980s**: Harry secretly watches *Sleeping Beauty* from behind the Dursleys' living-room door. DIV-009

## B1 · 1991–92

- **1991-07-31**: Diagon Alley. Harry and Dracaena meet at Madam Malkin's. DIV-009
- **1991-08**: Dracaena reads the Muggle books Narcissa obtained for her. DIV-010
- **1991-09-01**: Hogwarts Express. Harry rejects her; her grudge begins. DIV-009
- **1991-09**: First Potions lesson. She knows the Draught of Living Death question Harry can't answer. DIV-011

## B2 · 1992–93

- **1992-12**: Lockhart's Duelling Club ends in disaster (canon). Afterwards the students petition to keep it open, and Flitwick takes over. Harry and Dracaena become regular, evenly matched opponents. DIV-028

## B3 · 1993–94

- **1993-09**: The hippogriff lesson. She milks the injury for weeks: the match delay, the Potions servant. DIV-017
- **1994, during Harry's Patronus lessons**: she spies on them and studies the charm for him, getting only mist herself. The lap-pillow scene. DIV-018, DIV-027
- **1994-06**, the night of Buckbeak's execution: her fake lead sends the Dementors to the lake. She frees Buckbeak, ends up at the lake, fails to cast a Patronus and confesses. Harry casts his, with a first flicker of resonance. She flies Buckbeak to Sirius, who escapes. Sequence provisional (Q-003). DIV-003, DIV-018

## B4 · 1994–95

- **Between the visitors' arrival (1994-10-30) and the Yule Ball**: at the Duelling Club, Harry beats a Durmstrang student who has taken an interest in Dracaena, too aggressively. Rita Skeeter gets her ammunition. DIV-029
- **Before the Yule Ball**: Sirius plants the idea of asking her. DIV-019

- **1994-12-25**: Yule Ball. Harry asks, Dracaena accepts. DIV-004, DIV-013
- **After the Yule Ball**: its memory gives her her first corporeal Patronus. DIV-027
- **1995-02-24**: Second Task. Dracaena is Harry's hostage, and the dream begins. DIV-005, DIV-013
- **End of B4 (June 1995)**: Lucius comes up with the "keeping tabs on Potter" strategy. DIV-013, Q-022
- **Summer 1995**: Snape teaches Dracaena Occlumency. DIV-020

## B5 · 1995–96

- **1995-09**: Umbridge shuts down the Duelling Club, one of her first acts. DIV-028
- **1995–96**: Lucius meets Voldemort regularly, never at the Manor, and tells him he has planted his daughter as a spy. DIV-020
- **1996-06-05**: Dracaena turns 16.
- **1996-06**: Department of Mysteries. Sirius dies; Lucius is arrested (canon). Soon after, she comforts Harry and promises to be there for him. DIV-014
- **Summer 1996**: She is Marked and meets Voldemort for the first time. He shatters her Occlumency with ease, and her faith dies. Lucius is in Azkaban (Q-030). DIV-015

## B6 · 1996–97

- **1996-09**, start of term: she breaks up with Harry, brutally. DIV-015, Q-031
- **1996-09**: First Potions lesson with Slughorn. Harry beats her at the Draught of Living Death using the Prince's notes. DIV-011
- **1996–97**: she can't cast a Patronus. DIV-027
- **Spring 1997**: Sectumsempra (canon, kept).

## B7 · 1997–98

- **1997-07**: Malfoy Manor. Burbage pleads with Snape and with Dracaena before she's killed. DIV-016
- **1998-05-02**: Battle of Hogwarts. Her Patronus drives off the Dementors for a while (DIV-027). After Snape's memories, Harry stops for Dracaena on his way to the forest and tells her never to give up hope (DIV-022). With Harry shown dead, she challenges Voldemort. Harry rises and joins her, and together they defeat him (DIV-006, DIV-023), while Neville and the DA hold off the risen dead and the Dementors (DIV-025)
