# Divergence Ledger

Every departure from canon. Anything not listed here follows the original books.

Entry format: **Status** · **Canon** (what the original does, in our own words) · **Ours** · **Why** (if it isn't obvious) · **Home** (where it's written out in full) · **Ripples** (affected files, open questions).

### DIV-001 · Draco is Dracaena Malfoy

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Draco Malfoy, only son of Lucius and Narcissa. Harry's school rival, never a love interest.
- **Ours:** Dracaena Malfoy, only daughter. Harry's rival and his tsundere love interest. Canon Draco is her baseline, adjusted for the knock-on effects of her being a daughter.
- **Home:** [characters/dracaena-malfoy.md](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)

### DIV-002 · No Time-Turners

- **Status:** 🔒 locked

### DIV-009 · The first meeting at Madam Malkin's

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco brags about brooms and houses, asks about Harry's family and sneers at Hagrid. Harry gets out before giving his surname, disliking him.
- **Ours:** Sunlight on her hair reminds Harry of Disney's *Sleeping Beauty*, which he once watched in secret from behind the living-room door. When she asks who he is, he blurts out whether she's Sleeping Beauty. Then he notices she doesn't have the princess's purple eyes, and tells her hers are pretty too. She's had plenty of compliments, but never one this sincere and awestruck. It sours when she learns it's a Muggle story: flustered, she changes the subject by sneering at Hagrid. Harry leaves thinking the "princess" is cruel, and regrets being nice. Neither learns the other's name.

### DIV-010 · Dracaena's secret Muggle books

### DIV-011 · Dracaena is gifted and brave

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco is a capable student who leans on his father's name and folds under real danger.
- **Ours:** An excellent potioneer, cunning, and a voracious reader on whatever grips her: poisons, the strange boy's stories. Competent and no coward, as befits wizard royalty.
  - **Potions:** the best potioneer in her year, and possibly in the castle, thanks to a six-year head start with Snape as her master (DIV-031). Hermione comes second. At least in Potions, she isn't the brightest witch of her generation.
  - **B1, the first Potions lesson:** Snape has her answer the questions Harry can't, to show her off. Hermione blurts out the answer to the third and loses points: right by the book, imprecise in practice. "Anything to add, Miss Malfoy?" Dracaena corrects her from years of experience and wins points for Slytherin. Hermione's hatred of her starts here.
  - **B6, the first Potions lesson:** Harry beats her at the Draught of Living Death using the Prince's notes. The sixth-year Prince's tricks are experimental and less elegant than the method the adult Snape taught her, and she has no drive left to one-up Harry: her priorities are elsewhere (DIV-015). She and Hermione both notice how much his new tricks resemble her technique.
- **Why:** her skill needs a source, and Hermione needs taking down a peg.

### DIV-015 · Between B5 and B6: the death of faith

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco takes the Dark Mark and the mission to kill Dumbledore, as punishment for Lucius's failure. He's proud of it until it crushes him.
- **Ours:** She is Marked and co-opted (canon default). That summer she meets Voldemort for the first time, and her Occlumency (DIV-020) is no match for him: he shatters her walls with ease. She sinks into despair and panic and goes along with the assassination plot, to at least save her parents. She's back in the cage, and the promise she just made is broken: at the start of B6 she breaks up with Harry, brutally enough to lead in the end to Sectumsempra. B6 is the nightmare.

### DIV-017 · B3: her play-acting, and what it costs

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco milks his hippogriff injury for weeks. Slytherin uses it to postpone their match against Gryffindor, and he makes Harry and Ron prepare his Potions ingredients. He also joins the fake-Dementor prank at the Ravenclaw match.
- **Ours:**
  - **The injury:** she milks it for weeks too. She's not above subterfuge and pettiness when it comes to throwing darts at her target.
  - **The match:** she uses the injury to delay the match against Harry, because after losing in B2 she knows she needs more practice.
  - **Potions:** she badgers Harry into preparing her ingredients and turns him into her servant. When his poor technique spoils the potion she steps in, strangely sweet under the barbs. In her head they're a married couple cooking together, until she realises she's daydreaming in front of the whole class and scrambles to save face. Harry is weirded out.
  - **The lesson:** later she realises her play-acting gave Lucius the fuel to have Buckbeak executed. That lesson in consequences is what pushes her to save him.
  - **The prank:** she takes no part in the fake-Dementor prank at the Ravenclaw match. Crabbe and Goyle pull it on their own initiative, thinking she'll approve, and are confused when she greatly disapproves. Kicking him while he's down isn't her game ([lap-pillow](../scenes/b3/lap-pillow.md)).

### DIV-018 · B3 climax: the swarm, the confession, the flight to Sirius

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** The Dementors converge on the lake, drawn by Black. Harry is saved by a Patronus his time-travelling self casts. Then he and Hermione fly Buckbeak to free Sirius.
- **Ours:**
  - **The lead:** to make room to free Buckbeak, she plants a fake lead with the Ministry that draws the Dementors to the lake (how: Q-025).
  - **The lake:** she can't control Buckbeak and ends up at the lake herself. She knows the Patronus Charm, having watched Harry attempt it and studied it for him, but she tries and can't cast it (DIV-027).
  - **The Patronus:** Harry re-evaluates her. She stole Buckbeak to save him, the lap pillow and her Patronus study weren't jokes, and now she's trying to drive the Dementors off herself: there's something more to this girl. His Patronus drives off the swarm and saves everyone, powered by the memory of the lap-pillow scene. As he casts there's a first flicker of resonance between them (DIV-021): love, however vague, is already there. How it works is open (Q-028).
  - **The confession:** under the Dementors' influence she breaks down sobbing and confesses, unprompted, that the swarm is her fault.
  - **Hermione's idea:** ask her to fly Buckbeak to Sirius.
  - **Why she agrees:** she owes Harry, and knows it. And she's just been thrust into the fairy tale: her prince drove off a horde of demons, for her.
  - **Sirius:** he recognises Narcissa's daughter, and they talk briefly. Maybe the Black family made something good after all, if Narcissa gave birth to a girl this brave.

### DIV-020 · Occlumency: the Malfoys, and Snape's lessons

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco is an Occlumens, taught by his aunt Bellatrix (mentioned in B6). Lucius isn't shown to be one.
- **Ours:**
  - **The family:** no one is born an Occlumens, but the discipline the Malfoys instil lends itself to learning it.
  - **Lucius** is a skilled Occlumens. He can't lie to Voldemort's face as Snape does, but in their regular B5 meetings he tweaks what Voldemort perceives through passive Legilimency. He tells Voldemort he has planted his daughter as a spy on Potter (DIV-013). Voldemort never comes to the Manor before B6.
  - **Dracaena** learns Occlumency from Snape in the summer between B4 and B5 (the author has the hook). Snape's cover holds: he's a fine Occlumens, and he has plausible deniability with Lucius, since he trained her as a general precaution for someone working under cover, not to deceive Voldemort.
  - **It isn't enough.** In summer 1996 Voldemort shatters her walls with ease (DIV-015). In the final duel she uses it another way (DIV-021).
- **Why:** Legilimency isn't mind reading, and Voldemort is very arrogant. That leaves Lucius room to tweak and Snape room to hide.
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md) (the rules) · [characters/dracaena-malfoy.md](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md) (her lessons) · [characters/lucius-malfoy.md](../characters/lucius-malfoy.md) (Lucius)

### DIV-021 · Resonance

- **Status:** settled. The link rule in its home is draft.
- **Canon:** Love works as magic through sacrifice: Lily's death protects Harry, and his in B7 protects the defenders. In B5 Voldemort can't stay in Harry's mind once it fills with love. Wands bond strangely through twin cores (Priori Incantatem, B4).
- **Ours:** A wand resonates when two people wield it in total sync, and their magic becomes exponentially stronger. It's literally the power of love: friendship isn't enough, or it would be more common. It's obscure, extremely rare and extremely hard to pull off.
  - **Sync:** both draw the same radicals (DIV-007) at the same instant, with no leader. Harry and Dracaena can because of years of feuding and duelling (DIV-028).
  - **Volatile:** no instant win. It shows cracks, but it holds.
  - **Occlumency:** in resonance Dracaena wraps Harry's mind in hers. Voldemort's Legilimency finds nothing but her love for Harry.
  - **The stained soul:** until the forest, Harry's Horcrux blocks it. In near-misses she's hurt by the stained, mutilated soul she senses in him, without either of them knowing what it is.
  - **How it builds:** a first flicker at the B3 lake (DIV-018). Beyond that it's kept vague until a dedicated session (Q-028).
  - **Nobody knows:** in school no one knows what resonance is. Dracaena finds the rule by research and tells no one.
- **Note:** builds on 🔒 DIV-006 and 🔒 DIV-007 without changing them.
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-012 (answered), Q-014, Q-024, Q-028 · DIV-018, DIV-023 · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md)

### DIV-023 · The final sequence

- **Status:** settled. Harry's broken wand is draft.
- **Canon:** Narcissa declares Harry dead and Hagrid carries his body to the castle. Neville defies Voldemort, who sets the Sorting Hat burning on his head. Neville pulls the sword from it and kills Nagini, and the battle resumes into the Great Hall, where Voldemort duels McGonagall, Slughorn and Shacklebolt at once. Harry reveals himself, explains the Elder Wand's allegiance while they circle, and the curse rebounds off his Expelliarmus.
- **Ours:** While Voldemort taunts Neville, Dracaena steps forward and challenges him to a duel, taking up the mantle of the boy she loves. She's outmatched. Their spells ricochet, Neville kills Nagini (canon), and a brawl breaks out. She's wounded, and McGonagall, Slughorn and Shacklebolt cover her and are overwhelmed. She's back on her feet with her wand on Voldemort when Harry, risen in the chaos, walks up behind her, wandless and smiling, and closes his hand over hers on her wand. They stare Voldemort down and duel him in resonance (DIV-021). He throws everything at them, Legilimency included, along with the risen dead and the Dementors, which Neville and the DA hold off (DIV-025). He ends up cornered. His Killing Curse meets their Expelliarmus and rebounds. No wand technicalities decide it (DIV-024): together, they are simply stronger.

### DIV-024 · No wand allegiance

- **Status:** settled. The Elder Wand's own lore is open (Q-011).
- **Canon:** A wand's allegiance passes to whoever defeats its owner, by disarming or overpowering them, and a won wand works better for its new owner. The Elder Wand's chain (Dumbledore → Draco → Harry) decides the final duel.
- **Ours:** Purged. Wands have no allegiance that passes by conquest. The Elder Wand is the exception, with lore of its own: it passes by blood, through killing. So Voldemort killing Snape is the correct move, and pointless (DIV-023). Only the idea is set.
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-011, Q-027 · DIV-023

### DIV-025 · Neville holds the line

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** After killing Nagini, Neville disappears into the general fighting. When Harry and Voldemort face off in the Great Hall, everyone falls silent to watch, and nothing threatens the duel.
- **Ours:** Voldemort can't get past the resonance, so he turns the battlefield against them. With the Elder Wand at full power he raises every body in the Great Hall as an Inferius, friend and foe alike: he sees only bodies, and any will do. He calls back the Dementors Dracaena drove off earlier (DIV-027) and sets the whole horde on the duellists, who are locked on him and can't defend themselves. Neville stands against it with his own wand and the sword of Gryffindor, which burns through the Inferi, and keeps fighting as his injuries mount. Surrounded and down on one knee, he hears Godric Gryffindor tell him to stand his ground as Gryffindor's heir. He gets up and rallies the DA, who raise walls of flame against the Inferi, and together they shield Harry and Dracaena long enough for them to win. On the way he sees Bellatrix and lets her go, because he's needed to protect his friends: he too chooses love and justice over vengeance. He survives.

### DIV-028 · Flitwick's Duelling Club

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** In B2 Lockhart starts a Duelling Club, with Snape as his assistant. Its one session ends in chaos with Draco's conjured snake and Harry speaking Parseltongue, and the club is never heard of again.
- **Ours:** After Lockhart's disastrous first session, the students petition the teachers to keep the club going with a proper instructor, and Flitwick steps in. Harry and Dracaena become regular, evenly matched opponents: she's the better technician, he's instinctive and thrives in chaos. Over the years they learn to read each other, and come to enjoy the fights. In B4 it stays open, and the visiting students join. Early in B5 Umbridge shuts the club down, one of her first acts.
- **Why:** it's where they come to know each other. It gives the years of duelling behind resonance a concrete home (DIV-021), and shows her sincerity in a way that sets up the B3 lake (Q-035).

### DIV-029 · B4: the Durmstrang challenger

### DIV-030 · Narcissa's curse

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco is an only child, and canon gives no reason. Narcissa is never Marked but stays in the Death Eaters' orbit until B7, when love for her son leads her to lie to Voldemort.
- **Ours:** After Voldemort's fall, Lucius escapes Azkaban by renouncing him and claiming the Imperius Curse (canon). A Death Eater still at large tries to curse him for it, and hits Narcissa instead: she can never have another child. The curse is still at work: a magical necrosis on her belly, like Dumbledore's hand. A very complex potion that Snape brews shrinks it and keeps it at bay, without ever lifting it (DIV-031). After years of treatment it's weaker, and she lives a more or less normal life, but she needs regular doses or it regresses, and she still bears its dark marks. One day Dracaena takes over brewing it in his stead (when: Q-041). Lucius never forgives himself. Narcissa forgives him, but uses his guilt to manoeuvre him where Dracaena is concerned. The Death Eaters took her future children, so she has soured on the cause and wants Dracaena to be happy, almost in her stead. That makes her Harry's ally inside the house. Lucius stays on board only out of fear, the same reasoning Dracaena will follow in B6 (DIV-015).
- **Why:** it explains the only child, and gives Lucius's sheltering, his guilt and Narcissa's indulgence one root. The medicine keeps the Snape–Malfoy connection going, and gives Dracaena a tangible way to show her growth when she steps in to brew it.

### DIV-031 · Uncle Severus: Snape is her potions master

- **Status:** settled. The annotated book is draft.
- **Canon:** Snape is a friend of the Malfoys and favours Draco in class, but there's no private bond and no tutoring. Draco is a capable potioneer, nothing more.
- **Ours:** Snape has brewed Narcissa's medicine since soon after the curse (DIV-030), and Lucius pays him for it. Around 1985, when she's about five, Dracaena meets him at the Manor. She hugs him for making mama feel better and asks to learn to brew it herself, so mama never runs out. Her eager curiosity reminds him of Lily, so instead of refusing he offers to set her on the path. First comes one of his old potions books, then more instruction once she gets through it. He becomes her master, and she calls him "uncle Severus". What he teaches her is the adult Snape's refined method, more elegant than the experimental tricks in the Prince's sixth-year book. By Hogwarts she has a six-year head start (DIV-011).

- **Why:** it grounds her skill, explains why Snape favours her, roots her potions in her love for her mother, and keeps the Snape–Malfoy connection going.

### DIV-032 · Sectumsempra scars her for life

- **Status:** settled. How far the scars extend is open (Q-044).
- **Canon:** Harry hits Draco with Sectumsempra without knowing what it does. Snape arrives, closes the wounds, and says dittany straight away may spare him even a scar.
- **Ours:** Snape saves her, but arrives too late to prevent the scars, and she bears them for life. At least one runs from her belly to her neck, which reminds her a little of her mother's marks (DIV-030).
  - **Harry** didn't know what the spell would do. He can never forget the mistake he made in a moment of anger.
  - **Dracaena** goes through something similar at first, but she comes to like them, as a symbol of what they got over together. They match his lightning scar.
  - **Their game.** She sometimes uses them to guilt-trip him into doing her favours. He always kisses them when he sees them, hoping one day it'll make them disappear.
- **Why:** the moment of anger has to cost them both something permanent, and the scars are what they get over together. Dark magic leaves lasting damage, as it does in canon (George's ear) and with Narcissa's curse (DIV-030).
