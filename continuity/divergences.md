# Divergence Ledger

Every departure from canon. Anything not listed here follows the original books.

Entry format: **Status** · **Canon** (what the original does, in our own words) · **Ours** · **Why** (if it isn't obvious) · **Home** (where it's written out in full) · **Ripples** (affected files, open questions).

---

### DIV-001 · Draco is Dracaena Malfoy

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Draco Malfoy, only son of Lucius and Narcissa. Harry's school rival, never a love interest.
- **Ours:** Dracaena Malfoy, only daughter. Harry's rival and his tsundere love interest. Canon Draco is her baseline, adjusted for the knock-on effects of her being a daughter.
- **Home:** [characters/dracaena-malfoy.md](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-001 · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md) · [setting/society.md](../setting/society.md)

### DIV-002 · No Time-Turners

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Time-Turners exist. In B3 Hermione uses one to attend overlapping classes, then she and Harry use it to save Buckbeak and Sirius. The Ministry's stock is destroyed in B5.
- **Ours:** Time-Turners do not exist.
- **Why:** Time travel breaks the plot. See [problems/time-turners.md](../problems/time-turners.md).
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-002, Q-003, Q-004 · DIV-003 takes over the B3 rescue.

### DIV-006 · Harry and Dracaena defeat Voldemort together

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Harry duels Voldemort alone, using the hawthorn wand he took from Draco. The Killing Curse rebounds off his Expelliarmus because the Elder Wand's allegiance had passed to Harry (Dumbledore → Draco → Harry).
- **Ours:** Harry and Dracaena duel Voldemort together, both using Dracaena's wand. Their "love" rebounds the Killing Curse onto Voldemort through Expelliarmus.
- **Note:** Canon already puts her wand in Harry's hand for the final duel. Ours puts her there too.

### DIV-009 · The first meeting at Madam Malkin's

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco brags about brooms and houses, asks about Harry's family and sneers at Hagrid. Harry gets out before giving his surname, disliking him.
- **Ours:** Sunlight on her hair reminds Harry of Disney's *Sleeping Beauty*, which he once watched in secret from behind the living-room door. When she asks who he is, he blurts out whether she's Sleeping Beauty. Then he notices she doesn't have the princess's purple eyes, and tells her hers are pretty too. She's had plenty of compliments, but never one this sincere and awestruck. It sours when she learns it's a Muggle story: flustered, she changes the subject by sneering at Hagrid. Harry leaves thinking the "princess" is cruel, and regrets being nice. Neither learns the other's name.
- **Home:** [scenes/b1/madam-malkins.md](../scenes/b1/madam-malkins.md)
- **Ripples:** DIV-010 · [threads/sleeping-beauty.md](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md) · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md)

### DIV-010 · Dracaena's secret Muggle books

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco shows no interest in anything Muggle.
- **Ours:** She wants to know what the boy saw. Narcissa has a contact fetch "a lot" of Muggle books: many versions of Sleeping Beauty, other fairy tales, even a picture book of the Disney film. She reads them before term starts and begins casting herself and the boy as a fairy tale. Fairy tales become her gateway to Muggle novels (wizards are a small minority and don't write many) and eventually to Muggle Studies (DIV-012). She tuts at the Muggles who get real magic wrong.
- **Why:** A gradual way out of her bigotry. And it's cute.
- **Home:** [threads/sleeping-beauty.md](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-016, Q-018, Q-019 · [continuity/reveals.md](reveals.md) · [problems/house-elves.md](../problems/house-elves.md)

### DIV-013 · B4–B5: the dream

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco's hostility toward Harry never lets up. In B5 he joins Umbridge's Inquisitorial Squad.
- **Ours:** Harry's feats (Quirrell, the Basilisk, the Dementors) repair her opinion of him, because she prizes competence, daring and valour. They also convince her that if *Potter* can step outside the script, so can she. (That's incoherent, by design.) So she accepts Harry's invitation to the Yule Ball, half-believing he might really be her prince. Being his Second Task hostage tips her fully into the dream. She keeps her edge, but for the rest of B4 and nearly all of B5 she's simply happy with him. She tells Lucius she's keeping tabs on Potter and uses the Inquisitorial Squad as cover.
- **Home:** [threads/sleeping-beauty.md](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)

### DIV-017 · B3: her play-acting, and what it costs

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** Draco milks his hippogriff injury for weeks. Slytherin uses it to postpone their match against Gryffindor, and he makes Harry and Ron prepare his Potions ingredients. He also joins the fake-Dementor prank at the Ravenclaw match.
- **Ours:**
  - **The injury:** she milks it for weeks too. She's not above subterfuge and pettiness when it comes to throwing darts at her target.
  - **The match:** she uses the injury to delay the match against Harry, because after losing in B2 she knows she needs more practice.
  - **Potions:** she badgers Harry into preparing her ingredients and turns him into her servant. When his poor technique spoils the potion she steps in, strangely sweet under the barbs. In her head they're a married couple cooking together, until she realises she's daydreaming in front of the whole class and scrambles to save face. Harry is weirded out.
  - **The lesson:** later she realises her play-acting gave Lucius the fuel to have Buckbeak executed. That lesson in consequences is what pushes her to save him.
  - **The prank:** she takes no part in the fake-Dementor prank (Q-026).
- **Home:** [scenes/b3/potions-servant.md](../scenes/b3/potions-servant.md) (the lesson) · [scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md](../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md) (the motive)
- **Ripples:** Q-026

### DIV-018 · B3 climax: the swarm, the confession, the flight to Sirius

- **Status:** settled
- **Canon:** The Dementors converge on the lake, drawn by Black. Harry is saved by a Patronus his time-travelling self casts. Then he and Hermione fly Buckbeak to free Sirius.
- **Ours:**
  - **The lead:** to make room to free Buckbeak, she plants a fake lead with the Ministry that draws the Dementors to the lake (how: Q-025).
  - **The lake:** she can't control Buckbeak and ends up at the lake herself, unable to do anything because she doesn't know the Patronus Charm.
  - **The Patronus:** Harry drives off the swarm with his Patronus and saves everyone (which memory: Q-003).
  - **The confession:** under the Dementors' influence she breaks down sobbing and confesses, unprompted, that the swarm is her fault.
  - **Hermione's idea:** ask her to fly Buckbeak to Sirius.
  - **Why she agrees:** she owes Harry, and knows it. And she's just been thrust into the fairy tale: her prince drove off a horde of demons, for her.
  - **Sirius:** he recognises Narcissa's daughter, and they talk briefly. Maybe the Black family made something good after all, if Narcissa gave birth to a girl this brave.
- **Note:** This builds on 🔒 DIV-003 without changing it. The canon sequence has to be rebuilt without Time-Turners: Q-003.
- **Home:** [scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md](../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-003, Q-007, Q-025 · DIV-019 · [continuity/reveals.md](reveals.md)
