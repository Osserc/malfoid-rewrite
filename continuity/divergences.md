# Divergence Ledger

Every departure from canon. Anything not listed here follows the original books.

Entry format: **Status** · **Canon** (what the original does, in our own words) · **Ours** · **Why** (if it isn't obvious) · **Home** (where it's written out in full) · **Ripples** (affected files, open questions).

---

### DIV-001 · Draco is Dracaena Malfoy

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Draco Malfoy, only son of Lucius and Narcissa. Harry's school rival, never a love interest.
- **Ours:** Dracaena Malfoy, only daughter. Harry's rival and his tsundere love interest. Canon Draco is her baseline, adjusted for the knock-on effects of her being a daughter.
- **Home:** [characters/dracaena-malfoy.md](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-001 · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md) · [setting/society.md](../setting/society.md)

### DIV-002 · No Time-Turners

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Time-Turners exist. In B3 Hermione uses one to attend overlapping classes, then she and Harry use it to save Buckbeak and Sirius. The Ministry's stock is destroyed in B5.
- **Ours:** Time-Turners do not exist.
- **Why:** Time travel breaks the plot. See [problems/time-turners.md](../problems/time-turners.md).
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-002, Q-003, Q-004 · DIV-003 takes over the B3 rescue.

### DIV-003 · Dracaena saves Buckbeak and frees Sirius with him

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Draco provokes Buckbeak and gets hurt, and his father has Buckbeak sentenced to death. Harry and Hermione use the Time-Turner to save Buckbeak, then fly him to free Sirius.
- **Ours:** Dracaena regrets getting Buckbeak condemned. She saves him herself, then brings him to Sirius so he can escape.
- **Home:** [scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md](../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-005, Q-006, Q-007 · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md) · [continuity/reveals.md](reveals.md)

### DIV-006 · Harry and Dracaena defeat Voldemort together

- **Status:** 🔒 locked
- **Canon:** Harry duels Voldemort alone, using the hawthorn wand he took from Draco. The Killing Curse rebounds off his Expelliarmus because the Elder Wand's allegiance had passed to Harry (Dumbledore → Draco → Harry).
- **Ours:** Harry and Dracaena duel Voldemort together, both using Dracaena's wand. Their "love" rebounds the Killing Curse onto Voldemort through Expelliarmus.
- **Note:** Canon already puts her wand in Harry's hand for the final duel. Ours puts her there too.
- **Home:** [scenes/b7/final-duel.md](../scenes/b7/final-duel.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-011, Q-012, Q-013, Q-014 · [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md) · [threads/harry-dracaena.md](../threads/harry-dracaena.md)

### DIV-007 · Spellcasting is technical: radicals

- **Status:** 🔒 locked (principle). Design open.
- **Canon:** A spell is an incantation plus a loosely described wand movement. There's no consistent system behind it.
- **Ours:** Spellcasting is technical. Wand motions draw "radicals" that combine into spells.
- **Home:** [setting/magic.md](../setting/magic.md)
- **Ripples:** Q-015, Q-012, Q-014

### DIV-008 · The pureblood ideologues have a point

- **Status:** 🔒 locked (principle). Design open.
- **Canon:** Pureblood supremacy is presented as plain bigotry, with no real argument behind it.
- **Ours:** The pureblood ideologues have an actual point.
