# Open Questions

Undecided points and ripples from decisions already made. IDs are permanent. When a question is answered, move it to **Answered** with a one-line answer and a link to where it's written out.

## Open

### Q-001 · What does being a daughter change for Dracaena? (DIV-001)

Her standing as the Malfoy heir, marriage expectations, what Lucius wants from her, how pureblood society treats a daughter differently from a son.

### Q-002 · Hermione's B3 timetable (DIV-002)

In canon a Time-Turner explains how she attends overlapping classes. Cut the subplot, or explain it another way?

*Partly answered:* she keeps Muggle Studies (DIV-012). What she drops instead is open.

### Q-003 · Rebuilding the B3 climax (DIV-002, DIV-018)

Canon's night depends on the Time-Turner, so most of it has to be reworked until every beat lines up. That covers:
- who is where and when (the Shrieking Shack, the lake, Snape, Ron)
- whether Sirius is captured
- how the flight to him works

These have to fit alongside her fake lead, Buckbeak's freeing, Harry's Patronus, her confession and her talk with Sirius.

Also open: which memory powers Harry's Patronus. It should be something Dracaena-related set up earlier, helped along by him realising what she just did. Candidate: the author's lap-pillow scene (Q-026).

### Q-004 · The Time Room in B5 (DIV-002)

The Department of Mysteries stores Time-Turners in canon. Cut the room, repurpose it, or does some time magic exist that isn't time travel?

### Q-007 · Who knows she did it? (DIV-003)

*Partly answered:* Harry and Hermione learn at the lake, and Sirius recognises her during the escape (DIV-018).

Still open: Ron, Dumbledore, anyone else. Track in [reveals.md](reveals.md).

### Q-008 · How do they end up at the Yule Ball together? (DIV-004)

Still open: where the relationship stands going in and coming out, and the knock-on effects for Cho, Parvati, Pansy and Ron.

### Q-009 · The Second Task makes Harry's feelings public (DIV-005)

*Partly answered:* privately, she's elated (DIV-013).

Still open: who made the choice, and how Harry, Slytherin, the school and her family react. Track in [reveals.md](reveals.md).

### Q-010 · Ron in B4 (DIV-005)

### Q-011 · The Elder Wand and the Hallows (DIV-006)

In canon the curse rebounds because of the Elder Wand's allegiance. If love is the mechanism, what's left of the allegiance chain and of the Hallows plot?

### Q-012 · Two casters, one wand (DIV-006, DIV-007)

How do they both use Dracaena's wand, physically and magically? How does that fit the radical system?

### Q-013 · How does Dracaena end up at Harry's side? (DIV-006, DIV-015)

Her path through B6 and B7: Death Eater pressure, her family, Malfoy Manor, the Battle of Hogwarts. She starts from the cage of DIV-015: how does she wake?

### Q-014 · Love as magic that follows rules (DIV-006, DIV-007)

Canon already treats love as magic through Lily's sacrifice. How do we make it work alongside a technical magic system without it feeling like a cheat, and where in B1–B6 do we plant it?

### Q-015 · Designing the radical system (DIV-007)

Which radicals exist, how they combine, what incantations add, how nonverbal and wandless casting work, and what makes a spell hard.

### Q-016 · What is the pureblood point? (DIV-008)

What's the real grievance or truth, and where does it turn into Voldemort's ideology? Where do the Malfoys stand?

*Lead from DIV-010:* Muggle literature dwarfs wizarding literature, which is what being culturally swallowed looks like. And Muggle fairy tales cast magic users as the villains.

### Q-018 · Who fetches the books? (DIV-010)

Narcissa is the conduit but would never shop herself. Options:

- Dobby. This commits us on the house elves, and Harry freeing him at the end of B2 would cut off her supply.
- A Squib: someone who moves in both worlds and whom purebloods never look at twice.
- A bigger swing: Andromeda, Narcissa's disowned sister, married to a Muggle-born.

### Q-019 · Narcissa and Lucius (DIV-010)

How their marriage works (the author has ideas), why Narcissa indulges the books, and what Lucius knows and when.

### Q-020 · Muggle Studies: when, and how does it get out? (DIV-012, DIV-016)

Which year she starts, how she justifies it at home ("know your enemy"?), when Lucius learns, and how it becomes common knowledge among the Death Eaters by B7.

### Q-021 · What are they to each other in B5? (DIV-013)

A secret couple, secret friends, something unnamed? Who knows? What happens with Cho? And what about the DA bust, where canon Draco is the one who catches Harry?

### Q-022 · What does she feed Lucius? (DIV-013)

The "keeping tabs on Potter" cover only holds if she hands over something. What does she give him, and does any of it help set the Department of Mysteries trap?

### Q-023 · Her B6 attempts (DIV-011, DIV-015)

Canon Draco's attempts (the cursed necklace, the poisoned mead) are clumsy, which shows his heart isn't in it. She's competent and has lost faith. Does she try in earnest, sabotage herself without admitting it, or fail for other reasons? The mead nearly killing Ron sits here.

### Q-024 · Harry's Horcrux

It plays a bigger role than in canon, possibly in his hostility. Harry can be harsher than canon Harry. How exactly?

### Q-025 · Planting the fake lead (DIV-018)

How does she get the Ministry to send the Dementors to the lake? Floated so far:

## Answered

### Q-005 · What makes Dracaena regret Buckbeak? (DIV-003)

She realises her play-acting with the injury gave Lucius the fuel to have Buckbeak executed: a lesson in consequences (DIV-017). Lucius wants the beast dead because no one crosses House Malfoy. She finds punishing a creature for her own mistake petty and beneath a Malfoy, and can't stand the thought of Potter beating her at standing by his principles. → [scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md](../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)

### Q-006 · How does she know to bring Buckbeak to Sirius? (DIV-003)

It's Hermione's idea. She agrees because she owes Harry, having just confessed the swarm was her fault, and because her prince just drove off a horde of demons for her. → DIV-018
