## Open

Her standing as the Malfoy heir, marriage expectations, what Lucius wants from her, how pureblood society treats a daughter differently from a son.

### Q-002 · Hermione's B3 timetable (DIV-002)

In canon a Time-Turner explains how she attends overlapping classes. Cut the subplot, or explain it another way?

### Q-003 · Harry's Patronus at the lake (DIV-002)

In canon Harry drives off the Dementors because he has already seen himself do it, a time-loop beat. What replaces that moment, and the confidence it gives him?

### Q-004 · The Time Room in B5 (DIV-002)

### Q-007 · Who knows she did it? (DIV-003)

Who asks, why the other says yes, where the relationship stands going in and coming out. Knock-on effects for Parvati, Pansy and Ron.

### Q-009 · The Second Task makes Harry's feelings public (DIV-005)

The hostages are the people the champions would miss most, so choosing her says something about Harry in front of the whole school. Who made the choice, and how do Harry, Dracaena, Slytherin, the school and her family react? Track in [reveals.md](reveals.md).

### Q-010 · Ron in B4 (DIV-005)

### Q-011 · The Elder Wand and the Hallows (DIV-006)

### Q-012 · Two casters, one wand (DIV-006, DIV-007)

How do they both use Dracaena's wand, physically and magically? How does that fit the radical system?

### Q-013 · How does Dracaena end up at Harry's side? (DIV-006)

Her path through B6 and B7: Death Eater pressure, her family, Malfoy Manor, the Battle of Hogwarts.

### Q-014 · Love as magic that follows rules (DIV-006, DIV-007)

Canon already treats love as magic through Lily's sacrifice. How do we make it work alongside a technical magic system without it feeling like a cheat, and where in B1–B6 do we plant it?

### Q-015 · Designing the radical system (DIV-007)

Which radicals exist, how they combine, what incantations add, how nonverbal and wandless casting work, and what makes a spell hard.

### Q-016 · What is the pureblood point? (DIV-008)

What's the real grievance or truth, and where does it turn into Voldemort's ideology? Where do the Malfoys stand?
