# Open Questions

Undecided points and ripples from decisions already made. IDs are permanent. When a question is answered, move it to **Answered** with a one-line answer and a link to where it's written out.

## Open

### Q-001 · What does being a daughter change for Dracaena? (DIV-001)

Her standing as the Malfoy heir, marriage expectations, what Lucius wants from her, how pureblood society treats a daughter differently from a son.

*Partly answered:* Lucius isn't resentful about having a daughter, but thinks she should be somewhat sheltered ([lucius-malfoy](../characters/lucius-malfoy.md)). She's a tomboy in spirit and a refined heiress at once ([dracaena-malfoy](../characters/dracaena-malfoy.md)). Ripples: Q-036.

### Q-002 · Hermione's B3 timetable (DIV-002)

In canon a Time-Turner explains how she attends overlapping classes. Cut the subplot, or explain it another way?

*Partly answered (draft):* electives are timetabled in blocks (DIV-034), and two of her choices clash. That, and finding the subject is mostly wizards getting Muggles wrong, is why she drops Muggle Studies. Dracaena calls it conceding defeat (DIV-012). Still open: how long she juggles before giving up, and when in B3 she drops it.

### Q-003 · Rebuilding the B3 climax (DIV-002, DIV-018)

*Partly answered:* the memory behind Harry's Patronus is the lap-pillow scene, and at the lake it all adds up and he re-evaluates her (DIV-018). Canon's Harry drives off the swarm sure he can, having seen himself do it. Ours has the first flicker of resonance instead (Q-028).

### Q-011 · The Elder Wand and the Hallows (DIV-006, DIV-024)

In canon the curse rebounds because of the Elder Wand's allegiance. If love is the mechanism, what's left of the allegiance chain and of the Hallows plot?

*Partly answered:* allegiance is purged (DIV-024). The Elder Wand keeps lore of its own and passes by blood, through killing, so Voldemort killing Snape is the correct move, and pointless. Only the idea is set.

Still open:
- **The canon chain breaks.** Grindelwald stole the wand from Gregorovitch without killing him, and Dumbledore beat Grindelwald without killing him. Options floated:
  - A general "violence" rule, so stealing counts. Risk: disarming is violence too, which brings the disarming chain back, starting with Dracaena disarming Dumbledore on the tower.
  - Retcon: Grindelwald kills Gregorovitch. Doesn't fix Dumbledore, and changes canon's B7 visions of Voldemort hunting Gregorovitch.
  - Claude's proposal: the wand serves at full strength only for someone who killed its previous owner, and in anyone else's hand it's just a very good wand. Grindelwald and Dumbledore held it asleep (Dumbledore by choice: he refused to kill Grindelwald). Snape's kill wakes it, and it underperforms for Voldemort, as in canon, until he kills Snape. No retcons needed. The author isn't convinced yet.
- **Who masters it after the final duel.** Proposal: no one, since Voldemort dies by his own rebounded curse.
- **What's left of the Hallows plot.**

### Q-013 · How does Dracaena end up at Harry's side? (DIV-006, DIV-015)

*Partly answered:* they're back together by the time Harry walks to the forest (DIV-022).

Still open, as part of a broader B7 redesign:
- **Her presence.** Canon Draco barely appears in B7: the Manor, then the castle. Our heroine can't be absent for most of the book, so B7 changes a lot. The Manor may not happen at all.
- **She wakes herself** (settled, [sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)). Her turn to Harry's side has to be her own act. The reconciliation follows it rather than causing it.
- **Does Voldemort use her against Harry?** He saw her love in summer 1996 (DIV-015), and in B5 he used Sirius as a lever on Harry. Arrogance explains why he doesn't care she lied, not why he'd ignore the lever.
