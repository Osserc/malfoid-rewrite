### Q-011 · The Elder Wand and the Hallows (DIV-006, DIV-024)

In canon the curse rebounds because of the Elder Wand's allegiance. If love is the mechanism, what's left of the allegiance chain and of the Hallows plot?

*Partly answered:* allegiance is purged (DIV-024). The Elder Wand keeps lore of its own and passes by blood, through killing, so Voldemort killing Snape is the correct move, and pointless. Only the idea is set.

Still open:
- **The canon chain breaks.** Grindelwald stole the wand from Gregorovitch without killing him, and Dumbledore beat Grindelwald without killing him. Options floated:
  - A general "violence" rule, so stealing counts. Risk: disarming is violence too, which brings the disarming chain back, starting with Dracaena disarming Dumbledore on the tower.
  - Retcon: Grindelwald kills Gregorovitch. Doesn't fix Dumbledore, and changes canon's B7 visions of Voldemort hunting Gregorovitch.
  - Claude's proposal: the wand serves at full strength only for someone who killed its previous owner, and in anyone else's hand it's just a very good wand. Grindelwald and Dumbledore held it asleep (Dumbledore by choice: he refused to kill Grindelwald). Snape's kill wakes it, and it underperforms for Voldemort, as in canon, until he kills Snape. No retcons needed. The author isn't convinced yet.
- **Who masters it after the final duel.** Proposal: no one, since Voldemort dies by his own rebounded curse.
- **What's left of the Hallows plot.**

### Q-013 · How does Dracaena end up at Harry's side? (DIV-006, DIV-015)

Her path through B6 and B7: Death Eater pressure, her family, Malfoy Manor, the Battle of Hogwarts. She starts from the cage of DIV-015: how does she wake?

*Partly answered:* they're back together by the time Harry walks to the forest (DIV-022).

Still open, as part of a broader B7 redesign:
- **Her presence.** Canon Draco barely appears in B7: the Manor, then the castle. Our heroine can't be absent for most of the book, so B7 changes a lot. The Manor may not happen at all.
- **She wakes herself** (settled, [sleeping-beauty](../threads/sleeping-beauty.md)). Her turn to Harry's side has to be her own act. The reconciliation follows it rather than causing it.
- **Does Voldemort use her against Harry?** He saw her love in summer 1996 (DIV-015), and in B5 he used Sirius as a lever on Harry. Arrogance explains why he doesn't care she lied, not why he'd ignore the lever.

### Q-014 · Love as magic that follows rules (DIV-006, DIV-007)

*Partly answered:* the mechanism is resonance, which runs on the radicals (DIV-021). Planting it: Q-028.

Still open: how resonance relates to love as sacrifice. That means Lily's protection, and Harry's in the forest, which in canon stops Voldemort's spells binding on the defenders. If Harry's stays, it lowers the danger of Dracaena's duel (DIV-023).

### Q-028 · Seeding resonance (DIV-021)

The hard part. Across B1–B6 we have to orchestrate moments where resonance happens at all, so that before the finale the reader knows the rule, what it takes, and that it can fail. Open:
- **Where each rung happens:** face to face with two wands, then side by side with two wands. The one-wand rung is reserved for the final duel.
- **The near-misses** where the stained soul hurts her. Candidate: B5, when Harry is failing Occlumency with Snape and she, an Occlumens, tries to help him and recoils. That also keeps the Department of Mysteries trap working.
- **How and when she finds the rule.** A teacher might allude to it in passing (Flitwick, perhaps in B1) and point her to where to look.
- **Hermione as a witness** during the school years, if needed. She can't resonate herself (DIV-021).
- **The author's further hook** for the feud that makes the shared radicals possible.

## Answered

### Q-005 · What makes Dracaena regret Buckbeak? (DIV-003)

She realises her play-acting with the injury gave Lucius the fuel to have Buckbeak executed: a lesson in consequences (DIV-017). Lucius wants the beast dead because no one crosses House Malfoy. She finds punishing a creature for her own mistake petty and beneath a Malfoy, and can't stand the thought of Potter beating her at standing by his principles. → [scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md](../scenes/b3/buckbeak-rescue.md)

### Q-006 · How does she know to bring Buckbeak to Sirius? (DIV-003)

It's Hermione's idea. She agrees because she owes Harry, having just confessed the swarm was her fault, and because her prince just drove off a horde of demons for her. → DIV-018

Harry's hand closes over hers on her wand, and they draw the same radicals together, in resonance. → DIV-021, [scenes/b7/final-duel.md](../scenes/b7/final-duel.md)
