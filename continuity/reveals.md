# Reveals

Who knows what, and when. The tsundere dynamic is a gap between what Dracaena feels, what she shows and what Harry reads into it. That gap sits on top of the series' mysteries, so both are tracked here.

Cells give the book and the scene or chapter where each party learns the truth. `—` means never; `?` means undecided.
